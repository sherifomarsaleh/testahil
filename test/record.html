From 27 July 2026 every new forecast is checked at 1 month and 3 months from the day it was published — real calendar dates. If that date is a weekend or a public holiday on that exchange, we check on the next day it actually trades. Nothing else moves it. The engine’s backtest measures the same calendar window we publish, instead of a fixed session count.
Each forecast gives a likely range. When its date comes, we see whether the real price landed inside that range, and keep a running tally. If our "9 times out of 10" ranges are honest, the price should land inside them about 90% of the time. The big tally above is the backtest record across every covered name’s resolved, non-overlapping 3-month windows; each study page carries its own name-level record.
