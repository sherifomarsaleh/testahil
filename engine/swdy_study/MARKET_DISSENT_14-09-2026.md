# SWDY — MARKET DISSENT

DISSENT_AT_GAP: -35.4%

Central EGP 87.94 against a last known price of EGP 136.20 (2026-09-06). This
document is the CASE for publishing a number that far below the market. It is
not the authorisation to publish it [R-GAP-02 CLAUSE FOUR], and it makes no
claim to an edge — only that the disagreement is real and survives our own
audit.

---

## MECHANISM

**The whole gap is the reinvestment charge, and the June balance sheet measures
it.**

SWDY is spending heavily. FY2025 capex was EGP 13,112mn against depreciation and
amortisation of EGP 3,009mn — 4.4x. The forward model carries PP&E up 178% to
buy 40.9% real revenue growth, which takes asset turns from 7.82x to 6.45x, a
capital intensity the company has never operated at.

A DCF charges that spending against the cash it returns. The market price does
not appear to. The study's own expert panel splits on exactly this line and
makes the mechanism visible from both sides:

| expert | treatment of reinvestment | reads |
|---|---|---|
| earnings-power | capitalises earnings, charges NO reinvestment | **154.41** — above the price |
| cash-based #1 | charges it | 85.56 |
| cash-based #2 | charges it | 78.29 |

The lens that ignores the reinvestment charge lands above the market. The two
that charge it land where we publish. That is the mechanism, stated as an
arithmetic split rather than as an opinion.

**The filings corroborate the charge rather than the story.** Between
31-Dec-2025 and 30-Jun-2026 net debt rose EGP 8,069mn while the group earned
EGP 10,647mn after tax and paid EGP 3,960mn of dividends — an implied free cash
flow of **negative EGP 4,109mn in the half**. The model's FY2026E net debt of
28,039 lands within 2% of the June actual of 28,629. We are not forecasting a
cash drain that the company is not already reporting.

The terminal is not where the gap is made: terminal ROIC 22.43% sits 7.09
percentage points ABOVE the terminal WACC of 15.34%. This terminal creates
value. The gap is in the explicit window, where the spending happens.

## REVERSE READ

What EGP 136.20 must believe, taking the study's own drivers one at a time and
solving for the level that reaches the price:

| driver moved alone | required to reach 136.20 | reachable? |
|---|---|---|
| **Segment margins** | FY26E EBITDA margin **12.63%** (adopted 10.34%) | **YES** |
| Uniform WACC | −189bp (terminal 15.34 → 13.45) | arguable |
| Terminal real growth | above the 4.5% economy cap — at the cap, 124.95 | **no** |
| Beta | at beta 0.00 the value is 96.75 | **no** |
| Capex | at ZERO capex the value is 115.10 | **no** |
| NWC | 6.53% of revenue — never observed | **no** |
| Tax | 5.0% | **no** |

Four of the seven cannot reach the price at any admissible value, and two of
those cannot reach it at physically impossible values — zero capex and zero beta
both leave the price above the model. The disagreement therefore reduces to
**one driver**: the segment margin.

And that one is reachable. The market's implied 12.63% EBITDA margin is inside
this company's own printed record: **13.17% in FY2023 and 13.62% in FY2024**,
against 10.09% in FY2025 and 11.12% in H1-2026.

**So the dispute is a single, honest question: does the FY2023-24 margin come
back?** The market says yes. We say no. That is a disagreement about the future,
not a modelling artefact, and it is stated here in the one form that can be
graded later.

## WHY NOT CREDIBLE

We do not claim the market's belief is absurd. We claim it is not supported by
what the company has filed since, and that the burden sits on the recovery.

1. **The margin fell as the capital base grew.** The FY2023-24 margin was earned
   on a smaller asset base. The forward case requires the old margin AND the new
   spending, which is the combination the filings have not yet shown.

2. **H1-2026 is the most recent evidence and it reads 11.12%**, not 12.63%. Our
   FY26E segment margin of 12.34% is already 29bp ABOVE the seasonal analogue of
   12.05% derived from the company's own H1/H2 split. We are not modelling a
   collapse; we are modelling slightly better than the last comparable half.

3. **The spending has not stopped.** H1-2026 capex of 5,436 against H1-2025's
   5,387 is +0.9% — flat, not falling. A price that implies the margin returns
   while the capital programme continues is asking for both at once.

4. **The cash statement does not corroborate the recovery.** Negative EGP
   4,109mn of implied free cash flow in the half is the opposite of what a
   margin recovery of that size would produce.

all. Both were checked because they were the most likely way our own number
could be too low on a currency that halved, and neither was.

## FALSIFIER

**This dissent is wrong if SWDY's segment EBITDA margin returns to 12.6% or
above on a full financial year while the capital programme continues at its
current rate.**

That is one number, from the company's own reporting, on a date that is already
fixed. Specifically:

- **FY2026 full-year segment EBITDA margin at or above 12.63%** falsifies the
  central directly — that is the level the reverse read says the price requires.
- **H2-2026 capex falling below roughly EGP 3,000mn** (against H1-2026's 5,436)
  would mean the programme has ended and the reinvestment charge should not be
  carried forward at the modelled rate. Either alone moves the answer materially
  toward the price; both together close most of the gap.

Two further conditions, narrower but faster:

- If the FY2026 effective tax rate prints at or below 24.5%, the tax judgement
  we flagged against ourselves is vindicated and the downward correction of
  −4.59 to −23.59 falls away.
- If FY2026 free cash flow turns positive without a fall in capex, the
  mechanism in this document is wrong at its root.

Nothing here is graded on the price moving. A price that rises toward 87.94
proves nothing and neither does one that falls to it; the ledger grades the
forecast, not the disagreement [R-LENS-02].
