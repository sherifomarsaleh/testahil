## Analytical honesty (internal record)

The single most consequential judgement in this study is that the **full Egyptian cost of equity is
charged to a company earning over 70% of its revenue abroad**. This is the conservative reading and
it produces a central estimate 24% below the market price. The alternative — discounting the
hard-currency cash-flow leg at a hard-currency rate — produces EGP 134.40, *above* the market price,
and is computed, published in the summary table, in §4, on the `Fundamental Valuation` sheet and in
Expert 3's range. **The two readings are shown side by side and are never averaged into a single
number that would hide the disagreement.** A reader who resolves the currency question differently
gets a materially different answer from the same cash-flow forecasts, and the study says so
explicitly rather than implying more precision than the evidence supports.

## Known limitation, stated plainly

The audited FY2025 consolidated statements could not be retrieved: the company's investor-relations
site and the exchange's filing archive are both unreachable from this environment, and the
company's own document repository stops at Q1-2025. FY2023 and FY2024 are therefore fully audited
and unestimated, while FY2025 rests on disclosed headline figures with the intermediate lines
derived. Every derived line is labelled in the study, in the Excel, and in the bibliography. The
audited FY2025 accounts are the named falsifier.
