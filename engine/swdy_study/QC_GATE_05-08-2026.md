## Analytical honesty (internal record)

The single most consequential judgement in this study is that the **full Egyptian cost of equity is
charged to a company earning over 70% of its revenue abroad**. This is the conservative reading and
it produces a central estimate 24% below the market price. The alternative — discounting the
hard-currency cash-flow leg at a hard-currency rate — produces EGP 134.40, *above* the market price,
and is computed, published in the summary table, in §4, on the `Fundamental Valuation` sheet and in
Expert 3's range. **The two readings are shown side by side and are never averaged into a single
number that would hide the disagreement.** A reader who resolves the currency question differently
gets a materially different answer from the same cash-flow forecasts, and the study says so
explicitly rather than implying more precision than the evidence supports.

## REVISION 1 — 05-Aug-2026 (full bottom-up rebuild of the forecast engine, per instruction)

**Challenge:** "Have you built the DCF bottom up?" Answered honestly: no. The delivered forecast
applied assumed growth rates to a currency-split revenue line and assumed segment EBITDA margins.
Group margin was an output of the mix, but nothing was built from units. The unit-economics data
had been extracted from the company's filings during research and then not used — a real gap, not
a presentational one.

**What was rebuilt.** Revenue and gross profit are now built from volumes and prices per unit, on
the company's own disclosed segment data, across seven sub-segments (cables, raw material, E&C,
transformers, meters, other electrical products, infrastructure).

**The validation that makes it trustworthy:** the historical sub-segment build reconciles to the
AUDITED income statement to within EGP 0.2mn on both revenue and gross profit in FY2023 and
FY2024, and reproduces every disclosed per-unit figure exactly — gross profit per tonne of cable
(90,020 / 119,043), per meter (418 / 707) and per MVA (136,345 / 221,065). The reported "Wires &
Cables" segment was decomposed into Cables and Raw Material (the copper-rod pass-through arm),
which sum exactly to it. Asserted, not asserted-by-eye.

**Three modelling errors the rebuild caught and fixed, each found by an assert:**
1. **FY2025 unit margins were 24% too generous.** The disclosed Q1-2025 cable gross profit per
   tonne (88,173) implies a conversion margin of ~13.8%, against 22.8% in FY2024 — the cable
   margin roughly HALVED, and that single fact is most of the group's gross-margin decline. FY2025
   margins are now pinned to that disclosure, with the non-cable lines scaled by one solved
   compression factor (0.874) so the total reproduces the gross profit assembled from the
   disclosed prints.
2. **Gross profit was modelled as a percentage of a copper-inflated price.** Wrong for a converter:
   when copper spikes, revenue rises because the metal is passed through, but the conversion margin
   earned per tonne does not. Modelling it as a percentage manufactured profit out of a commodity
   move. Now per-unit — per tonne, per MVA, per meter — growing with cost inflation only, with no
   real recovery in unit profitability assumed anywhere.
3. **The first fix over-corrected and contradicted the Q1-2026 print.** It produced FY2026 EBITDA
   falling 19% while the company actually reported revenue +26.8% and attributable profit +16.8%.
   The FY2026 cable conversion margin is therefore SOLVED to reproduce the EBITDA margin the
   Q1-2026 disclosure implies (11.24%), not assumed. The solved figure sits inside the historical
   range, is below what pure copper-cost scaling of FY2024 would give, and as a share of realised
   price sits between the FY2025 trough (13.8%) and the FY2024 peak (22.8%).

**Disclosed attribution caveat:** the solve attributes all of the Q1-2026 improvement to cables,
where the volatility demonstrably is. If it in fact came from another line the sub-segment split
changes, but GROUP EBITDA — the only thing the valuation consumes — is pinned by the print either
way. Stated in the assertion log and in the study.

**Effect on the valuation (top-down → bottom-up):**

| Reading | Top-down | Bottom-up |
|---|---|---|
| DCF base | 70.99 | **61.70** |
| DCF bull | 129.37 | 115.43 |
| Currency-of-discounting alternative | 134.40 | 108.27 |
| Relative lens | 108.89 | 117.41 |
| Normalised earnings | 106.01 | 108.34 |
| Book lens | 33.86 | 33.86 |
| **Weighted central** | 80.00 | **78.00** |
| Expert panel median | 65.22 | 55.10 |
| Terminal value share of EV | 76.8% | 76.3% |

The central barely moved (80.00 → 78.00) but the *composition* changed materially, and the
cash-flow lens fell 13%. The currency-of-discounting alternative fell hardest, from 134.40 to
108.27, because the foreign revenue share is now **derived from the build at 60-62%** rather than
taken as the disclosed "over 70%" — the copper-linked lines are dollar-priced but the domestic
E&C and infrastructure books are not. That is a more honest number and it materially narrows the
gap between the two readings of the currency question.

**Gate items re-verified after the rebuild:** (a) structure unchanged, 16 sections / 16 sheets;
(b) formatting unchanged; (c) the indicator set is now genuinely bottom-up for the class — tonnes,
MVA, meters, conversion margin per unit, order-book conversion rate, fabrication uplift over
copper; (e) waterfall unchanged and intact; (o) all 42 tables re-checked within the text block
with no starved or bloated columns; (h)/(n) all 8 figures regenerated and re-inspected. Workbook:
89 formulas, 0 unresolvable, **28** cell-level reconciliation checks passed (two added for the new
segment structure), balance-check row zero. Procedure scrub clean on both documents. Input
register grew from 113 to 131 four-field inputs, all registered in the bibliography.

**Honest limitation, unchanged and now sharper:** FY2025 volumes are not disclosed. They are
implied from the disclosed Q1-2025 prints and the FY2024 seasonal share, and the FY2025 cable
price per tonne is the residual against disclosed group revenue. That residual back-solves a
fabrication uplift of 1.295, sitting between the FY2023 (1.386) and FY2024 (1.261) prints — the
check that it is economics rather than a plug absorbing an error. The audited FY2025 accounts
remain the named falsifier.
