## What is genuinely strong here, and should not be lost in a rebuild

The workbook reproduces the model on all **630** formula cells with 41 headline
reconciliations, reproduces **cell for cell across 1,562 populated cells** against a clean
`git archive HEAD` rebuild, and **evaluates 630 of 630 formulas with zero errors under an
independent engine**. Both delivered documents rebuild to **0 text differences**. The free
cash-flow waterfall foots by hand on every year and reaches present value inline, with the
discount factors chaining correctly off a forward-rate schedule. The beta is on the
sanctioned route against the published EGX30, `assert_beta_provenance()` is genuinely called
before the record is written, and the correction it forced raises the cost of equity by
203bp and moves the answer **further below** a price it already sits far below — the only
direction that proves the discipline is not fitting to the market. **Three of the four fixes
this pass was asked to verify are real and one is exemplary**: the bridge guard in
`engine/dcf_table.py` was tested with a negative control that injects the *condition* rather
than the key, and it raised. **The revenue and margin engine is now live formula in the
delivered workbook** — 56 of 60 driver rows consumed, the pasted segment forecast gone, and
a new repository-level gate behind it. The Cables segment, 55.4% of revenue, is built at

SWDY. The defects above came from three things no gate here does: **reading the twenty-nine

sensitivity grids, the star case and the gap review — to ask what else in the study had been
written on the assumption that those numbers were the old ones.** The last of those is where
both new hard fails came from, and it is the general shape: a fix that moves a number must
be followed to every sentence that describes it.
