## The one finding the 10-September gate got wrong, and the correct version

Its row 27 said the delivered document *"claims a build the model does not carry"* — that
the disclosed cable tonnage never reaches the Cables driver. **That is backwards.**
`compute.py:1632` is `r_cab *= ((1 + cu_growth) * (1 + _PASSTHRU[i]) * (1 + V['cables_volume_growth'][i]) * (vol_mult ** 0.2))`,
and `cables_volume_growth` is `[0.1071, 0.090, 0.075, 0.065, 0.055]` — the company's own
+10.71% reviewed-half tonnage growth and a taper off it. The model IS built on the tonnage.
What fails is the other direction: **`cables_real_growth` (3.0% flat) is consumed by no
line of the model and is what both delivered artefacts publish as the live Cables driver**,
while the two drivers that do run it appear in neither. Row 11.
