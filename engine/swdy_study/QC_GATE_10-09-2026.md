## What the filings disclose that the model does not consume

The self-audit question [L-006], asked against the Sweep Register — which does not exist,
so it is asked against the filings themselves and the study's own input register.

**1. Cable tonnage, for the segment that is 55.4% of revenue.** 144,997 / 156,748 /
167,665 / 185,449 tonnes over FY2022-25 and 99,239 in the reviewed half against 89,636.
The model reaches it only through `cables_passthrough`, a price-realisation gap. Revenue is
still `(1+copper)(1+FX)(1+3.0% held)`, and the disclosed volume series compounds at 7–11%
while the held driver is 3.0%. A tonnes × price-per-tonne build with a conversion cost per
tonne is reachable on this disclosure and is not made — which is the whole of row 3, and it
is what ELEC, the only other listed Egyptian cable maker, is built on in this same book.

**2. The engineering and construction order book, for 32.4% of revenue.** EGP 293bn at
December 2025 and **EGP 346bn at 30 June 2026**, with cables 43.5bn and meters 8.8bn
beside it. §7 says it is *"read and NOT burnt down: it corroborates the Constructions taper
rather than producing it"*. So a disclosed, dated, 3.8× -revenue backlog sits outside the
model while the segment is forecast on a CAGR taper from 27.4% to 9.0%. That is a stated
choice, and it is still a disclosure the answer does not use.

**3. The 30 June 2026 balance sheet.** Read for segment revenue, margins, capex, net debt,
associates and the employees' share; not used for the bridge, which subtracts the

## What is genuinely strong here, and should not be lost in a rebuild

The workbook reproduces the model on all **594** formula cells with 41 headline
reconciliations, and reproduces **cell for cell across 1,550 populated cells** against an
independent sandbox rebuild. The free-cash-flow waterfall foots by hand on every year and
reaches present value inline, with the discount factors chaining correctly off a forward
rate schedule. The beta is on the sanctioned route, the composite is withdrawn rather than
deleted, and both delivered documents now credit EGX30 — the correction raises the cost of
equity by 203bp and moves the answer **further below** a price it already sits far below,
which is the only direction that proves the discipline is not fitting to the market. The
cost-of-capital record names its own construction from a closed list, states that no
relevering was used and why, and declares the one clause it fails against the house path
with the direction and size of the error. The terminal stands on a life derived by
identity from the audited note and reproduces to 1e-6. The segment build ties to audited
revenue exactly in all three years and to operating profit through an exactly-reconciling
load, and FY2026E is cross-checked against a disclosed quarter in a test that could have
failed (+4%). The contested currency judgement is published in all four required places
and averaged in none. The sensitivity grids now reproduce the published central to
fourteen figures. The `forecast_anchor` record deliberately strikes itself on the
comparison that makes its own breach larger. And the gap review, the cost-of-capital
record, the lens record and the bridge record each state, in their own words, the clause
they fail and why it was not fixed in this pass — which is worth more than a green tick.

`driver_test.py` all ran and all reported clean, and thirty repository gates ran over
