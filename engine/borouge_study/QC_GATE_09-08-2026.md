## Known limitations, stated rather than buried

1. **The probability bands fail their own test on this share** (item d). Reported in full in §3, in §6, and as a caveat row in §7. No parameter was tuned to make it pass — the market-level fit is the standing gate and the per-name overlay that would address over-wide bands is not active on this market.
2. **Only 4.17 years of price history exist.** A five-year backtest is not available for this name and cannot be manufactured.
3. **The beta is still statistically weak, even on the right index.** R-squared 0.094 and a 90% interval spanning 0.70 times the estimate. Moving to the ADX general index raised it from 0.271 to 0.415 and improved the fit slightly, but it did not resolve the underlying problem: a share with roughly a 10% free float that has traded in a narrow band since listing does not give a precise market sensitivity. That is exactly why both betas are still published side by side.
4. **Two top-down drivers**, both justified by a named negative search: maintenance capital expenditure (no medium-term programme is published) and ethane escalation (the pricing formula is not disclosed; carried at zero and sensitised).
5. **Segmental profitability is not disclosed** — cost is allocated across the two products on physical drivers, and the gap is stated in the study and in the bibliography.
6. **The expected 2027 tender offer has no published exchange ratio.** No conversion value enters any number. If a ratio is published, this study's subject changes materially.
