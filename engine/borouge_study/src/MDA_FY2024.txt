Summary of Operational & Financial Performance
Borouge reported fourth quarter revenue of $1,621 million, representing a quarter-on-quarter increase of
1 percent. Sales volumes for Polyethylene (PE) and Polypropylene (PP) were up by 2 percent and 5 percent,
respectively, versus the previous quarter. Average selling prices for both PE and PP decreased by 3 percent
on a quarter-on-quarter basis. The company continues to maintain high utilisation rates in the quarter of
113 percent for PE and 98 percent for PP.
On a year-on-year basis, overall sales volumes are up by 8 percent in the fourth quarter. Sales volumes for
PE and PP are up by 10 percent and 5 percent, respectively. Blended average selling prices moderately
improved by 1 percent, on a year-on-year basis while selling prices for PE recorded a moderate decline of
2 percent and PP average selling prices are up by 3 percent.
Borouge achieved a premium above benchmark price of $178 per tonne for PE and $140 per tonne for PP
during the fourth quarter. Premium above benchmark for PE is down 5 percent while premium for PP
improved by 26 percent, respectively, versus the previous year. On a quarter-on-quarter basis, premia
above benchmark for PE and PP are down by 10 percent and 13 percent respectively.
The company reported an adjusted EBITDA of $650 million, up 8 percent on a year-on-year basis and
EBITDA margin of 40 percent in the fourth quarter. Total operating costs (excluding depreciation &
amortisation) in the fourth quarter increased by 2 percent on a quarter-on-quarter basis and up 6 percent
on a year-on-year basis, driven by strong production and sales volumes. The company maintained a high
cash conversion of 88 percent during the quarter.
On a full-year basis, net profit is up 24 percent to $1,239 million and EBITDA is up by 14 percent to $2,477
million. This is supported by record production and sales volumes which are up by 3 percent and 4 percent,
respectively, versus the previous year. The company continues to maintain cost discipline as reflected by
a 2 percent decrease in the total operating costs (excluding depreciation & amortisation) on a year-on-year
basis, despite higher volumes.
Net Debt stood at $2,691 million as of 31 December 2024.

1. Operational Review
Borouge achieved a record production volume of 5.2 million tonnes in 2024, supported by a high asset
reliability rate of 98.5 percent.
During the fourth quarter, production operated at one of the highest utilisation rates of 113 percent for PE
and 98 percent for PP, achieving high utilisation rates on a consecutive basis.
The Olefin Conversion Unit (OCU) was maintained at a high-capacity utilization rate. Ethylene is typically
prioritized for use in maximizing PE production and the additional quantities are sent to the OCU. Any
excess ethylene can be sold in the market.

During the quarter, total sales volumes are up by 3 percent versus the previous quarter and up by 8 percent
on a year-on-year basis. Quarterly sales volumes from Polyethylene (PE) and Polypropylene (PP) are up by
2 percent and 5 percent respectively versus the third quarter. Sales volumes from infrastructure solutions
contributed 39 percent to total sales volumes. Asia Pacific region continues to be the region with highest
sales of 63 percent of total sales volumes followed by Middle East and Africa at 7 percent. This is driven by
company’s strategic focus on differentiated, durable products, geographic optimization, and agility in
navigating market challenges.
Borouge achieved a premia over benchmark of $178 per tonne for PE and $140 per tonne for PP. Premia
for PE is slightly below management’s through-the-cycle guidance of $200 per tonne for PE and in-line with
$140 per tonne for PP.
In the fourth quarter, blended average prices were up 1 percent versus the previous year and down by 3
percent on a quarter-on-quarter basis. Average selling prices for PE declined by 2 percent versus the
previous year and 3 percent on a quarter-on-quarter basis. Average selling prices for PP increased by 3
percent on year-on-year basis and declined by 3 percent on quarter-on-quarter basis. Product
benchmarks for PE and PP remained almost flat on a quarterly basis. Premium above benchmark for PE
and PP declined by 10 percent and 13 percent, respectively, versus the previous quarter.
On a full-year basis, blended average selling prices are flat and average selling prices for PE are down by 3
percent and up by 3 percent for PP versus the previous year. The company achieved premia above product
benchmark of $198 per tonne for PE and $150 per tonne for PP.
Borouge’s commercial excellence, has been consistently reflected in the company’s strong financial
performance.

During the fourth quarter, overall operating costs (excluding depreciation & amortisation) increased 6
percent on a year-on-year basis and 2 percent versus the previous quarter driven by higher volumes.
Cost of goods sold (excluding depreciation and amortization) increased by 11 percent on a year-on-year
basis and up by 7 percent versus the previous quarter. On a full-year basis, total cost of goods sold
declined by 4 percent despite record volumes. This decrease was supported by 5 percent decline in
feedstock cost and 3 percent decrease in other variable and fixed costs for the full year.
General and administrative expenses are down significantly both year-on-year and on a quarterly basis by
40 percent and 50 percent respectively due to one-off adjustments. Selling and distribution expense
increased on a quarter-on-quarter basis by 14 percent due to higher shipping related costs during the
quarter. Other income in Q4 2024 relates mainly to a one-off receipt from an insurance claim.
On a full-year basis, the overall operating cost base (excluding depreciation & amortisation) declined by 2
percent on a year-on-year basis, reflecting company’s focus on strategic cost management even after the
successful completion of the Value Enhancement Program in 2023 that delivered $607 million through
revenue optimization and cost reductions.
Selling and distribution expenses are up 18 percent on year-on-year basis due to freight costs. General and
administrative expenses increased by 5 percent on a year-on-year basis due to one-off items.

Expectations of a stable macro environment in Borouge core markets with positive expectations from
Chinese market, following ongoing stimulus efforts.

tonne for PE and $140 per tonne for PP.
Management intends to pay a dividend of $1.3 billion for FY 2025. The final dividend for FY 2024 of $650
