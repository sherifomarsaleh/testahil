## Known limitations, stated plainly

1. The FY2023 income-statement column is the continuing basis of the FY2024 statements and still
   contains the Türkiye business; Savola never re-presented FY2023 ex-Türkiye, so the study flags the
   basis wherever the column appears rather than reconstructing an unpublished figure.
2. The H1-2026 reviewed interim statements were filed but not yet mirrored on savola.com at the study
   date; all H1 figures come from the company's own results release and presentation. Reading the
   reviewed statements when they post is a named follow-up item.
3. The 10Y SAR risk-free rate is a constructed figure (no public quotable curve point); the
   construction is disclosed, cross-checked and priced ±50bp in the study.
4. The Al Mehbaj acquisition consideration is undisclosed; the nuts leg carries a small flagged
   revenue step instead of an invented purchase price, and a disclosed consideration is a named
   re-anchoring trigger in §5.
