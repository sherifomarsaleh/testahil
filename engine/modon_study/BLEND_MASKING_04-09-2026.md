# MODON — the published central is below the downside this study says was falsified

**Not a rebuild and not a gap review.** A finding recorded where the work is. Nothing in
this study was changed.

## The arithmetic

The published central is a typed blend of four lenses at 40 / 20 / 20 / 20, and it
recomputes to the published figure exactly:

| Lens | Base (AED) | Weight | Against the struck spot of 2.83 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discounted cash flow — *declared the primary* | 3.5423 | 40% | **+25.2%** |
| Relative multiples | 2.1954 | 20% | −22.4% |
| Normalised earnings power | 1.4604 | 20% | −48.4% |
| Book value and sustainable return | 1.7354 | 20% | −38.7% |
| **Published central** | **2.4952** | — | **−11.8%** |

**The blend flips the sign of this study's disagreement with the market.** Its primary
lens says the company is worth a quarter more than the price. The number it publishes
says a tenth less.

## And it goes below the study's own falsified downside

This study's central contested judgement is the development sales trajectory, and it
publishes three paths:

| Path | AED |
|---|---|
| Base — sustained, normalising | 3.5423 |
| **Run-off — the stress case** | **2.6432** |
| Bull | 4.1338 |
| *Published central (the blend)* | *2.4952* |

The study's own note on that judgement reads: *"the run-off is now labelled a stress: the
H1-2026 disclosure falsified it as a live central path."*

**So the published number is 5.6% BELOW a downside the study itself says the filings ruled
out.** No path in the contested set is that low. It is not a scenario anybody chose; it is
where four weights happen to land, and nothing in the study asserts it.

## What the gates see, and why neither is wrong

- The **gap gate** reads −11.8% and lists MODON as outside the band with no review. It is
  therefore auditing a below-price answer that this study's own primary lens calls
  above-price. The review it asks for would interrogate the wrong direction.
- The **publication block** is one-sided below the price by instruction, so at −11.8% it
  would **HOLD** this study — while its primary lens says there is nothing to hold.

Neither gate is wrong. Both read the number the study publishes, and the number the study
publishes is the blend.

## The lens that should not be there at all

MODON is a developer. `LENS_REGISTRY` carries two developer rows and both read
`dcf` primary with `rnav`, `relative_multiple` and `book_value` beside it. **Normalised
earnings power is deliberately absent from both**, because a developer recognising revenue
on handover reports earnings that are an accident of which project completed in which
year.

MODON carries that lens at **a fifth of the weight**, reading 1.4604 against a cash-flow
3.5423. That is the same defect, at the same weight, on the same class, as the case that
retired the typed blend on 02-Sep-2026 — where the normalised lens was the worst read at
5.17 against a cash-flow 14.86 and also carried a fifth.

## What this study needs, and the order

1. **The central becomes the cash-flow lens**, with RNAV, the relative multiple and the
   book floor beside it as cross-checks, and the bear/full envelope the range of the
   present-value reads. Normalised earnings comes out entirely — not re-weighted, removed,
   because it is not a lens this class publishes.
2. **Then [R-GAP-01] is owed on the new number**, at roughly +25% above the struck spot,
   which is the ABOVE-price direction the audit gate is two-sided for and the publication
   block deliberately is not.
3. This study commits **no macro, bridge, forecast-anchor, cost-of-capital or lens record
   at all**, and its spot carries no date, so the re-issue is a full pass rather than a
   patch.

## What is deliberately NOT concluded

**That 3.5423 is right.** This note makes no case for the cash-flow lens; the run-off
stress and the bull case are published beside it precisely because the trajectory is
contested. What is concluded is narrower and is arithmetic rather than judgement: **the
published number is produced by an architecture this house retired, it reverses the sign
of the study's own disagreement with the market, and it sits below a downside the study
says its filings falsified.**
