### Standing-rule consequences adopted this edition
1. **A number stated in prose must be computed, not typed** — extended from workbook builders to
   the four-field register's own justification text. Enforced by assertion.
2. **Where a company discloses the same total two ways, recover the joint and test it** — both
   marginals plus a structural assumption often pin down a cross-tabulation the company never
   publishes, and the test is that the residual must be positive and foot exactly.
3. **A near-term reviewed actual outranks a stale full-year rate** — and where the second half of a
   year is structurally cheaper, carrying the first-half rate forward must be shown to be
   conservative with numbers, not asserted.
4. **An unidentified split must be demonstrated unidentified, not asserted** — solve for it across
   every available period pair and publish the range.
5. **Reading the rendered PDF and inspecting figures as images are gates, not formalities** — on
   this edition they caught a self-contradiction, two wrong numbers, a retired unit in an axis
   label, and a promise the page did not keep.
