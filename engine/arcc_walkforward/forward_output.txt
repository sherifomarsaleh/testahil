!! HOW THIS RANGE MUST BE READ, and it is not a forecast of the company:
   it is the measured record of HOW WRONG THIS METHOD HAS BEEN at this
   distance, and that record is dominated by a single unrepeatable event
   -- the pound going from 15.6 to 49.2 between 2021 and 2025. The median
   error is negative because no origin could foresee that. Carrying the
   width forward assumes another shock of that size is as likely as not,
   which is a statement nobody has evidence for. The range is published
   with that caveat attached, and the bias inside it is NOT promoted into
   the drivers -- clause 2 refused exactly that correction on cost per
   tonne, and re-centring the range would have smuggled it back in.

AND THE WIDTH IS THE POINT. At h=5 the measured spread runs from
-1.483 to -0.267 in log terms — a range of roughly 3.4x on the level.
A POINT ESTIMATE AT FIVE YEARS WOULD BE A FICTION, AND THIS RECORD IS
WHY THE STUDY PUBLISHES YEARS 3-5 AS RANGES.
