3.2.3 Provisions
The carrying amount of provisions at the end of period ended December 31, 2025 is EGP 111 684 116 (2024:
EGP 77 899 139). This amount is based on estimates of future costs for legal cases and other claims in
connection with the Company’s operations (Note 27). As the provisions cannot be determined accurately,

changes in management estimates and are accounted for on a prospective basis and recognized in the period
in which the change in estimates arises.
