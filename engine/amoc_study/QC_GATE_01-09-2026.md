## Known weaknesses carried forward, stated not hidden

- Roughly half the base year is an exchange disclosure rather than an audited filing.
- Two exchange disclosures that would move the answer in opposite directions (a capital-expenditure budget and a revised operating-profit budget) could not be opened.
- The FY2023 audit opinion is **qualified**, on two balance-sheet matters that do not touch the forecast lines.
- The processing-intensity weights behind any per-line margin are a **construction**: AMOC discloses cost by nature only, and had no per-product costing system before 1 July 2023.
- The forecasting method's measured three-year profit band is roughly fifteen-fold. The study says so in §7 rather than implying a precision it has not earned.

## What the first pass of this edition got wrong, and how it was found

The first pass of this rebuild published EGP 5.53 — 39% below the market price. That was
challenged on the ground that a fair value at half the traded price should be treated as a
finding about the model before it is published as a finding about the company. It was right.
Five defects, each of which pushed the same way:

| # | Defect | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The reviewed H1-2026 statements were never opened.** They were in the 104 documents downloaded from the company's own archive on the first day of this run. The study went on describing that half as "a press release rather than a filing", rejected its gross-profit line on a coherence test and solved gross profit from the profit line. The filing confirms the released figure to 0.03%. | base-year gross margin 8.997% → **9.653%** |
| 2 | **The coherence test that did the rejecting was itself wrong.** It estimated the half's other income by doubling the March quarter's — EGP 451mn against a filed EGP 197mn. The most volatile line in the statement was the one extrapolated. → **L-053** | the rejection reversed |
| 3 | **Three macro paths contradicted each other.** Costs escalated at Egyptian inflation of 14.5%→9.5%, product realisation at 9.0%→5.2%, the currency at 7.7%→3.6%, with no mechanism offered for the gaps. On a company whose feedstock is 82% of revenue and is priced off the same barrel as its output, that alone manufactured the forecast margin decline. **This is L-048, the lesson this run's own walk-forward had just produced, sitting unnoticed in the study being rebuilt.** | EBIT path 2,239→1,370 became 2,809→3,070 |
| 4 | **The cash was charged for twice.** Operating cash flows were discounted at a net-cash-weighted 31.19% — 374bp ABOVE the cost of equity, because the debt weight is −26% — and the bridge then added the cash back at face. → **L-054** | operating discount rate 31.19% → **27.45%** |
| 5 | **Terminal growth of 5% against a terminal risk-free of 12.5%**, which embeds the central bank's 7% inflation target. That is a business shrinking 2% a year in real terms for ever, never stated as an assumption. | g 5.0% → **7.0%**, inflation only, zero real |

And one claim that was simply false: the headline said the market price required "a PERMANENT
gross margin near 12.2% — above the best single quarter this company has ever filed". The figure
was typed rather than solved and never recomputed; solved, it is **9.37%**, BELOW the 9.653% the
company has just filed for twelve months. And AMOC had already printed 13.84% for the full year
to June 2022 and 13.92% in the June 2026 quarter. It is now computed by the model and read into
the headline, section 1.14 and Figure 2, so it cannot drift from the model again.

**The balance sheet was also a year stale** and is now rolled to 30 June 2026: net cash EGP
3,002mn (EGP 2.32 a share) against 2,443mn, book equity EGP 4.70 a share against 3.71, and
dividends payable halved to 258mn.

**What did NOT change, and is worth saying:** the walk-forward, its finding that the method does
not beat "no change", the conforming beta, the flat volume path (which the filed twelve-month
tonnage of 1,502,325 t supports better than the annualised half it replaced — the half to June
2026 annualises to 1,388,482 t, so flat remains the optimistic case), and the retirement of the
skill verdict. The corrections above are all of the "the model was wrong" kind, not the "the
company is better than we thought" kind.
