## VERDICT: NOT DELIVERABLE

The model, the workbook and the committed records are in good order. **The delivered narrative
document is not.** Nine of the twelve rows that fail are places where the Word document and the
PDF a reader receives disagree with the study's own numbers file, with its own tables, or with
themselves — and every programmatic gate in the repository reports clean on this edition because
each of them reads the record, the workbook or the vocabulary rather than the sentences.

Shortest list of what blocks it:

1. The delivered document still publishes the **retired 45/20/20/15 blend as its central** — Table 1
   row `WEIGHTED CENTRAL · the four, weighted · 100%`, its caption, the Headline's "weighted range",
   §4's "weighted fair value", Figure 1's bottom bar and Figure 4's x-axis. The workbook was fixed
   this edition; the document was not. `check_lens_vocabulary.py` waives it.
2. **The Headline is inverted.** "Fair value sits −8.9% BELOW the price", "the price stands −8.2%
   ABOVE fair value", "the price is outside it", "Every one of the four lenses lands below the
   price" — all four false at a central of 9.91 against 9.10, and contradicted by the summary table
   on the same page.
3. **Two of five figures publish superseded numbers.** Figure 3 ends at EGP **9.77** a share against
   Table 13's 9.91; Figure 5 draws a gold line labelled **"fair value 8.64"** — the 1-Sep edition's
   central. Neither reproduces from the committed builders.
4. **`case_adversarial.json` is two editions stale** (base EGP 5.95, last written at `66df1c84`)
   and drives Table 7, Table 18, Figure 4 and the Headline. Table 7 is incoherent on its face:
   removing a charge is shown lowering value by 34–38%.
5. **Table 10 publishes the removed defect as the method** — net-debt weights 134.30% / −34.30%
   and "the negative debt weight RAISES the operating rate above the cost of equity", while
   `wacc_exp == ke_exp` exactly. The caption reads "27.5% — ABOVE the 27.5% cost of equity".
6. **§1.2 contradicts itself four lines apart** on whether the second half of the base year is a
   filing.
7. **The expert appendix is below the depth bar** — no worldview, when-it-works/when-it-fails,
   falsifier, cross-examination, three-in-one-room or divergence table; two of three expert
   centrals sit outside their own published ranges.
8. **Three typed numerals disagree with computed values**: 1.23x vs 1.225x, 36% vs 54.4%, "about
   26%" vs 36.5%.
9. **No peer frame, risk register or research register**; the bibliography carries an
   edition-history section the model report excludes by construction.
