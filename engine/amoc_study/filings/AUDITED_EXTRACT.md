# WHAT THIS OVERTURNS IN THE PUBLISHED STUDY
1. **Capex.** Study modelled 1.45%->1.25% of revenue (EGP 649mn in 2026E). ACTUAL cash capex was
   **30.4mn in the 6 months to Dec-2025** and **65.0mn in Q1-2026** — roughly 130-260mn a year,
   a quarter to a third of the assumption. Free cash flow was materially understated.
2. **Depreciation.** Study used 1.1% of revenue (440mn for CY25). ACTUAL 70.1mn in the half,
   40.6mn in Q1-2026 — about **150-165mn a year**, a third of the assumption.
3. **Cost stack.** Study built feedstock/energy/chemicals/fixed from house estimates. ACTUAL note 15-A:
   raw materials **90.7%** of cost of sales, salaries 4.5%, other (gas/power/water/spares/EPROM) 4.2%,
   supporting materials 0.3%, depreciation 0.3%. The study's chemicals charge was ~5x too high and it
   carried no labour line at all.
4. **Product table.** Study used a 3-line build (base oils / wax / one fuel slate) from a REPORTED
   FY2023/24 table. The audited note 14-A gives **eight lines with tonnes and value** for the
   transition half, and the fuel slate is not one product: fuel oil (mix) alone is 57% of tonnage.
5. **NCI.** Study used 3.0% of group profit. ACTUAL **4.645%**, and the subsidiary is Alexandria Wax
   Products at 86.45% owned.
6. **Shareholder.** Study said EGPC is the second-largest shareholder at 20%. ACTUAL: **Alexandria
   Petroleum Company 20.77%**; EGPC is not a direct shareholder at all. EGPC is the dominant
   COUNTERPARTY (76.5% of sales, 16.3bn of purchases in the half), which is a different claim.
7. **Balance-sheet composition.** Study reconstructed PPE at 2,403mn against disclosed total assets;
   ACTUAL fixed assets 893.0mn + PUC 407.3mn = **1,300.3mn**. Inventory was understated (1,436 vs
   actual 2,847). Total assets matched only because the study anchored on the disclosed total.
8. **Provisions.** A **904.6mn tax-disputes provision** exists and was never modelled.
9. **Other income.** Study assumed zero from 2026 and called the historical figure devaluation FX
   gains. ACTUAL note 14-B: it is mostly **credit interest (173.2mn)** and **provision reversals
   (154.3mn)**; the FX gain line was zero in the transition half.
10. **Margin.** Q1-2026 gross margin is **10.19%** against 5.05% in Q1-2025 — the step the study
    deliberately forecast under. Six months to Dec-2025 printed 6.145%.
11. **Employees' profit share and BOD bonuses** (67.5mn in the half) reduce EPS and were not modelled.
12. **The offtake is not purely state.** Receivables run to Shell, ExxonMobil, Chevron, Total,
    TAQA/Castrol, Emarat, OLA Energy alongside the state companies.
