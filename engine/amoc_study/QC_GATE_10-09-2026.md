## VERDICT: NOT DELIVERABLE

**The model, the numbers file and the workbook are in good order, and the workbook repair
holds under an independent recalculation.** The three delivered documents are not. Four of
the five figures in the study publish **EGP 11.40** as the answer while every table in the
same document publishes **EGP 20.05**; one of those figures flatly contradicts its own
caption. Nine prose statements still describe an answer below the market price when the
answer is 48.5% above it. The bibliography is stamped to a five-week-old edition, carries a
section the model report forbids, documents a base-year construction the study abandoned,
and ships its negative-results section EMPTY. The file staged for publication is a
different rendering from the file in the study directory. The blocking list is at the end.
