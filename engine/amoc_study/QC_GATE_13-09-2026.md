## VERDICT

**NOT DELIVERABLE.** Fifteen defects reach a reader and block delivery; eight more are defects
of record or of construction.

What is in good order should not be disturbed. The study rebuilds byte-for-byte from its own
source — 9,041 workbook cells, 1,471 study units, 898 bibliography units, all zero differences
against the delivered files. The workbook is 96% live formula and recalculates completely under
an independent evaluator. The beta is conforming on its record against the published index of
the exchange the share is listed on. The waterfall runs to present value and through a bridge
that carries every disclosed claim. The cost-of-capital construction is genuinely built rather
than asserted, counts country risk once, and publishes both premium bases. Figure 4 now agrees
with its own caption, Table 10 no longer names a retired real-rate convention, and the staged
file in `publish_queue/` is byte-identical to the delivered file — four of the last gate's ten
findings are properly closed.

What blocks delivery sits in five places. The gross margin the whole valuation turns on is a
flat residual added on top of the unit build, worth EGP 7.20 of the EGP 20.05, and the study's
own record calls it an output. The filing that half the base year and 49 registered inputs rest
on is not in this repository. Figure 1 — the first picture a reader sees — still publishes the
superseded EGP 11.40 beside a table publishing 20.05, and the freshness gate built to catch
exactly that is defeated by the build manifest. The delivered workbook's first page still
describes the base-year construction this edition exists to replace. And three prose statements
still tell a reader the answer is below the market price on a study whose central is 48.5%
above it — one of them four lines after its own computed correction.
