## Why this name matters more than its size

It is the **last of the five studies still carrying the retired reinvestment identity**, and
it is the **only Egyptian one left** — so it is simultaneously the remaining terminal
rebuild and the single outstanding test of the jurisdictional hypothesis registered under
[L-328]: that the measured-age route works where a company writes off cost at disclosed
RATES with no residual (the Egyptian presentation, which is what unlocked EGCH) and fails
where the depreciable amount is cost less a residual (the IFRS form, which stopped ADNOCLS,
ADNOCDIST, BOROUGE and AMR on 5 September). Four failures against one success, one test
left. **That hypothesis is thin and stays thin until this name answers it.**

## What the retired construction implies here

reinvestment. Read as a capital-maintenance programme, the implied replacement cycle is

rebuild would be *expected* to raise this value; [CLAUSE TWO CORRECTED] is equally explicit
that the sign is measured per name and never predicted, and this file does not predict it.

**This base is OLDER than uniform — 13.64 against 9.83, thirty-nine per cent older — which
is the opposite of EGCH's** and consistent with the note's own summary that the plant is
67.4% written down. If the age turns out to be usable, the correction here would run the
opposite way to EGCH's: an older base costs MORE to replace at today's prices, so
maintenance rises and the value falls, against a defect that is otherwise pessimistic in
this market. **Two corrections pulling opposite ways on one name is exactly the case
[R-REBUILD-01]'s ledger exists to make visible**, and it is why the audit point must be
declared before the rebuild starts rather than after.

**One caveat on the implied life, recorded because it is the same caveat EGCH turned on.**
EGP 273,466,121 of assets are fully depreciated and still in production — **10.3% of the
depreciable cost**, against 1.3% at EGCH. Where a large share of a base sits past its
accounting life, gross-over-charge returns an ECONOMIC cycle longer than the accounting
life, so 19.67 years is an upper reading. It matters less for the AGE, which is what the
escalator uses, than for the life; but it means the half-life fallback here is standing on
a softer number than it looks.

## The default, and why it is not the naive age

If no document arrives by 12 September, **AMOC stays on the terminal ratchet with its
reason**, exactly as the other four do. It is NOT rebuilt on the naive age, even though the
error direction would be safe: an overstated age overstates maintenance and understates the
value, which is the conservative side. [L-328] already records a name where precisely that
argument was made and the figure was withdrawn anyway, and the reason holds here — **a safe
direction is not a reason to keep a number the accounts may say is not measuring what it
claims.**

## What arriving would settle, in one pass

- If the note writes off **cost at disclosed rates**: the age is measured, the terminal
  rebuilds, and the jurisdictional hypothesis has its second Egyptian success.
- If it writes off **cost less residual**: AMOC joins the other four, the hypothesis is
