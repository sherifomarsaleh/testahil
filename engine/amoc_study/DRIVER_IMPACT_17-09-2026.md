## What was found, and why none of it moves a driver

**The Intro Utilities CHP heads-of-terms (30 July 2026)** is real and independently reported
by two trade outlets, with named signatories and EGPC officials present: BOOT, 16MW expandable
to 30MW, USD 50mn, targeting 100% of electricity and 70% of steam, with a projected EGP 49mn a
year accruing to the company out of EGP 174mn total.

**It does not move a driver, for three reasons, and the third is decisive:**

1. It is a **memorandum of the basic terms for beginning a detailed technical study** — not a
   construction contract, not a financial close, not a commissioning date.
2. **Both sources are trade press.** The company's own media centre carries nothing after
   30 June 2026, so a USD 50mn agreement signed at its headquarters is absent from its own
   channel.
3. **EGP 49mn a year is a projected saving in a press account of a heads-of-terms.** Against
   this company's scale it is immaterial, and capitalising a projected saving from an
   unexecuted agreement is the opposite of the discipline this house applies to guidance.

**Registered as a WATCH item**, to be revisited when the company discloses it or a definitive
agreement is signed.
