## Verdict

**The answer is audited and it does not change for the price.** The correction that produced
it took a house macro rate off a house file, was owed under [R-MACRO-01], and would have been
taken in either direction.

**What a reader should weigh, and every item is larger than it was two days ago:** the central
rests on a single reviewed half whose margin is roughly double the half before it; the
terminal block is now 62.3% of the answer while the company's own reinvestment record implies
negative steady-state growth; and three of four cross-check lenses sit below the traded price
with the spread to the nearest one up by half.

**THIS IS NOT A STUDY WHOSE GAP SHOULD BE READ AS CONFIDENCE.** It is a study whose primary
lens sits on the newest evidence, with everything that disagrees published beside it and
priced. The one thing that would make it the study that is wrong is named and dated: if the
half to 31 December 2026 prints a gross margin back near the 6.1% of the transition half, the
anchor is a peak and not a level, and the bear corner at EGP 1.66 is the relevant number
rather than the central.
