## 3. MACRO COHERENCE

**This is where most of today's movement came from, and it is now coherent for the first
time.** The study previously carried its own Egyptian inflation path of 12.0 / 10.0 /
8.5 / 7.0 / 6.0 — a number that appears in no growth line and drove both the domestic
cost stack and, through the purchasing-power wedge, the entire currency path. Against the
house ladder of 16.0 / 12.0 / 9.0 / 7.5 / 7.0 it compounds 7.3% lower over the window and
terminates a point below the long-run rate the same model's terminal value uses.

The currency is now derived by relative purchasing-power parity on that ladder rather
than hand-set: EGP 55.11 → 70.11 to the dollar, against the previous edition's 50.5 →
57.7. The old path escalated domestic costs at Egyptian inflation while depreciating the
pound at roughly a third of the differential — two views of one economy inside one model.

**A registered tension in the house path, not in this study.** The derivation compounds a
full year of depreciation onto a quote taken *eight months into that year*, so FY2026
averages 56.87 against the same path's own spot of 50.25 on 6 August 2026 — which would
need roughly 70 by December. The study conforms rather than inventing an anchor, and this
is recorded for the pass that owns the house path. It will reach every Egyptian study
that commits a currency path. **Cleared for this study; escalated for the path.**

## 4. DISCOUNT RATE

Operations are discounted once, on a glide whose fractions are the cost-of-debt path's

## 5. TERMINAL

Rebuilt through the sanctioned construction on a **disclosed weighted useful life of
13.80 years**, derived from EIPICO's own FY2024 accounts: the estimated lives in note 3.1
(buildings 50, production and service machines 15, transport and tools 5, furniture 10,
land not depreciated) weighted by the gross cost by class in note 4.1, on a depreciable
base of EGP 2,701,916,171 that foots to the note's stated total once land is excluded.
Every life is a single figure rather than a band, which is unusual and makes the
weighting unambiguous.

Terminal growth is stored as **zero real** and the nominal rate derived at 7.0%. The
previous edition typed 5% and justified it as "approximately zero real terminal growth" —
which against a 7% long-run rate is a real **decline** of 1.87% a year, in perpetuity.
The sentence asserted the opposite of what the number did.

**And the direction of the retired defect is the opposite of what its own rule predicts.**
The reinvestment identity implies a replacement cycle of 1/g; at the old 5% that is 20.0
years against a disclosed 13.80, so it **under**-charged — in a high-inflation economy,
where the standing account says 1/g runs short and starves the asset. The direction is set
by the **typed growth rate**, not by the market: a study that types its terminal growth
away from inflation can land either way whatever its currency does. **Cleared.**

## What this review concludes

The gap is **not** explained, and the honest reading is that the corrections have
overshot on this name.

**Where the remaining error most likely is, in order:**

1. **The currency path, and the direction is counter-intuitive.** EIPICO is **net short
   hard currency**: dollar-linked revenue is 33% of the total while cost of sales is 61%
   and its raw materials are predominantly imported active ingredients. So a weaker pound
   *destroys* value here, and the house derivation takes the pound 21% weaker by FY2030
   than the previous edition did. The house path's own first-year tension (heading 3) runs
   in the same direction. **If that first year is wrong, this study is wrong with it.**
2. **The biosimilars plant carries no revenue line at all.** The model charges its
   depreciation and its interest — both mechanical consequences of the company's own
   disclosed December-2025 licensing — and books nothing against them, because the company
   has published no volume, price or utilisation guidance. That is the right treatment of
   an undisclosed quantity and it is a *one-sided* omission: every cost, no revenue. The
   reverse read says the plant must reach about USD 68 million a year of revenue by
   FY2030E to close the gap to the market, against the USD 100 million the company says it
   invested — a hurdle a reader can test against the first year the company discloses
   biosimilar revenue.
3. **The terminal risk-free rate at 12.5% may be right for the house and wrong for this
   company.** A third of revenue is earned in dollars; a company whose cash flows are
   partly hard-currency discounted entirely at an Egyptian-pound terminal rate is charged
   country risk on earnings that do not bear all of it. That is a live method question
   across this book, it is registered, and it is **not** resolved by moving one study's
   rate.

**No number was moved toward the price.** A fair value adjusted to meet a quote is the
reverse-engineered rate this method prohibits outright, and the honest output of a review
is frequently an unchanged answer with a stated reason. What is stated here instead is
that the answer is held, the corrections stand, and the three candidates above are what a
further pass must price before this study is delivered to anyone.
