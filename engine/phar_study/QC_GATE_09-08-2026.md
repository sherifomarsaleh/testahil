Two expectations in the test harness failed after these changes and were investigated before
anything was altered, because the standing rule is that the first hypothesis is a stale
expectation rather than a broken model. Both were stale. The statutory tax rate no longer
taxes free cash flow — it now sizes only the interest shield, so raising it RAISES value — and
bringing construction into service earlier now helps rather than hurts, because the explicit-
year tax shield (+22.5 on the sum of present values) and the smaller never-depreciated balance
left for the terminal block to charge (+21.5) both point the same way. The decompositions are
recorded in `driver_test.py` beside the expectations they replaced.
