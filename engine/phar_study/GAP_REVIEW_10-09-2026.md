## What moved: the terminal discount rate, and it cut both ways

The principal supplied seven EFG Hermes reports covering Egypt, the Gulf and Saudi Arabia
between November 2023 and June 2026. Two facts in them bear on every Egyptian valuation
this house holds.

**Their Egyptian terminal cost of capital does not move with the cycle** — 15.1% for Edita
in October 2025, 14.2% for EIPICO and 14.8% for a second pharmaceutical name in June 2026,
across a devaluation and a full rate cycle. **And it is nearly company-independent**: three
very different businesses inside a single point.

Two changes followed, both instructed:

| | Retired | Adopted |
|---|---|---|
| Egypt long-run real risk-free | 5.50% | **3.50%** |
| Terminal beta | measured (0.6658) | **1.00** |

**THE TWO PULL IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS ON THIS NAME, AND THAT IS THE POINT.** The real-rate
cut lowers the terminal rate and raised this study to 112.80 / 134.79. The beta
convergence then RAISES it, because EIPICO's measured beta is 0.6658 — BELOW one — so
converging toward the market makes its terminal cost of equity higher, not lower. The
answer lands at 85.25 / 102.89, marginally below where it started the day.

| | Terminal rf | Terminal Ke | Terminal WACC | Frame A | Frame B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 September | 12.50% | 17.16% | 15.22% | 88.52 | 106.68 |
| real-rate cut alone | 10.50% | 15.16% | 13.73% | 112.80 | 134.79 |
| **both changes** | 10.50% | **17.50%** | **15.47%** | **85.25** | **102.89** |

**A rule applied only where it helps is not a rule.** The convergence was adopted because
today's beta should not describe a business in perpetuity, and it costs this study 27.55
on Frame A. It is applied here for the same reason it was applied to a study it helped.

The terminal WACC of 15.47% sits just above EFG's 14.2–15.1% band, against 15.22% before —
so on this name the two changes very nearly cancel, and the agreement with the outside
benchmark was already close.

## A defect found while making the change

The sensitivity harness re-implemented the terminal cost of equity with the SENSITIVITY's
beta rather than the terminal beta, so once the two differed it valued a company the base
case had stopped describing. It refused, loudly, on its own reconciliation assert —
85.2465 against 112.7993 — which is the assert doing exactly its job. It now reads the
same construction as the base case. This is the second instance of the same shape found
today: a fix that goes into one of two functions doing the same job leaves the other one
a defect.
