This study's register carries a dated negative search from 2026-08-09: no volume, price or
utilisation guidance for the **biologicals facility**, sought across the annual reports, the
investor presentation and every 2026 press release on the company's own site — "the plant's

**Both engines re-ran it and both came back empty**, and what they found in the meantime is
what makes the re-confirmation worth more than the original:

- **EIPICO's own 2025-results release** (`eipico.com.eg`, which is **reachable** from here,
  HTTP 200) says EIPICO 3 has received operating licences from the **Egyptian Drug Authority**
  and the **Industrial Development Authority**, has **launched its first biological product,
  ADALIMAB**, and was expected to begin operations shortly.
- A report indexed 23 August attributes to the Ministry of Industry an **inauguration of
  EIPICO 3** in 10th of Ramadan City, investment **above USD 100 million**.

**The plant is licensed, inaugurated, and has a product in the market — and the company still
publishes no volume, utilisation, price, revenue or margin figure for it.** That is not a
failed search; it is a stronger negative than the one on file, because the facility has
passed every milestone at which a company that intended to guide would have guided.

**Consequence for the model: none, and that is the finding.** "Biologicals facility revenue"
is a committed **TOP_DOWN** driver precisely because the disclosure does not support a
bottom-up build. Two independent re-searches past three milestones confirm the treatment
rather than challenging it. **The negative search is re-dated to 2026-09-17, not replaced.**

The other two standing negatives — a counterparty or ageing breakdown of the expected-credit-
