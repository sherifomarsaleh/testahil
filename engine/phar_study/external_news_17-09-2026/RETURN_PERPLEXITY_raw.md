## Biologicals facility

The material post-cutoff facility development I found is NOT NEW after 9 August, but is worth flagging because it provides a primary-source path. EIPICO's own 2025-results release says EIPICO 3 received operating licences from the Egyptian Drug Authority and Industrial Development Authority, launched its first biological product, ADALIMAB, and was expected to begin operations shortly. It also says the plant is designed to make biologicals and biosimilars from cell to finished product. Crucially, the company still gives NO volume, utilization, selling-price, revenue, margin, or commissioning-date guidance for the facility.

An independent report indexed on 23 August attributes to the Ministry of Industry an inauguration of EIPICO 3 in 10th of Ramadan City with investment above US$100 million. This is a useful lead for the Ministry's original announcement, but it does not itself establish revenue timing or financial contribution.

Implication: The plant has moved beyond a purely prospective project narrative — licensing, first-product launch, and inauguration are all reported — but the key valuation bridge remains missing: commercial-scale output, reimbursement/pricing, utilization ramp, and unit economics.
