**VERDICT: NO DRIVER MOVES. One standing negative search is materially strengthened.**


## The biologicals driver — the treatment is confirmed, not challenged

This study's register carries a dated negative search from 2026-08-09: **no volume, price or
utilisation guidance for the biologicals facility**, sought across the annual reports, the
investor presentation and every 2026 press release on the company's own site.

**Both engines re-ran it and both came back empty** — and what they found meanwhile is what
makes the re-confirmation worth more than the original. From **EIPICO's own 2025-results
release** (`eipico.com.eg` is reachable, HTTP 200): EIPICO 3 has received operating licences
from the **Egyptian Drug Authority** and the **Industrial Development Authority**, has
**launched its first biological product, ADALIMAB**, and was expected to begin operations
shortly. A report indexed 23 August attributes to the Ministry of Industry an **inauguration**
of the facility, investment above **USD 100 million**.

**The plant is licensed, inaugurated and has a product in the market, and the company still
publishes no volume, utilisation, price, revenue or margin figure for it.** That is a stronger
negative than the one on file, because the facility has passed every milestone at which a
company intending to guide would have guided.

**Consequence: none, and that is the finding.** "Biologicals facility revenue" is a committed
**TOP_DOWN** driver precisely because the disclosure does not support a bottom-up build. Two
independent re-searches past three milestones confirm the treatment. **The negative search is
re-dated to 2026-09-17, not replaced.**

The other two standing negatives — a counterparty or ageing breakdown of the expected-credit-
loss allowance, and standalone accounts for either equity-accounted associate — were re-run
and also came back empty.
