**THE ENTIRE FIFTEEN-POINT DISCOUNT IS THE BLEND.** This study's own cash-flow work lands on
the traded price to within five hundredths of one per cent, and then three lenses that value
an offshore driller on reported accounting earnings and historical-cost book pull the
published answer 15% below it.

## What the rule says, twice over

[R-LENS-03]: **one class primary IS the central**; every other lens is a cross-check
published in the same table; the envelope is the RANGE of the present-value reads, **never
averaged into the answer**. And separately: **book value is a disclosed floor, published as
such and NEVER weighted.** It carries 15% here.

The nearest registered class — *marine logistics and shipping, chartered fleet on global day
rates* — makes the primary a **DCF**, with replacement cost, an EV multiple on own history, a
relative multiple, SOTP and book beside it as cross-checks.

Under the rule the answer is one of the two cash-flow framings with the other published
beside it, and the envelope is **5.40 to 6.21**. **The market price of 5.80 sits inside that
range**, near its middle. There is no market disagreement here to explain.

**Why no gate saw it.** This study commits **no `lens_record` and no class**, so
`assert_lens_design()` cannot examine it at all — the same hole ADNOCDIST has. A study that
declares nothing is not a study that conforms; it is a study nothing is looking at
[R-ENF-04].

## This is the PHDC precedent, sharper

[R-LENS-03] was adopted on PHDC, where *"the cash-flow lens landed within 2.2% of the market;
the blend landed 28% below"*. Here the cash-flow work lands within **0.046%** and the blend
lands **15.2%** below. Same architecture, same direction, and the weights here have cleared
no out-of-sample test either — chosen, written down, inherited.

**The general point, which is not about this company:** a number produced by averaging
several methods is not more robust than the best of them. It is a new method with free
parameters nobody tested, wearing the appearance of caution, and it imports every weakness of
the weakest lens at whatever weight somebody typed.
