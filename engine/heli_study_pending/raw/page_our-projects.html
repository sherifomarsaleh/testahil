    BRONZE VILLAS Project

    The 24-Villa Project is a residential compound consisting of 24 villas (Models A & B) in Block 52F (Bronze),in the 5th District.

    Each villa consists of: Ground Floor + 1 Upper Floor, where the built-up Area per Villa is 297 m² with a maximum height of 10 meters.

    JEWELS Project

    Jewels Project consists of 10 residential buildings in the 9th District.
    This is the 3rd phase of a project that contains a construction of 28 buildings in Block 91A of the 9th District.

    The project consists of three phases:
    The 1st phase: 7 buildings.
    The 2nd phase: 11 buildings.
    The 3rd phase (Jewels): 10 buildings.
    Jewels Project encompasses 10 buildings of types (B – C – D), and all types consist of (Basement + Ground Floor + 4 Repeated Floors).

    MEDALLION Project

    Medallion Project has a total of 85 residential units and 29 Commercial Shops.
    It consists of 6 residential and mixed-use (residential & commercial) buildings in the 4th District, Block 40A (Phase Three) — Buildings (8–9–10–11–12–13) assorted as the following:

    Building 8 – Model (N-C), mixed-use:
    Ground Floor: 10 Retail Shops + 1 Residential Unit
    4 Repeated Floors: 3 Residential Units per floor
    Building 9 – Model (M-C), mixed-use:
    Ground Floor: 9 Retail Shops + 3 Residential Units
    4 Repeated Floors: 3 Residential Units per floor
    Building 10 – Model (N-C) Reversed-mixed-use:
    Ground Floor: 10 Retail Shops + 1 Residential Unit
    4 Repeated Floors: 3 Residential Units per floor
    Building 11 – Model (N-r) Residential, building 12 – Model (M-r) Residential, and building 13 – Model (N-r) Reversed-residential:
    Ground Floor: 3 Residential Units
    4 Repeated Floors: 3 Residential Units per floor

    SCARLET Project

    Scarlet Project consists of 6 residential buildings in Block 36A, 3rd District, with a total of 94 residential units (68 apartments + 26 Villas).
    Each building consists of: Basement + Ground Floor + 4 Repeated Floors, assorted as the following:

    Buildings (1–6) has a total of 14 Residential Units + 1 Villa:
    Basement: 1 Lower Villa Floor
    Ground Floor: 1 Upper Villa Floor + 2 Apartments
    1st , 2nd , 3rd , and 4th Floors: 3 Apartments per floor
    Buildings (2–3–4–5) has a total of 10 Residential Units + 6 Villa:
    Basement: 2 Lower Villa Floors
    Ground Floor: 2 Upper Villa Floors + 2 Apartments
    1st & 3rd Floors: 2 Lower Villa Floors + 2 Apartments
    2nd & 4th Floors: 2 Upper Villa Floors + 2 Apartments
