such as interest rates, foreign exchange rates and equity prices, whether those changes are caused by
factors specific to the individual investment or its issuer or factors affecting all investments traded in

- Market risk is managed on the basis of pre-determined asset allocations across various asset categories,
diversification of assets in terms of geographical distribution and industry concentration, a
