**VERDICT: NO DRIVER MOVES. Central unchanged at EGP 0.3357. Two structural defects are recorded and neither is fixed here.**


## The two defects

**1. The share count is vendor-sourced.** This study divides by 3,313.540373mn shares on the
authority of a **Mubasher profile**, dated "2026 (mid)". That is a data vendor standing in for
the issuer on the single divisor the whole per-share answer rests on, and the date is not a
date. The arithmetic foots (EGP 662,708,074.60 ÷ 0.20 par) and both returns give the same
paid-up capital independently, **so the number is probably right and its provenance is not.**

**2. The company's own channel carries nothing.** `egyptelectriccables.com` is reachable
(HTTP 200) and is a **product-marketing site with no investor-relations section of any kind** —
28 pages of products, gallery, services, contact, a copper and aluminium reference page and a
catalogue PDF. Guessed IR paths all 404. **The primary route for this issuer is the EGX
disclosure portal, which refuses from this environment.**
