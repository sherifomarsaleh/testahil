## 1. PRIMARY ACCESS — REACHABLE, AND EMPTY. THAT IS THE FINDING.

`egyptelectriccables.com` returns **HTTP 200**. It is a **product-marketing site with no
investor-relations section of any kind**: 28 internal pages — products, gallery, services,
contact, an Arabic market-data page carrying live copper and aluminium reference charts — and
a product catalogue PDF. **No financial statements, no disclosures, no governance documents,
no shareholder information.** Guessed IR paths (`/investor-relations`, `/investors`, `/ir`,
`/financial-information`) all return 404.

This is the opposite result to the two names before it, and it matters more than a refusal
would: AMOC and ARCC were blocked by a proxy and turned out to publish everything; **ELEC
publishes nothing for an investor and is reachable while doing so.** The primary route for
this issuer is the EGX disclosure portal, which refuses from this environment.

**Registered as a dated negative search**, and it corroborates this study's own note that
"the statements themselves are not held."

## 2. THE SHARE COUNT IS VENDOR-SOURCED, AND IT IS ABOUT TO CHANGE

This study divides by **3,313.540373mn shares**, and its own input register records the
source as:

    Mubasher ELEC profile (paid-in capital EGP 662,708,074.60 / par 0.20)
    date: "2026 (mid)"

**That is a data vendor standing in for the issuer's own disclosure, on the single divisor
the whole per-share answer rests on** — SIGCM clause 1, and the same shape as the composite
beta. The date is not a date. The arithmetic foots (662,708,074.60 ÷ 0.20 = 3,313,540,373)
and **both returns independently give the identical paid-up capital**, so the number is
probably right; **its provenance is not**, and "probably right" is not the standard.

## 3. THE CAPITAL INCREASE — BOTH ENGINES AGREE, AND THE ARITHMETIC FOOTS THREE WAYS

Board approved **9 August 2026**:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Authorised capital | EGP 850,000,000 | EGP 3,000,000,000 |
| Issued and paid-up | EGP 662,708,074.60 | EGP 1,312,708,074.60 |
| Shares | 3,313,540,373 | **6,563,540,373** |
| Par | EGP 0.20 | EGP 0.20 |

3,250,000,000 new shares × EGP 0.20 = **EGP 650,000,000 cash**, offered to existing holders.
Every one of those three identities closes. **The share count would very nearly double.**

**This study was struck on 2026-08-05 — four days BEFORE the board resolution.** It could not
have known, and this is not a defect in it.

### 3.1 Priced rather than caveated — ORDER OF MAGNITUDE ONLY

Taking the study's own lens values as equity value, adding EGP 650mn of cash and dividing by
the post-issue count:

| | equity (EGP mn) | + cash | ÷ 6,563.5mn | vs today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bear 0.1826 | 605.0 | 1,255.0 | **0.1912** | **+4.7%** |
| base 0.3357 | 1,112.4 | 1,762.4 | **0.2685** | **−20.0%** |
| bull 0.9488 | 3,143.9 | 3,793.9 | **0.5780** | **−39.1%** |

**The direction is not uniform and that is the whole point of computing it.** Injecting cash
at par into a company the study values *below* par is accretive at the bear end and heavily
dilutive at the bull end — the raise transfers value toward the cash and away from the
operating business, so the more the business is worth, the more the issue costs.

**Every assumption here is unestablished**: that the raise completes, that it completes in
full, that it is priced at par, that the cash arrives with nothing else changing, and that
the study's central is the equity value. **Nothing moves on this.** What settles it is the
board-resolution disclosure, the EGM approval, the FRA approval and the subscription
timetable — all EGX documents, none reachable from here.

## 5. OPERATING LEADS — ONE SOURCE READ TWICE IS NOT TWO CONFIRMATIONS

**Zain Saudi Arabia fibre framework, up to SAR 100mn.** Both returns carry it. Both trace to
the same July press report, and the language in it is "**final stage of signing**" — not an
executed contract, not an award notice, not backlog. Perplexity searched for a Zain primary
announcement or an EGX disclosure and **found neither**. **Two engines quoting one article is
one source, not corroboration**, and it is registered that way.

**The 80–90% local-content claim** is management commentary in that same article. No
technical release, audited note, procurement document or filing substantiates it. A diligence
question, not an operating assumption.

**Gemini additionally describes Giza Cable Industries as "the corporate group's core operating
subsidiary"** and has it bidding a 402 MVA substation. That is a **structural claim about
what this issuer consolidates**, it appears in neither the study nor the other return, and it
would change what the accounts even cover. **Unverified, and flagged as the single most
consequential unchecked assertion in this pair.**

**Both engines: searched, not found** — no plant commissioning or capacity addition, no
litigation, no tax assessment, no FRA sanction, no competitor or regulator statement, no
executive change beyond the 6 May board election.

## 6. WHAT THIS PAIR ESTABLISHES ABOUT THE STUDY ITSELF

ELEC publishes a central of **EGP 0.3357 against a spot of EGP 2.19** struck 2026-08-05 —
**−84.7%**, by some distance the largest gap in this pack. That study is held from publishing
and is right to be. The returns do not close the gap and were never going to: what they
supply is that **the divisor is vendor-sourced, the divisor is about to change by ~98%, the
issuer's own channel carries nothing, and the statements are not held.** Those are four
reasons to re-open the name before anyone argues about the answer.
