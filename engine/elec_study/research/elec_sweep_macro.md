### 1.2 Copper — 12-month trend and tariff dynamics
- **Finding:** Copper closed **2025 ~42% higher** (LME above $12,000/t, a record; COMEX ~$5.72/lb at year-end 2025), then climbed to a new COMEX high of **$6.73/lb on 03-Jun-2026** before consolidating; ~$6.63/lb in early Aug-2026. Analysts (INN consensus) project **average LME 2026 ~$12,600/t (+26% vs 2025 average)**; Goldman Sachs sees possible **>$14,000/t in H2-2026** if tariffs bite. Drivers: mine supply disruptions, smelter concentrate shortage, AI/data-center-driven electricity demand.

### 3.4 Copper import dynamics under FX constraints
- **Finding:** Egypt imported **$2.31bn of copper & articles in 2024**; copper cathode/rod is essentially fully imported and USD-priced. FX availability remains the binding constraint of the economy (McKinsey); import prioritization persists — strategic/industrial inputs get FX allocation priority, but the 2023-24 LC crisis showed cable makers' exposure to import financing. With copper up ~50% y/y in USD and EGP flat, **EGP working-capital needs per ton of output have roughly doubled since 2024** — inventory gains flatter P&Ls in rising markets but strain funding at 20%+ lending rates.
- **Source:** Trading Economics Egypt copper imports; McKinsey FX growth sectors; WorldsTopExports. **URLs:** https://tradingeconomics.com/egypt/imports/copper ; https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/middle-east-and-africa/engines-of-growth-foreign-exchange-at-the-heart-of-egypts-journey **Date:** 2024 data / 2025-26 analysis.

### 3.5 Pricing pass-through practice
- **Finding:** Egyptian cable pricing is driven by three volatile inputs — **copper (single most influential), energy, FX** — and **LME moves are "directly and rapidly reflected" in quotations; contracts commonly carry raw-material price-adjustment clauses**. Government tenders decided on price competitiveness + local content. Implication: gross margins are structurally thin and quasi-tolling; value accrues to players with scale, cheap funding, and export access (Elsewedy model). Lag risk sits in fixed-price public tenders during copper spikes.
- **Source:** IndexBox Egypt MV cables report. **URL:** https://www.indexbox.io/store/egypt-medium-voltage-cables-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/ **Date:** 2025-26 edition.

### 3.6 Export opportunity
- **Finding:** Cables are one of Egypt's strongest manufactured exports (see 3.3); EGP real appreciation erodes competitiveness at the margin, but USD-linked pricing of cable exports makes exporters a natural FX hedge — a key differentiator between Elsewedy (large export/off-shore book) and ELEC (predominantly domestic). EU grid-link ambitions (post-2030 GREGY) and African grid programs are the long-run export thesis.
- **Source:** WorldsTopExports; Middle East Observer (EU grid export groundwork, 28-Jul-2026). **URLs:** https://www.worldstopexports.com/egypts-top-10-exports/ ; https://meobserver.news/energy/2026/07/28/egypts-e690mn-grid-upgrade-lays-groundwork-for-european-power-exports/ **Date:** 2025–Jul-2026.
