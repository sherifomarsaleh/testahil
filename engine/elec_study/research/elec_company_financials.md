### Accumulated losses / equity restructuring history
- The company historically carried accumulated losses (loss-making through the 2010s under Pioneers ownership; returned to sustained profitability around **2021** — FY2021 NP EGP 224.6 m, FY2022 542.2 m {Zawya, Nov-2023}).
- **A capital reduction was proposed/announced 02-Mar-2023** {Mubasher news listing extract, https://english.mubasher.info/markets/EGX/stocks/ELEC/news/} — **execution status not verifiable** from accessible sources (**flag**). Note the paid-in capital today (662.7 m) equals shares × 0.20 par with no post-2021 change visible, so if a reduction was executed it acted on reserves/accumulated losses rather than share count.
- EGM approved a **1:5 share split** (par EGP 1.00 → EGP 0.20); EGX approved listing of split shares ~**24-Jan-2021** {Mubasher news/Investing.com splits page extracts}.
- By FY2024 the group showed positive equity ~EGP 3.6 bn against paid-in capital of 0.66 bn, i.e. **retained earnings/reserves turned strongly positive** {SWS health page, 22-May-2025}.
