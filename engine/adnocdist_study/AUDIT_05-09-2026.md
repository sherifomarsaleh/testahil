**Dated 5 September 2026.** Written from outside the study, verified against its own
committed record and its own builders, and **nothing here is repaired in this pass**:

## 1. The published central is the retired four-lens blend, with book value weighted

`lenses.centre_A` = **4.411292110312444** reproduces to **8.9e-16** as

    0.40 x 4.783957863288807   cash flow   (dcf/frame_A/per_share)
  + 0.25 x 4.013924856360926   normalised earnings
  + 0.20 x 4.953421712278697   relative multiple
  + 0.15 x 3.356956056339670   book and sustainable return
  = 4.411292110312445

and `centre_B` = 4.582096886797197 the same way on the Frame B reads. The workbook computes
it directly: `Fundamental Valuation!B7 = =B2*D2+B3*D3+B4*D4+B5*D5`.

[R-LENS-03] forbids this **twice over** — once because one class primary IS the central and
a blend is a new method with parameters nobody tested, and once because **book value is a
disclosed floor, published as such and NEVER weighted**. Three of the four lenses here value
the company on reported accounting earnings and historical-cost book.

It is the answer the repository itself reads: `check_valuation_gap.py` resolves this study
through `lenses.centre_A` / `centre_B`.

**Why no gate saw it.** `check_lens_vocabulary`'s BLEND pattern spelled the noun CENTRAL in
every alternative and this document says **CENTRE** — five times, in a Table 1 row reading
"WEIGHTED CENTRE — Frame A" with "cash flow 40%, normalised earnings 25%, relative 20%, book
and return 15%" printed beside it. Measured: `weighted centre` occurs 5 times, `weighted
central` never. That hole is closed as of 5 September and this study is now on that gate's
ratchet. `check_lens_design` cannot see it either, because **this study commits no
`lens_record` at all**.

Correcting this moves the central and is therefore the re-issue itself, not a fix in
passing. The study's own `GAP_REVIEW_05-09-2026.md` prices the correction at +0.3727 /
+0.5186 a share.

## 3. Table 12 — a ten-times unit error across every forecast cell, beside a correct base

`docx_adnocdist.py:702-705` renders the base column `n1(UB[...] * 100)` and the forecast
columns `n1(x * 1000)`:

| row | base | forecast cells as printed | forecast cells as they should read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retail margin per litre (fils) | 36.0 | 366.9 · 374.3 · 381.8 · 389.4 · 397.2 | 36.7 · 37.4 · 38.2 · 38.9 · 39.7 |
| Commercial margin per litre (fils) | 43.6 | 510.5 · 520.7 · 531.1 · 541.8 · 552.6 | 51.1 · 52.1 · 53.1 · 54.2 · 55.3 |

against `forecast.margin_retail` = [0.36694, 0.37428, 0.38177, 0.38940, 0.39719] AED/litre.
**One row, two scale factors.** This is the table that is the study's whole evidence for
margins being outputs.

Two more defects in the same table:

- The column is **headed FY2025 and carries H1-2026 figures**: the base cells read
  `margin_retail_h126` (0.35975 → 36.0) and `margin_comm_h126` (0.43634 → 43.6), while
  `margin_retail_fy25` (0.35428 → 35.4) and `margin_comm_fy25` (0.36740 → 36.7) sit
  committed and unused. The commercial base is overstated **18.8%**. Every other row in that
  column *is* FY2025.
- The caption at line 712 reads "Retail margin per litre begins at 36.0 fils in FY2025 —
  comfortably above the 45-fils floor the parent supply agreement guarantees." **36.0 is
  below 45**, and the study's own sweep entry CO-08 registers the floor at 45.0.

No repository instrument reaches any of it: `prose_figures.NUM` matches only a numeral
immediately followed by a per-cent, an x or "times", so a bare `366.9` in a cell is never
examined. The scale mismatch is found by asking whether a **column is internally
consistent** — which nothing does.

## What this list is, and what it is not

Findings 2 to 6 do not move the fair value; finding 1 does. They are held together because
repairing the five while leaving the first would produce a document that is internally
tidier and still publishes a blend this house retired — and because the re-issue that fixes
the first has to rebuild the tables the others live in anyway.

Nothing here is a modelling error of the *the company turned out differently* kind. Every
one is the *the document does not describe the model* kind, which is the class that reaches
a reader while every process gate reports clean.
