# STC — the terminal, and the first name in a while where the identity actually works

**5 September 2026.** Fifth of the names carrying the retired terminal construction to be
re-tested, and the first to come through. Written so the rebuild does not re-derive it.

## Why a life has to be derived here at all

Note 10 of the FY2025 audited statements gives useful lives as RANGES, not figures:

> Buildings **25 – 50 years** · Telecommunication network and equipment **3 – 30 years** ·
> Other assets **2 – 20 years**

[R-TERM-01] needs a life and **a life this desk chose is not a disclosed life**, so a
midpoint is not available. What is available is the identity [L-309] established on SAVOLA:
note 10 discloses gross cost AND the year's own charge, and **gross cost over charge IS the
weighted life**. An identity is not an assumption.

## [L-328]'s three conditions, checked on the policy note FIRST

This is where the last four names stopped, so it is where this one starts.

**(i) Residual value — CLEAR.** Note 4.11:

> *"Depreciation is charged using mainly the straight-line method **over the estimated
> useful lives** of property and equipment, other than land."*

The charge is cost over life. It is NOT cost less a residual over life — compare ADNOC
Distribution's *"to allocate their cost **to their residual values** over their estimated
useful lives"* and Americana's *"at rates calculated to **reduce the cost of assets to their
estimated residual value**"*, both of which broke the identity. The whole FY2025 document
contains three occurrences of "residual value": the IAS 16 review sentence at note 4.11
("residual values, useful lives and methods of depreciation are reviewed at each financial
year end"), one about lease **residual value guarantees**, and one about an investment-
property **valuation technique**. No residual is deducted in the charge and none is
quantified anywhere.

**(ii) A life reassessed mid-life — CLEAR.** Note 5.2.2 carries only the standard sentence
("asset lives are assessed annually and changed where necessary"). A search of the FY2025
statements for a change in accounting estimate on property and equipment returns nothing but
boilerplate on consolidation, business combinations and leases. There is no quantified
event, and that is the distinction that matters: ADNOC Distribution disclosed one with a
number on it — a lower charge of AED 90,917 thousand, 15.3% of the year's entire charge —
and that is what closed the route there.

**(iii) A base assembled by business combination — CLEAR at FY2025.** "Effect of acquisition
of new subsidiaries" adds SAR 9,898 thousand of cost against a base of 141,541,105 —
**0.007%**. FY2024 carried a large DISPOSAL (note 14.1) rather than an acquisition, removing
cost of 11,329,587 and accumulated of 3,302,684; that moves both columns together and is
noted below where it shows.

## The identity, on three filed years

All figures SAR thousands, read from each year's own audited note by text layer (all five
filings carry a real one; no OCR was needed), FY2023 taken from the FY2023 statements **as
originally reported** rather than from the later comparative.

| | FY2025 | FY2024 | FY2023 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cost | 141,541,105 | 136,362,024 | 141,045,871 |
| less capital work in progress | (4,910,376) | (3,829,207) | (3,536,015) |
| less land (note 10(1)) | (1,996,000) | (1,835,000) | (1,898,000) |
| **depreciable gross cost** | **134,634,729** | **130,697,817** | **135,611,856** |
| the year's own charge | 6,453,343 | 6,690,097 | 6,834,977 |
| accumulated depreciation | 98,254,770 | 94,847,791 | 92,944,538 |
| **implied life, gross over charge** | **20.86 y** | **19.54 y** | **19.84 y** |
| **measured age, accumulated over charge** | **15.23 y** | **14.18 y** | **13.60 y** |
| depreciated share of the base | 73.0% | 72.6% | 68.5% |

**Every year foots**: cost less accumulated reproduces the stated net book value to the
riyal in all three (43,286,335 / 41,514,233 / 48,101,333). Arithmetic is the arbiter, and it
agrees three times.

**The age moves the way a year passing should**: 13.60 → 14.18 → 15.23. The 2024 step is
+0.58 rather than about +1.0, and the reason is in the same note — that year's disposal of
subsidiaries removed a base only 29% depreciated against a pool at about 70%, which moves
the ratio; the 2025 step of +1.05 on a year with no such event is the clean one.

## What this says about the terminal, and it is not what the ratio-flag predicted

**The base is OLD.** The measured age of 15.23 years sits at **1.46 times half the implied
life** — the opposite of ADNOC Distribution's textbook steady state, and the opposite again
of EGCH's young plant at 4.45 years against an 11.04-year half-life. Seventy-three per cent
of the depreciable base is written off. On [R-TERM-01]'s construction, maintenance at
CURRENT cost is book depreciation escalated over that age, and a fifteen-year escalation is
a large multiple of the book charge, not a rounding on it.

**The direction is not predicted here.** [R-TERM-01 CLAUSE TWO CORRECTED] is explicit that
the sign of a terminal rebuild is measured per name and never read off a ratio, on the
evidence of a name that read 0.26x and came out roughly 5% HIGHER. Nothing above claims to
know which way this one moves before it runs.

## The one limitation, stated with its direction

The note's line is **"Depreciation and impairment"**, and no impairment figure for property
and equipment is separately disclosed, so both derived quantities carry whatever impairment
is inside the charge. Three things bound it:

- **The ratio that matters most does not depend on the charge at all.** The depreciated
  share — accumulated over depreciable gross, 73.0% — has no charge in it, and it is what
  establishes that the base is old.
- **The charge series is smooth**: 6,834,977 → 6,690,097 → 6,453,343, changes of −2.1% and
  −3.5% on a base that grew. A material impairment in any year would show as a spike and
  none does.
- **The direction is known.** Impairment inside the charge inflates the denominator of both
  identities, so it SHORTENS the measured life and age, which UNDER-states the escalation to
  current cost, which UNDER-states maintenance, which OVER-states the value. Using the
  note's own column is therefore the optimistic reading of the two available, and it is used
  because it is the column that foots.

Note 10(3) allocates SAR 6,085,426 (2024: 6,205,009) of depreciation and impairment to
operating cost lines from CONTINUING operations, against the roll-forward's 6,453,343 and
6,690,097. That difference is not an impairment split — note 10(3) is also "depreciation and
impairment" — and it is not adopted for anything here.

## What the rebuild does with this

The life is **20.86 years** on the latest filed year, with 19.54 and 19.84 behind it, and
the base is **15.23 years old**. Both come from the company's own note by an identity the
protocol already sanctions for capex, and both are labelled DERIVED. `terminal_value.build()`
takes them. What it must NOT be given is a life anybody here chose.
