# STC — the base extracted from the audited statements, and it foots

**5 September 2026.** The first material of a proper inputs register. Every figure is read
from Saudi Telecom Company's own consolidated financial statements for the year ended
31 December 2025 (audited by Deloitte and Touche & Co., text layer, no OCR needed), note 9,
"analysis of the Group's revenues and results from continuing operations based on segments".
SAR thousands. **Arithmetic is the arbiter and it agrees in both years.**

## The debt book and the cash, and two traps in reading them

From note 42.6's liquidity table (carrying amounts) and note 20, SAR thousands:

| | FY2025 | FY2024 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Borrowings, carrying amount (note 26) | 15,191,428 | 15,131,739 |
| Lease liabilities, carrying amount (note 28) | 2,253,230 | 2,164,085 |
| **Gross debt including leases** | **17,444,658** | **17,295,824** |
| Cash — banking operations (STC Bank) | 6,214,118 | 3,383,031 |
| Cash — non-banking operations | 7,161,953 | 12,160,410 |
| **Total cash and cash equivalents (note 20)** | **13,376,071** | **15,543,441** |

Both cash lines foot to their stated total in both years.

**TRAP ONE, AND IT WAS NEARLY WALKED INTO.** Note 42.6's columns are *carrying amount ·
one year or less · above one year but less than five · above five years* — UNDISCOUNTED
CASH FLOWS in the last three. Adding the first two columns to get gross debt reads a
maturity bucket as a balance and overstates borrowings by about 7%. The carrying amount is
the first column and nothing else.

**TRAP TWO, AND IT IS A BRIDGE CHOICE RATHER THAN A READING ERROR.** SAR 6.2bn of the
FY2025 cash is **STC Bank's**, and a regulated bank's cash and balances with the central
bank are its working capital rather than surplus an equity holder can have. Treating the
13.4bn total as net-debt-reducing cash and simultaneously capitalising the bank segment's
earnings charges the same asset twice, which is [R-BRIDGE-01](iii) in a shape that rule's
worked case did not have. The bridge record must say which treatment it takes; note that
the banking cash MORE THAN DOUBLED year on year while non-banking cash fell 41%, so the
choice moves with the year and cannot be settled once.

## The valuation-input block [R-FCAL-01 AMENDED], as far as it goes

Six of the seven items that rule requires beside a driver panel, so a value struck here can
be rebuilt at this origin later. SAR thousands, FY2025 unless stated.

| item | figure | where |
|---|---:|---|
| cash and equivalents | 13,376,071 | note 20, and see the banking caveat above |
| interest-bearing debt — borrowings | 15,191,428 | note 42.6, carrying amount |
| interest-bearing debt — leases | 2,253,230 | note 42.6, carrying amount |
| property and equipment, net book value | 43,286,335 | note 10 |
| depreciation on property and equipment | 6,453,343 | note 10, and it includes impairment |
| share count | see below | note 22 |
| working capital — trade and other payables | 22,259,436 | note 42.6 / note 32 |
| **working capital — receivables, contract assets, inventories** | **NOT YET EXTRACTED** | recorded as missing rather than omitted, per that rule's clause (i) |

**The share count FOOTS BOTH WAYS**, which is the clause that rule states most firmly —
issued capital divided by par must reproduce the count the same document states:

- issued and fully paid capital **SAR 50,000,000** ÷ par **SAR 10** = **5,000,000** thousand
  shares, which is exactly the total note 22 states;
- outstanding **4,989,798** + treasury **10,202** = **5,000,000** thousand, which is the same
  total from the other direction.

The delivered study uses 4,989.8mn shares, which is the outstanding count and is right.

Capex is not yet taken. Where the cash-flow statement discloses it, it is read; otherwise it
is DERIVED by the identity capex = change in property and equipment plus depreciation and
LABELLED as derived, which is what that rule permits and what the label keeps apart from an
assumption.
