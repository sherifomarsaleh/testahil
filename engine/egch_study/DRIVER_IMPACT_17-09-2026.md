plant — a world nobody is proposing."

**NOTHING MOVES ON IT, and the reason is the whole discipline:**
- `kima.com.eg` **does not resolve** from this environment and the exchange refuses, so the

- **Perplexity searched the same window and did not find it.**

**One document decides this study's answer**: the ANNA progress disclosure behind the

## The FY2025/26 amendment

The board approved **amended** unaudited FY2025/26 figures on 9-10 September 2026 after the
**Central Auditing Organization** required corrections, reducing stated profit by about
**EGP 101m**. The CAO is this company's own auditor, and this study's terminal record sources
its useful life and average asset age to the CAO's report dated 23 September 2025.

The secondary accounts **contradict each other on revenue** — ~EGP 22bn against EGP 11.23bn —
so neither is used. **Treatment: flag FY2025/26 reported figures as subject to a post-result
amendment and ingest no press-derived number.** This study stands on 31 March 2026 and is not
disturbed.
