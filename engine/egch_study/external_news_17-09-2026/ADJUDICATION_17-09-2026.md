per-share binary on an unopened source would be the worst trade in this pack.

## 2. THE FY2025/26 ACCOUNTS WERE AMENDED DOWNWARD AFTER THE STATE AUDITOR INTERVENED

Perplexity reports that on **9–10 September 2026** the board approved **amended** unaudited
FY2025/26 figures after the **Central Auditing Organization** requested corrections, reducing
initially stated profit by about **EGP 101m**.

Two things make this matter more than its size:

1. **The CAO is this study's own auditor.** Its committed terminal record sources the useful
   life and average asset age to "Audited financial statements, year ended 30 June 2025
   (Central Auditing Organization, report dated 23 September 2025), note 6". The same body
   that signs the accounts this valuation stands on has required a restatement of the next
   year's.
2. **The secondary accounts contradict each other on revenue** — one report says ~EGP 22bn,
   another EGP 11.23bn. Perplexity says so itself and draws the right conclusion: *neither
   should be used*. The only consistent element is that a correction was filed.

**Treatment: flag FY2025/26 reported figures as subject to a post-result amendment; ingest
no press-derived number.** This study stands on 31 March 2026 and is not disturbed.

## 3. THE AGM AGENDA IS AN ASSET-BASE EVENT

**30 September 2026**, three relevant items: approval of the accounts for the year to 30 June
2026 and the proposed distribution; **sale of an 8.08-feddan plot at Fatira Quarry, Kom Ombo,
to Aswan Governorate**; and **scrapping or sale of the remaining production units of the
legacy "KIMA 1" plant**.

The KIMA 1 item is the one that touches the model, and Perplexity is right that its effects
must not be conflated: a disposal or scrap result and a write-down of residual carrying value;
a reduction in residual depreciation afterwards; cash proceeds, which are a different thing
from an accounting gain; and any decommissioning, remediation or employee costs. **Each
pushes a different way and none is quantified anywhere.**

It also bears on the operating asset base: separating legacy capacity from the modern
ammonia/urea base is exactly the vintage question the asset-base rule asks. **This study
carries no asset-base record**, and it is on that gate's ratchet for that reason.
