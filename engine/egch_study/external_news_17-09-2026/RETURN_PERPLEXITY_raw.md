## KIMA 1 and asset action

The AGM agenda is potentially more important than it first appears. Scrapping the remaining units of the legacy KIMA 1 complex could produce several distinct effects that should not be conflated:
- A one-time asset disposal/scrap result and potential write-down of residual carrying values.
- A possible reduction in residual depreciation after disposal, depending on the remaining net book value and asset lives.
- Cash proceeds, if any, from the asset sale — separate from accounting gain/loss.
- Decommissioning, site remediation, dismantling, or employee-related costs, if the resolution or later minutes disclose them.
- A clearer separation of the modern ammonia/urea asset base from legacy capacity, relevant to recurring production-volume assumptions.

Likewise, the 8.08-feddan Fatira Quarry transaction is a corporate-action lead, not a recurring operating driver. The exact consideration, whether it is cash or another form of transfer, and whether the land is operationally necessary remain unverified pending the issuer documents.
