# EGCH — QC gate, 3 September 2026

Egyptian Chemical Industries "KIMA" · Egyptian Exchange: EGCH · Aswan · nitrogen fertilizers
and industrial chemicals

Audited from outside the study by an evaluator who did not build it. Nothing here is
self-certified: every row names the artefact, the command or the number that carries it.
Where a defect was found it is recorded as a FAIL with evidence and handed back — it is not

## VERDICT: NOT DELIVERABLE AS IT STANDS

The three substantive corrections this edition set out to make — the flat dollar export price,
the CDS equity-risk-premium basis, and the retirement of the weighted blend — **all reached the
committed record and the workbook, and two of the three did not reach the Word document.** The
delivered study still names the rating basis as the one carried into the valuation, still
publishes the book lens at zero where the record carries the disclosed floor of EGP 8.16, and
still carries five prose claims that its own tables on the same page contradict. Separately the
study is struck against a price it labels with the wrong date, and its price map is the previous
edition's simulation relabelled.

Blocking, in order of how much a reader would be misled:

1. **§1.8 names the wrong central premium basis and prints the same number twice.** Delivered
   table: `Cost of capital, year one | 24.62% | 24.62% | "The rating basis is carried into the
   valuation as the more conservative"`. The committed record is `wacc_published = wacc_cds =
   24.617%` and `wacc_rating = 25.743%`. The workbook is right (`Assumptions` rows 98–100 read
   "CDS basis — THE CENTRAL" / "rating basis — published, not central"); the document is wrong,
   and the rating-basis WACC appears nowhere in it. Source: `docx_egch.py:647` feeds
   `DR['wacc_path'][0]` — the *adopted* glide — into the column headed "Rating basis".
2. **The book lens is published at EGP 0.00 where the record carries EGP 8.16.**
   `lenses.json['synthesis']['field']` reads "Book value, disclosed (a floor, never weighted)":
   **8.1578**. The summary table, §1.2, §1.5 and Figure 9 all publish **0.00** at −100% vs spot,
   which is the *justified* price-to-book read the study's own `lens_record` note calls "a
   derived valuation wearing the name of a disclosed figure".
3. **The anchor price carries the wrong date, and the price map was never re-struck.** Masthead:
   "Anchor price EGP 14.41 at the close of **2026-08-06**". §2, eight pages later: "The shares
   closed at EGP **13.98** on **2026-08-06**". `engine/prices/SUPPLIED_03-09-2026.json` dates
   14.41 to 3 September. `strike_result.json` carries `anchor_date 2026-08-06, spot 13.98`, so
   §3's percentiles are the 6-August simulation with a 3-September label: the one-month median
   (14.19) sits **below** its own stated anchor while the table reports 56.7% of paths finishing
   above it.
4. **Five claims contradicted by the study's own tables.** Detailed at "What reading the rendered
   pages caught" below — the sign of the answer, the count of positive free-cash-flow years, the
   size of the debt against enterprise value, a section heading, and the export-price mechanism.
5. **The delivered bibliography still registers the retired blend as live.** `lens_weights |
   0.45, 0.2, 0.2, 0.15 | "the weighted central this edition publishes inside its field … SET IN
   THIS EDITION (1 September 2026)"`.
6. **`diagnostics.json` and `contested_judgements.json` are a full edition stale**, and
   `check_valuation_gap` / `check_output_records` are green on them because they check shape,
   not currency.
7. **Both delivered documents are dated 1 September 2026 on their faces** — "1 September 2026"
   appears twice in the study and eight times in the bibliography; "3 September 2026" appears
   zero times in the study.

None of these moves the answer. All of them are the delivery, not the model: the workbook
recalculates to the last cell and rebuilds byte-for-byte from the committed numbers.
