# EGCH — QC gate, 13 September 2026

Egyptian Chemical Industries "KIMA" · Egyptian Exchange: EGCH · reporting and valuation
currency Egyptian pounds · edition audited: `EGCH_Valuation_Study_10-09-2026` (Word + PDF),
`EGCH_Valuation_Model_10092026` (Excel + PDF), `EGCH_Bibliography_10-09-2026` (Word + PDF).

Filled from **outside** the study by an auditor who did not build it and who changed nothing
in it. Every gate below was re-run rather than read off its last recorded output; every
repository gate was run from the repository root and every study gate from an isolated copy
of the study directory in a scratch tree, so nothing in `engine/egch_study/` was written.
Nothing here is self-certified: each row names the artefact, the command or the number that
carries it, and where an item is met with a gap the gap is stated in the same breath.

`QC_GATE_10-09-2026.md` recorded nine blocking defects. Every one was re-tested individually
against this edition; the closing tally is at the end of this file, and five findings below
are new.

## VERDICT

**NOT DELIVERABLE.** Sixteen defects reach a reader and block delivery; five more are defects
of record that would mislead the next study rather than this one's reader.

The model itself is in good order and should not be disturbed. It rebuilds byte-for-byte from
its own source — 1,914 workbook cells, 1,881 study units, 1,851 bibliography units, all zero
differences against the delivered files. The workbook recalculates completely, 645 of 645. The
beta is conforming on its record against the published index of the exchange the share is
listed on. The waterfall runs to present value and through the bridge. The four-field register
is complete on all 310 inputs. The contested judgement is genuinely computed both ways and
published at full size in four places.

What blocks delivery is concentrated in four places: §1.9's sensitivity apparatus, which still
does not contain the study's own answer and now ranks incommensurable spans against each other;
six typed claims the study's own numbers contradict; the expert panel, whose published low is a
literal and whose stated ordering is backwards; and the delivered workbook, which mixes the two
price clocks the Word document is careful to keep apart and prints a truncated first page.

The study is separately and correctly **HELD** under [R-GAP-02]. Nothing in this edition may
reach the live site.

## What blocks delivery

Sixteen defects reach a reader. Fourteen were found only by reading the rendered PDFs as
images or by reading the study's own committed records against its printed pages; no
programmatic check in this repository sees any of them.

1. **§1.9's sensitivity grid still does not contain the study's own answer, and the defect is
   two-dimensional.** `engine/egch_study/sensitivity.py:10-11` types `PRICES = [380, 420, 460,
   500, 540]` and `WACCS = [0.17, 0.185, 0.204, 0.22, 0.24]`. The model's terminal cost of
   capital is **16.877%** (`study_numbers.json` `drivers.wacc_terminal`), **below every row**.
   The model's export-price path is **flat US$530** (`drivers.export_usd_path =
   [530.0, 530.0, 530.0, 530.0, 530.0]`) while every grid column is built as a glide,
   `[p+90, p+60, p+30, p+10, p]` — so no column reproduces it either. The published central of
   EGP 8.07 appears in **no cell** of Figure 9 or Table 20; the closest cells are 7.14 and 9.64,
   both at a discount rate the model does not use. This is the headline finding of the
   10-09 gate, unchanged.

2. **Table 21 ranks two grid-read spans against eight measured at the base, and its caption
   contradicts its own first row.** `docx_egch.py:967-968` reads `_pcol` off grid column index
   **2** (terminal WACC 20.4%) and `_wrow` off grid row index **2** (US$460/t) — a rate and a
   price path the model does not use — then sorts them together with eight spans measured
   against `AL['baseline']` = 8.0748. The grid-read export-price row ranks **first at a swing of
   8.40**; the caption at `docx_egch.py:1000` then says "**The capital programme dominates
   everything else on this company**" while the table immediately above ranks the capital
   programme **second, at 3.53**.

3. **"The widest of them is the gas price … EGP 3.00 … The narrowest is terminal growth, worth
   EGP 2.81."** `docx_egch.py:194-198`, printed under Figure 1 on page 4. `alternatives.json`:
   the widest is the discount-rate glide at **−4.929**, the narrowest is the project-spending
   profile at **−0.205**. The study's own Table 19 caption, fifteen pages later and computed
   rather than typed, says "The two that move the answer most are **glide (-4.93)** and gas
   (-3.00)". Two statements of the same fact in one document, one of them wrong.

4. **"7.0% inflation … compounded with a 3.5% real rate gives a normalised risk-free rate of
   10.50%."** `docx_egch.py:907`, page 17. Compounding gives 1.07 × 1.035 − 1 = **10.745%**.
   10.50% is the additive sum, which is what the model actually uses — so the sentence names
   the wrong operation for the number printed beside it, in the paragraph that builds the
   terminal rate carrying 77.8% of the value.

5. **"…larger than either the multiple lens or the book-value lens moves for any other reason

   lens is printed on page 2 at **14.25 to 25.60** — it moves **11.35 a share**, three times

## What the filings disclose that the model does not consume

Asked explicitly, against the Sweep Register, and it does not come back empty.

**The quota-shortfall levy.** The company's own audited FY2024/25 statements disclose an export
levy of **EGP 437.5 million actually charged** on the subsidised-quota shortfall, and trade and
industry ministry decree 241 of 2021 sets that levy at **EGP 2,500 a tonne** on the shortfall.
Both are registered — `export_levy_charged_FY2425` and `export_levy_egp_t`. Tracing them
through every `.py` in the study directory: `export_levy_egp_t` is referenced **only by
`inputs.py`**, which registers it, and by nothing else; `export_levy_charged_FY2425` is
referenced once outside the register, at `docx_egch.py:1499`, which prints it in the **risk
register as prose**. Neither enters a formula.

This matters because it is a **different instrument** from the one this edition repealed.
Ministerial Decision 340 cancels Decisions 190, 203 and 258 — the 10% ad-valorem export duty.
It does not touch decree 241. The study carries the shortfall as a fact (§7: "It delivered
147,000 tonnes of a 322,000-tonne requirement over fourteen months. The forecast does not assume
that gap closes, but it also does not price a penalty for it, **because none is disclosed**")
while its own register holds the disclosed penalty rate and the disclosed amount charged. At
EGP 2,500 a tonne on a shortfall running near 150kt a year, that is roughly **EGP 375 million a
year pre-tax**, against a company whose whole equity value on the primary branch is EGP 16,041
million — order of **EGP 0.15 to 0.20 a share a year** before discounting, on a central of 8.07.

Two further disclosures are priced as alternatives and not adopted, which is a weaker version
of the same question and is at least visible to the reader: the auditor's **disclosed standard
gas rate of 1,200 m³/t** (the model runs a constructed 1,292, worth +EGP 0.93) and note 20's
**disclosed realised nitrate price of EGP 20,000/t** (the model runs a typed US$280/t on the new
complex's identical output, worth −EGP 0.57). Both are argued in Table 19; neither is in the
model.
