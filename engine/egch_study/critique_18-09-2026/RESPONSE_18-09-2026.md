## PART 2 — WHAT I INDEPENDENTLY TESTED

### TEST A — Does the workbook run the 32% the register says was replaced? (decides #5)

**It does. Confirmed in full, and it is the most serious row in the audit.**

The register states:

> *"157.29 EGP/t … **ADDED 9 August 2026 to replace a flat 32% cash-margin assumption** — the
> company discloses what a tonne of this exact product costs to make, and the study was
> assuming a margin instead of using it."*

Measured against the delivered files:
- `Assumptions!C143` = **0.32**, labelled "Project cash margin, % of revenue".
- `study_numbers.json` carries `drivers.anna_cash_margin = 0.32`.
- **157.29 appears in no cell of the workbook and nowhere in the committed numbers.**

So the register claims a correction the model never received, and names the exact defect it
claims to have fixed. This is worse than an unregistered input: **it is a register asserting a
repair that did not happen**, on the driver that powers the ANNA leg — which is the one
judgement this study says decides its two-sided answer.

### TEST B — Compounded or additive? (decides #6)

The study says *"7.0% inflation **compounded with** a 5.5% real rate gives a normalised
risk-free rate of 12.50%"*. 1.07 × 1.055 − 1 = **12.885%**; 7.0 + 5.5 = **12.50%**. The model
uses **12.50%** — additive. The critique prices the difference at **−EGP 0.143 (−3.5%)**.

**The model is right and the prose is wrong.** The house macro rule states the identity as
*"TERMINAL RISK-FREE = terminal inflation + the real-rate convention, DERIVED"*, and the EG
path's own source field says the terminal nominal rate is *"DERIVED as this **plus** the
inflation target in force"*. **Premise right, conclusion wrong**: adopting 12.885% would break
the house identity. The fix is the word "compounded", not the rate.

### TEST C — Workbook liveness, sheet by sheet (the hardcoding question)

639 formulas / 323 constants = **66.4% live**. 107 of the constants are the Assumptions input
plane; 107 more are filed history in the statement sheets (Test in SA-5); 36 are a partly-live
Sensitivity grid; the rest are scattered. `formula_audit` passes — *"every formula is built
from cell references and structural constants only"* — so nothing in the live path is typed.
