## The five rejections, with their receipts

**#1, the sensitivity grid — rejected.** The study's own generator, re-run against the delivered
model, reproduces all 25 cells to four decimals. The +0.95 is the price of reading the axis label
as a flat price, measured at 0.9499 on every cell. The adjacent defect is real and is logged as
mine (SA-3, SA-4).

**#8, the terminal-growth alternative — rejected.** The critique says moving *g* to 3% gives
EGP 4.811 and "the sign reverses", because the real-growth capital line flips to a +EGP 2,032m
perpetual cash release. Re-run on the delivered model: **EGP 2.4481**, against a published 2.45
and a committed `alternatives.json` value of 2.4481349. Then re-run with the one-sided clamp
removed — exactly the critique's construction — and `engine/terminal_value.py` **refuses
outright**: *"implied payout of terminal NOPAT is 128.3%, outside [0, 1]."* `compute.py`'s own
comment predicted that number before the audit existed: *"a permanent capital release of about
EGP 1.9bn a year, which drives the implied payout to 128% of profit and the module REFUSES it."*
What a careful reader had to miss is one line of code under four lines of comment — and **nothing
in the delivered document tells a reader the growth-capital term is one-sided.** That omission is
logged as my finding.

**C.5, the project-capex alternative — rejected.** Re-run at the profile `alternatives.py` sets:
**3.8329**, against a published 3.83. The critique's 3.954 is its own rebuild. The adjacent
defect: Table 21 prints only the *opening* year of that alternative's capex path, so an auditor
has to guess the other four.

**D-09 and E-01** are the duplicate of #1 and the audit's own count table, rejected on the same

## The one `user-decision` row

**U1 — the realised gas price, worth +EGP 2.3831 (+59.0%) on the carried-through branch and
+EGP 2.3831 (+29.6%) on the stopped branch.**

The company values its own lost gas **twice**, and both valuations are in this study's four-field
register:

| Disclosure | Volume | Value | Unit | Implied |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FY2024/25 full year, auditor's report (**audited**) | 38,480,270 m³ | EGP 249.0m | EGP 6.4708/m³ | **US$3.74/mmBtu** |
| Q1 FY2025/26, three months to 30 Sep 2025 (**limited review**) | 31,313,235 m³ | EGP 251.0m | EGP 8.016/m³ | **US$4.68/mmBtu** |

The study adopts US$4.68 and says nowhere that the other exists.

**Branches priced.** Adopting US$3.74: carry **6.4227**, stopped **10.4219**. Keeping US$4.68:
carry **4.0396**, stopped **8.0388**.

**Recommendation: keep US$4.68 and publish the choice.** The standing rule is explicit — *a
near-term reviewed actual outranks a stale full-year rate* — so the later disclosure is the right
input and substituting US$3.74 would be the wrong correction. The counter-argument is real and is
why this is your call rather than mine: US$3.74 comes from the **audited** annual and US$4.68
from a **limited review**. What is not defensible either way is the silence.
`contested_judgements.json` registers "the gas price" against **one** alternative framing — the
*higher* US$5.75 contract price, the framing that makes the adopted figure look conservative —
while the company's own lower disclosure, worth 59% of a branch, appears in no judgement record,
no alternatives table and no sentence. [R-ENF-05] requires both framings of any judgement worth
more than 5% of value.

**No stack of verified corrections brings either branch inside 10% of the price.** The verified
set leaves the carried-through branch at **−52.6%** and the stopped branch at **−31.9%**. Only a
selection that takes every correction in the value-raising direction *and* adopts both unverified
readings reaches the band, and then on one branch only, with the carried-through branch still at
−22.0%. Choosing corrections by the direction they move the answer is the offence this house
forbids outright, so that row is printed as a ceiling and nothing else.

**[R-GAP-02] holds this study before the response and will still hold it after every accepted row
is implemented.** The audit's own closing verdict is confirmed by measurement: *the direction of
the conclusion survives every correction I can quantify; the number does not.*

What does change is the **shape** of the answer. The critique's own two corrections carry the
**carried-through** branch to EGP 7.9451 — which is where the **stopped** branch already sits.
The study's headline finding is that stopping the programme is worth EGP 4.00 a share more than
finishing it; on those two corrections that gap narrows from EGP 4.00 to EGP 2.87, and the binary
the whole study is built around gets materially less binary.

**One further thing to act on, and it cuts against us.** The audit's Section E solves the reverse
read in units the study never publishes: the traded price is reproduced by a long-run export
price of **US$731/t**. Re-run on the delivered model, a flat US$731 gives **EGP 14.4037** against
a strike of 14.41 — it reproduces. Expert 3's own sensitivity row in the delivered study says the
export price "moved between US$380 and US$730 inside eighteen months". [R-ENF-05] requires a
study to publish what the price implies under its own drivers; this one publishes the
discount-rate read (13.0% against a 23.0% sovereign, genuinely implausible) and not the price
read, which is a level the market has actually printed. [R-GAP-02] says in terms that **a reverse
read landing on a believable number is evidence against the dissent and the study stays held.**
Publishing it weakens our own position, which is exactly why it has to be published.
