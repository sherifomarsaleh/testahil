## Two findings the reader should not have to hunt for

1. **The model's answer is far below the traded price, and the study says so plainly rather than tuning toward it.** The fair-value range is EGP 0.00 to EGP 2.19 per share against a close of EGP 13.98. The gap is reported through a reverse discounted-cash-flow calculation — the market price is reproduced only at a flat nominal discount rate of about 10.0%, roughly thirteen percentage points below Egypt's own ten-year sovereign yield — rather than by asserting the market is wrong.

2. **Two directional expectations in the driver test were wrong, and the model was right.** Terminal growth was asserted first upward and then downward; both failed, and tracing the mechanism showed value peaks near g = 7.35% and falls away on both sides, so the base case sits 35 basis points below a turning point and has no first-order sensitivity at all. The test now asserts the concavity that is actually there. The ANNA-utilisation failure in the same run was the opposite case — a genuine defect, where the valuation sheets read their own switch cell instead of the driver — and it was fixed in the workbook.
