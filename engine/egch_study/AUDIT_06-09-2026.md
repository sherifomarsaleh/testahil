# EGCH — a hypothesis about its −72% tested and REFUTED

**Dated 6 September 2026.** This file records a hypothesis that turned out to be wrong. It is
here because a refuted hypothesis honestly reported is a result, and because the next reader
of this study would otherwise form the same one and spend the same hour.

---

## The hypothesis

EGCH carries the largest but one gap in the book: a two-sided answer of **4.0396** (the ANNA
capital programme carried through) and **8.0388** (the programme stopped) against a latest
known price of **14.41** — −72.0% and −44.2%. Its own crux is that *the plant, once built,
does not earn a return above the cost of the capital sunk into it*, which is why stopping is
worth twice completing.

The study's drivers carry **two** nameplate capacities:

| | tonnes per year |
|---|---|
| `anna_nameplate_an_t` | 264,000 |
| `anna_nameplate_derived` | 189,863 — **28.1% lower** |

A value-destroying capital programme is exactly what an understated capacity denominator
manufactures. If the model divided the sunk cost by the derived figure, the return on it would
collapse by construction and the whole crux would be an artefact. That is the same shape as
[R-TERM-01]'s defect and [R-ANCHOR-01]'s: a denominator nobody looked at.

## It is refuted, on two counts, and the study is ahead of the question

**One: the model uses the DISCLOSED figure.** `compute.py:527` reads
`an_t = D['anna_nameplate_an_t'] * util`. The derived plate feeds the cross-check and nothing
else.

**Two: the study already fixed this, and says so in its own source.** `compute.py:208-211`:

> *"DISCLOSED, not derived. The EPC award for this plant states 800 t/day of granulated
> ammonium nitrate. The study previously said 'no filing states it' and derived the plate from
> the ammonia surplus; **an external critique produced the award**. The derived figure is
> retained only as the cross-check it now is."*

So the defect existed, was found from outside, was corrected, and the superseded figure was
kept **labelled** rather than deleted — which is what the standing rule asks for and why the
two numbers sit side by side at all.

## What the crux actually rests on, and both inputs are disclosed

| | |
|---|---|
| bank-approved total cost | EGP 20,341.7 mn |
| disclosed nameplate | 264,000 t/yr |
| **capital per annual tonne** | **EGP 77,052** |
| product price | USD 280/t |
| capital ÷ a year of revenue at 100% utilisation (EGP 50/USD) | **5.5x** |
| … at the study's base 50% utilisation | **11.0x** |

A project costing five and a half years of its own gross revenue at full run-rate — eleven at
the utilisation this study forecasts — is a poor project on any discount rate, and **neither
input is this desk's estimate**: the cost is bank-approved and the plate is an EPC award.

The terminal tonne is also **built rather than assumed**: the study replaced a flat 32% cash
margin with ammonia (gas-driven) plus conversion (inflation-driven) off the auditor's own
product-cost table, which is a second correction already made in the same direction as the
house discipline.

## What this leaves open

EGCH's disagreement with the market is **not** explained by the defect class found on
ADNOCDIST, ADNOCDRILL, AMR and ELEC — it has no live blend of the shape those carry, its
crux inputs are disclosed, and the one derived figure that could have manufactured the
conclusion is not the one the model uses.

The question the eight headings should ask here is therefore the **other** one: at 14.41 the
market is paying 1.8x even the halt branch, so either it disagrees about the programme's
economics on evidence this study has not read, or it is valuing something outside the
programme entirely. That is the interrogation still owed, and it is not answered by anything
in this file.

## THE OWED INTERROGATION IS NOW DONE — added later the same day

The section above said the question still owed was the other one: at 14.41 the market pays 1.8x
even the halt branch, so either it disagrees on evidence this study has not read, or it values
something outside the programme. `diagnostics_egch.py` now answers it **quantitatively**, and
the answer is sharper than the question.

**Solved on the driver the disagreement can actually live in** — the dollar export price of
urea, this company's largest revenue driver, quoted daily by a market and disclosed by its own
auditor as a realised average:

| | US$ per tonne |
|---|---|
| implied by EGP 14.41, programme carried through | **731**, held for ever |
| implied, programme stopped | **654** |
| this study holds | 530 (its path's opening level; upside case 560) |
| the auditor's realised FY2024/25 average | **385** |
| front-month FOB Egypt, 7 August 2026 | **545** |

**The market is paying for a urea price about a third above the highest level either the market
or the company has ever printed, held flat in perpetuity.**

**Two alternatives are ruled OUT rather than left open.** Solved on the discount rate instead,
the same price implies a flat nominal pound rate of 13.0% carried through and 14.3% stopped —
**both below the 23.0% the Egyptian government pays to borrow**, so outside the feasible set.
Equity in this company cannot be priced to yield less than the government that taxes it, and a
reverse read landing on an impossible number rules the rate out rather than measuring anything.
And it is not the new complex: at 100% of the disclosed nameplate — twice this study's
assumption — the carried-through branch is EGP 5.10, and reproducing 14.41 on that lever alone
needs **537% of nameplate**.

What is left is that the market disagrees about a driver on evidence this study has not read,
or is paying for something this study does not model at all. That is a far more useful
statement than "the gap is 72%", and it is checkable against a daily quote.

**The sign test:** 17 judgements, 16 material, 9 up / 7 down, two-sided p = 0.804. No lean.

**[R-GAP-02] holds this study correctly** — every branch is more than 10% below the price, the
highest at −44.2%, and no market dissent is filed. Both gates read the two-sided answer
properly, which the digest records as a hole that was closed; it is verified closed here.
