## What the rendered PDF caught that no programmatic check could

Reading the rendered document and inspecting each figure as an image is a gate,
not a formality, and on this build it caught four defects:

1. A blank page where a section break landed on a page boundary.
2. The cash-flow waterfall as ELEVEN COLUMNS, whose headers had wrapped into
   fragments — "Revenu e", "Workin g capital", "Prese nt value". Transposed to
   read down the page.
3. The discount-factor row rendered as 1, 1, 0, 0, 0, because the shared money
   formatter rounds to whole numbers.
4. An intangible-asset line in whole millions formatted to two decimals beside
   neighbours that were not, which reads as a different unit.

Two label collisions in the figures were found the same way and fixed in-pass:
a traded-price annotation running through the x-axis tick labels on an inverted
axis, and a "reported" marker sitting under the legend.

  gap on each.
* **Peer multiples are not published.** Price histories are held for five
  Egyptian names; their financial statements were not sourced, so no multiple is
  computed for them and the relative lens is built on TMGH's own history.
* **The risk-free rate is 26 days old** at this build date. It is cross-checked
  against three current central-bank rates and sensitised across 18%–40%, and
  it should be refreshed before the study is relied on.
