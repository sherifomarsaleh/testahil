## PART 3 — THE ONE INFERENCE THAT IS WRONG, AND IT IS OUR FAULT

**Finding 18.** The audit applies the textbook identity for a one-regressor regression to the
four diagnostics the study prints, and finds them incompatible. **Its arithmetic is exactly
right:**

| | |
|---|---|
| printed | β 1.4718 · SE 0.1848 · R² 32.61% · n 251 |
| R² implied by (t, n) on **one** regressor | **20.30%** |
| SE implied by (R², n) on **one** regressor | **0.1341** |
| n at which the printed pair is consistent | **133** |

Its conclusion — *"these cannot come from one regression"* — is **also right.** They do not come
from one regression. `beta_regression.own_stock_beta()` runs a **Dimson estimator**: lead,
contemporaneous and lag market returns, β the **sum of three coefficients**, SE the square root
of the summed 3×3 covariance block, R² that of the whole model. The identity does not apply.

**So the inference that the diagnostics are unsound is rejected — and the defect it found is
real and is ours.** The study describes the beta as *"TMGH's own weekly returns regressed
against the Egyptian Exchange's published index"* and never says the estimator is Dimson. A
competent reader applying the standard identity concludes the numbers were fabricated, and is
entitled to.

**This binds the whole book, not this name.** All ten committed beta records are Dimson; five
delivered documents disclose it; TMGH's does not.

## PART 7 — WHAT THIS NAME SAYS ABOUT THE BOOK

**1. Two declared gaps were not gaps, and both were in a document the register already lists.**
The study's crux and its second-largest judgement each rest on a sentence of the form *the
company does not disclose this*. The company discloses both, in its own filings, reachable on
the first request. This is SIGCM clause 8 inverted: the rule exists to make an absence visible,
and the failure here is that **an absence was asserted without the last search being run.** That
is [R-IND-01]'s lesson arriving inside a study rather than inside a session.

**2. The conservatism on this name is not conservatism — it is a mechanism.** Fourteen years
against a published four to five is not a cautious reading of an undisclosed number; it is three
times a disclosed one. And beneath it the model cannot reach fourteen years at any horizon,
because an undisclosed 25% partial adjustment holds realised conversion near 5.9% while the
book it is meant to draw down compounds to **5,265,316**. **The label and the mechanism point in
opposite directions, and the mechanism wins.**

**3. This is the fourth mechanism of structural distortion measured today, and it is the first
that runs UP.** The three found this morning — the flat-nominal price convention, the retired
terminal identity, and the promotion guard used to defer corrections — all understate value. On
TMGH the compounding sales annuity, the working-capital harvest and the typed 20%/22% segment
growth rates all **overstate** it. **A house that had only ever measured one direction would
have called this study conservative too.** That is [R-TERM-01 CLAUSE TWO]'s own lesson: a defect
measured only where it hurts looks like a bias with a direction.

**4. And one finding is about our instruments.** Every study's beta is a Dimson three-regressor
estimate; most delivered documents describe a simple regression; a competent reader applying the
textbook identity to the four published diagnostics concludes they are fabricated. **The
estimator is sound and the disclosure is not** — which is [R-COC-02]'s general lesson arriving
on a different quantity: a check that fires on correct work has usually found a construction
nobody wrote down.

**5. The company publishes its own discount rate and nobody has read it.** TMG's goodwill note,
in the same filing, discloses a **pre-tax discount rate of 33.9%, a 20-year projection period
and terminal growth of 5%.** Our terminal WACC is 21.93% and our terminal growth 7%. Neither
audit found it, and it is the natural cross-check on both.
