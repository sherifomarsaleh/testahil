divides, §1.6's walk-forward table carries its rows, `meta.central_note` names the right
minority basis, the study masthead carries its own date, the cost-of-capital prose is

**The workbook is the failure at this edition, and the delivered documents still carry
six figures the study's own committed numbers contradict.**

   `TMGH_Sources_10-09-2026.docx` — nine days stale, from `docx_bibliography.py:27`. This

   **35** (#22).
