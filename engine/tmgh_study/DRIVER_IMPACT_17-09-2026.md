## The land bank

This study carries **`landbank_msqm = 20.0`** from the 1H 2026 earnings release and **no
asset-base record**, so it sits on that gate's ratchet with the reason recorded: the figure is
current, and nothing states its vintage in a form a gate can read. The gate reports no new
breach.

**Both returns describe a GROUP land bank of roughly 115m sq m rising to about 128m sq m**
after an Iraqi allocation, with Banan in Riyadh and Jood and Yamal in Oman beside it.

**These are almost certainly not the same quantity.** 20.0m sq m cannot be this group's total
holding — Madinaty alone is of that order — so one is a remaining or undeveloped measure and
the other a group total including the international projects. **Nothing in the study says
which**, and that is the scope gap the missing record leaves open.

**Both returns agree there was no closed land acquisition after 30 June 2026.** The Iraq
licence is dated May 2026, *inside* the information set; the PIF memorandum is June 2026 and
non-binding on the secondary account. **So the asset base is not stale against later events —
the record simply cannot say.**

### The question this raises, which is larger than the vintage

**Does this valuation reach the Saudi, Omani and Iraqi land at all?** A developer's lens
carries an RNAV beside the cash flow, and **a land bank that is printed rather than consumed
cannot carry one.** This study's land bank is bound out of its KPIs and used as a format
argument inside a sentence — it is not wired into any arithmetic. A land bank that moves by
13m sq m changes nothing in a model that never reads it.

**That is a study-design question for the next edition, not an edit**, and it is the single
most valuable thing this name's pass produced.
