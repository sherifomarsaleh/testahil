## VERDICT: NOT DELIVERABLE

Nine gates pass from outside, the workbook is arithmetically clean under an independent
evaluator, the beta is a properly evidenced tier-2 fallback, and the three items this
edition set out to change are all genuinely done. What blocks delivery is not the model.
It is that **the re-strike from EGP 59.00 to EGP 77.00 was carried into the valuation and
not into the document**, and that a superseded model is still printed inside the current
one. Shortest blocking list:

1. **Two spots in one document.** §2 states "the price closed 59.00"; the summary table,
   the bridge and every "versus spot" figure use 77.00. §3's price map, Figures 5, 6 and 7
   are the 6-August cone anchored at 59.00, drawn under a "spot 77" rule. (Rows 12, 24)
2. **A false date on the headline price, in four delivered places.** "Market price, latest
   known close (**6 August 2026**) — 77.00". The 6 August close was 59.00. (Row 11)
3. **A superseded model published inside the current one.** §1.10 and Figure 4 end at a
   "weighted central" of **54.65** against a market of **59.00**, and Table 15 publishes a
   *falling* Testahil EBITDA margin (36.0% → 35.0%) against Appendix A's *rising* one
   (39.0% → 40.4%). [L-016], on the name that carries the lesson. (Row 20)
4. **A figure that plots EGP millions on an axis labelled EGP per tonne.** Figure 2's cost
   bars are `c_mat`/`c_tra`/`c_ovh` — total cost — against a per-tonne price, clipped off
   the top of the plot, under a caption saying the margin "narrows" while the figure's own
   labels read 39% → 40%. (Row 17)
5. **Two typed words that reverse the model.** "This company sits at 10.52% — −498 basis
   points **above** it. Growth therefore **adds** value" — against the study's own assert 21
   ("growth DESTROYS value by 498bp") and the figure four lines below titled "higher growth
   gives a LOWER value". (Row 21)
6. **A broken sentence with empty slots in §4**, and three claims in it that the re-strike
   made false. (Row 22)
7. **The delivered workbook's first sheet is edition history, is titled "revision 3", and
   is stale** (34.3% terminal margin, a retired price path), with every long line clipped
   at the page margin in the rendered PDF. (Rows 2, 18, 23)
8. **Structure short of the model report**: no Company overview, no About this series, no
   Disclosure section, and Appendix C stops at the three experts — no cross-examination, no
   three-in-one-room, no divergence table. (Rows 1, 8)
9. **The Sweep Register does not carry the H1-2026 interim or the investor presentation**,
   has no COMPANY_IR-tagged source, and is dated 6 August. (Row 27)
10. **No ARCC entry in the Fundamental Driver Ledger** against 23 House-ring drivers. (Row 31)

Everything above is a document or register defect. **No defect was found in the delivered
valuation arithmetic**: the workbook recalculates cell for cell under an independent
engine, the bridge foots, and the central of EGP 53.2091 is reproduced from source.
