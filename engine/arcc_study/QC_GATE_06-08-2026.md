## Q1 — "Have you taken the critique seriously enough?"

No, and the re-do at finding level proved it. **Eight findings were rejected with "0.00%"
beside them and the number was never computed.** Pricing them:

* **The spot price.** Both Gemini critiques used stale closes (57.80 is 5 Aug, 56.43 is
  2 Aug; 6 Aug is 59.00 in the supplied series), so the rejections stand. But pricing them
  showed the headline moves **+3.9% / +6.1% / +8.6%** across three consecutive closes.
  The conclusion sat inside one week of tape and neither side had said so.
* **The share count.** 378.74 would be −1.02%. Receipt: the FY2025 distribution divided by
  the reported DPS of 5.34 gives 374.867mn, not 378.740mn.
* **The controlling shareholder.** Rejected against the primary source actually held.
  Audited note 1, verbatim: *"The main shareholder of the Company is Aridos Jativa –
  Spanish Company, and it owns 60% of the Company's capital."* Note 1 also showed the
  statements are under **Egyptian Accounting Standards, not IFRS** — which no reviewer
  caught, and which undercuts the reasoning of one finding without disturbing its
  substance.

Accepted findings were then verified rather than taken on trust. One reviewer predicted
price would beat cost in **FY2027 and FY2030 specifically** — confirmed, both years.

**93 findings raised, 93 answered, 0 unaddressed.**
