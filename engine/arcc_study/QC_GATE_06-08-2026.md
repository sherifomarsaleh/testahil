## The two things that failed first, and what was done

**The driver test failed three assertions, and the model was right all three times.**

1. *Depreciation.* The test asserted that more depreciation lowers the value, and bumped a
   **mid-window** year. It rose. Decomposed before touching anything: a heavier charge is
   added back inside free cash flow, so in a mid-window year it is worth only its tax shield
   and the terminal block is untouched (FY2027 +1pp of revenue: enterprise value **+35mn**,
   present value of terminal value **unchanged**). In the **terminal base year** the sign
   flips, because capital expenditure here is set in dollars per tonne and does not follow
   the book charge (FY2030 +1pp: enterprise value **−502mn**, of which **−527mn** is the
   terminal block). The expectation was wrong, not the model. Both years are now asserted,
   in opposite directions, and the mechanism is documented in the test itself.

2. *Cash and debt.* The test asserted that more cash raises the value and more debt lowers
   it. Neither held. The effective tax rate is **inferred** from the FY2025 closure —
   disclosed operating profit plus modelled net finance income against **disclosed** profit
   after tax — so a balance-sheet change moves the imputed tax rate on every forecast year.
   Adding EGP 1bn of cash adds EGP 924mn to net cash and simultaneously lifts the effective
   rate 2.8 points, cutting enterprise value EGP 940mn; the two legs cancel to within four
   piastres a share. Adding EGP 2bn of debt cuts net cash EGP 1,823mn, cuts the effective
   rate 6.5 points, cuts the blended rate 62 basis points and lifts enterprise value EGP
   2,333mn — a net gain. This is internally consistent: profit after tax is a fact, so more
   finance income necessarily means the operating business was taxed harder. Each leg is now
   asserted separately, the coupling is disclosed on the DCF sheet and in the study's
   caveats, and the clean net-cash sensitivity — the tax rate held, the balance varied — is
   published as its own grid.

**The dead-input sweep reported six live inputs as dead.** They were not dead; the sweep's
readout set did not span the corners of the workbook, so inputs feeding cells no headline
reached looked inert. Five readouts were added and the sweep now reports zero. A sweep is
only as strong as the span of what it watches.

## Provenance — corrected after delivery

The first delivery described the sourcing as "carried as disclosed via reporting of the
exchange filings". Challenged on whether the actual statements had been read, the honest
answer is **no, and not only the statements**: no source page was retrieved at all. Every
outbound fetch — the company website (including the FY2025 investor presentation), the
exchange disclosure portal, and eleven financial-data and press hosts — was refused by the
egress policy at the connection stage, which the proxy documentation classes as an
organisation policy denial rather than a site-side block.

What was available was a web-search tool returning synthesised summaries quoting figures
from those pages, plus the supplied price series. Every company figure therefore reaches
the model **two steps** from the audited print, not one: a figure as relayed in a search
result about the reporting, not a figure read in the reporting.

The original wording implied the reporting had been read. It had not. The bibliography's
limitation section, its source-catalogue reference column, the study's leading caveat and
the workbook's READ FIRST provenance paragraph were all rewritten to say plainly that no
source document was opened, and all three deliverables were rebuilt and re-verified (825
of 825 formula cells, 91 driver assertions, 0 dead inputs — unchanged).

**What holds the headline figures up is corroboration, not authority.** The share count
triangulates three independent ways to within 0.2%; the disclosed +42.6% revenue step
decomposes cleanly into a +5.3% volume step on a +35.5% price step; published earnings per
share reconciles to attributable profit within the statutory employees' and directors'
share; and the bottom-up cost stack reproduces the disclosed operating income to +0.018%.
**What does not hold up on the same test is the balance sheet** — it does not close across
sources, by EGP 1,247mn or 14.2% of total assets — and that is disclosed rather than
averaged away.
