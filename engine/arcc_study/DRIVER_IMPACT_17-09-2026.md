**The forecast opens 0.12 points ABOVE the reviewed half, +0.32% relative**, against a rule

rate of 0.856% of export revenue rather than extrapolating H1-2026's collection — **fourteen
times the whole of FY2025's, in one quarter.** No change.

## WHAT IS GENUINELY OPEN, AND IT IS NAMED RATHER THAN CLOSED

**This study holds no H1-2026 volume.** It calibrates FY2026 by scaling the half's *revenue*
to a full year on FY2025's own half-year split per channel, and takes its volume-versus-price
split from FY2025 relationships. The **Q2 2026 investor presentation**, uploaded to the
company's own site in September — after the 3 September re-strike — measures it:

| 1H2026 | | vs 1H2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Total volumes | 2,137.4 K tons | **−6%** |
| Revenue per ton | EGP 2,811 | **+17%** |
| Cash cost per ton | EGP 1,565 | +11% |
| Clinker exports | 269.3 K tons | **−51%** |
| Cement utilisation | 68% | from 74% |

**Sales volume (Mt) and realised price (EGP/t) are two of this study's ten committed drivers.**
A revenue calibration that is right in total can still carry the wrong split between the two
drivers it is built from, and nothing in the statements can say which.

**This is not corrected in passing.** Re-deriving the volume/price split from the presentation
is a driver rebuild with a rebuild ledger, not an edit, and it belongs in the next edition —
where it must be done before any argument about the −13.6% gap, because it moves both drivers
the gap depends on.

## Untraced, and the most consequential of them

The **Ministry of Industry roundtable of 16 September 2026** — national capacity 75.5 Mt, a
directive to reactivate idle kilns and raise exports, relaxing the Egyptian Competition
Authority quota regime in force since July 2021 — bears directly on the volume driver, and
this company's own cement utilisation is 68% and falling. **EGX and the ministry's release are
both unreachable from here.** Registered, not modelled.

Retail cement quotes of EGP 3,830–3,860/t are **not** realised price: this company realised
EGP 2,811/t in 1H2026 on its own figures, and conflating the two would overstate revenue by a
third.
