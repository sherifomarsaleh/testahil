**The correction runs AGAINST the value.** Moving the terminal risk-free rate up

suspecting; this one lowers ARCC while raising PHDC and TMGH, because it is
correcting constructions rather than steering an answer.

## The escalator question, and the filing that settled it

Expressed against the house path, the retired indices assumed an 11.0% cumulative
real decline in the cement price and a 5.2% real decline in cost by 2030 —
neither stated, both artefacts of nominal ladders typed against an inflation view
that sat 450 basis points below the house path in 2026. The company's own
reviewed first half of 2026 prints a gross margin of 40.5% against 40.6% for the
whole of FY2025: its realised price and its realised cost move together. Price
and cost now ride ONE path at zero real growth from FY2027, with the evidenced
FY2026 anchors kept, so the margin is an output rather than the residual of two
separately chosen indices.

Costs were NOT moved to the house path alone. That would have put 16% cost
inflation against an 8% price step and manufactured a 4.3% cumulative squeeze the
company's own accounts contradict.

## The margin guard, re-pointed rather than relaxed

The study asserts that its forecast window does not manufacture margin expansion.
Under the retired indices the cost path ran above the price path in every year,
so the assertion tested nothing an input could fail. With both legs on one path
the escalators are neutral by construction, so the guard now rebuilds the margin
path with the alternative-fuel saving switched off and forbids THAT path to
expand. The +1.4 point expansion the model shows (39.0% in FY2026 to 40.4% in FY2030) is the funded programme — EGP 240.2mn of
capacity in assets under construction with a EUR 25mn facility drawn against it —
and nothing else.
