# Forensic Audit — Testahil Independent Valuation Study, Arabian Cement Company S.A.E. (ARCC)

Documents audited: `01_valuation_study.docx` (issued 3 September 2026), `02_valuation_model.xlsx`, `03_bibliography.docx` (212 registered inputs).

---

## Section A — Audit Scope

1. The workbook's formula graph was re-implemented cell-for-cell in Python and reproduces the published central of **EGP 66.53004** against the workbook's own `Sensitivity!D8` of 66.5300405232932 — ten significant figures. Every finding below is therefore arithmetic against the delivered model, not inference.
2. Fully reconciled: the cost of capital (both anchors), the five-year FCFF waterfall, all five discount factors, the terminal block, the enterprise-to-equity bridge, all four lenses, the nine panel figures, the three audited income statements and the audited balance sheet.
3. Reachable primary sources: the study's own source register, which cites the audited FY2025 statements (Deloitte, signed 25 February 2026), the reviewed H1-2026 interim statements (Deloitte, filed 13 August 2026), the FY2025 Investor Presentation page-by-page, the Damodaran January-2026 country file and CBE policy material.
4. **Not reachable in this audit, therefore not independently confirmed:** the ARCC filings and investor presentation themselves; EGX price history; the Egyptian statutory export-cap instrument; the Damodaran file; the EFG Hermes note; the peer financials for Sinai Cement and Misr Beni Suef. Every input-plane claim resting on those is logged UNVERIFIABLE, not FAIL.
5. What this audit can therefore prove is **internal**: document against workbook, document against register, and document against its own arithmetic. That is where almost all of the findings sit.

**The three most valuation-critical findings.**

1. **The forecast is driven by four multipliers that appear in no source register** (#3, #2, #4). The two largest point in opposite directions, so the net understates how much of the answer is unsourced: the study's stated drivers do not produce the study's number.
2. **The beta choice contains the entire disagreement with the market, and the study misattributes its own alternative** (#8, #13). At the withdrawn basket beta of 0.628 — one of the two betas §1.5 discusses — the lens reads **EGP 78.54, a premium to the EGP 77.00 price.** The published "+12.0%" belongs to the 0.698 regression, is computed with the double-levering §7 claims to have fixed, and is really +13.2%.
3. **The terminal's stated construction is not its actual construction** (#6, #5, #19, #18). "5.0Mt at USD 130 a tonne" is silently escalated 56.6%, worth 14.5% of value; the growth axis slopes down for a reason the report does not give; the ROIC the model computes is the one the report disowns; and the terminal FCFF and terminal value — 53.5% of enterprise value — are never printed, so none of it can be checked from the page.

**Direction of the contested judgements.** Thirteen judgements worth more than ~5% of value: **nine resolve downward** (replacement-cost denominator and its escalation, peer-median beta, peers not unlevered, USD-per-tonne capex, export price path, services haircut, pound-equivalent cost of debt, terminal cost of debt, export subsidy as one-off) and **four upward** (local price calibration, terminal debt weight of 20%, median-seasonality gross-up, terminal risk-free on the 7% rather than 5% target). That is a lean, not a uniform sweep, and the three the study itself publishes in Table 13 are all value-lowering — which is honest. What it omits from that accounting is the single largest value-*raising* undisclosed item, the local price calibration. **This is a flag, not a failure:** a single-plant cyclical producer at its best filed margin may genuinely deserve a consistent read, and the study says so plainly.

**Does the headline range survive the corrections? It shifts materially.** The arithmetic survives intact — EGP 66.53 is exactly what the delivered model computes, and the bridge, the waterfall, the cost of capital and the implied asset life all reconcile. What does not survive is the claim that the study's *stated* construction produces it. On the study's own documented alternatives the cash-flow lens spans roughly **EGP 52 to EGP 79**: EGP 52.43 at the 8.0% local price step the report argues for, EGP 76.20 at the terminal capital base the report describes, EGP 78.54 at the beta the report names as its own withdrawn figure, EGP 79.54 with export prices held flat. **The sign of the disagreement with the EGP 77.00 market price reverses at three of those four points.** The published −13.6% is therefore not robust to the study's own stated inputs, and the reverse read the protocol demands — absent from §1.11 — lands on a perfectly believable number. The correct remedy is not a different valuation; it is to publish the drivers the model actually charges, at which point the range and the conclusion can be argued on the merits.
