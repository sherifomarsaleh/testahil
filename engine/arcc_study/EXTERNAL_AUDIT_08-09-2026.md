SECTION C — WHAT WAS REFUTED AND WHY THAT MATTERS

Five candidate findings were killed by the adversarial pass — one each in the market, anchor and lens_bridge lenses and two in the terminal lens. The verifier was instructed to refute and to default to refuted when uncertain, so the 16 above are what survived a hostile reading, not what a sympathetic one produced. A finding that dies under refutation is evidence the audit is calibrated rather than evidence it was lazy: an audit that never kills its own candidates is an audit that is not testing them.

Several dimensions also verified clean and are stated as such. The central is one lens, not a blend (the retired 50/20/22/8 weighting feeds nothing). No book value is weighted into the central. No relative multiple is derived from the current price — 4.5x, 7.0x, USD 95/t and USD 130/t are typed house constants, un-anchored but not circular. There is no clamp, floor or cap anywhere in the workbook. Cash is charged exactly once (WACC weight is gross debt over market cap; FCFF excludes treasury income). Minority interest sits on the correct side of the bridge. Country risk is counted once and on the same basis both ways in the explicit window. The forecast is anchored on the latest reviewed period, not on FY2025 or a blend, and year one does not open materially below it (38.90% reviewed vs 39.03%E). EBITDA is a genuine output of the cost stack, footing in every column. The beta regressor is the published index of the exchange ARCC is listed on, and the earlier composite basket is withdrawn and said to be withdrawn. Share count, market cap, the sovereign spread, the ERP arithmetic, the risk-free level, Ke, WACC, the four-facility cost of debt, the CBE rates and the peer financials all reproduce or match primary sources.

SECTION E — VERDICT

The three most valuation-critical findings are: (1) the three level shifts on the reviewed half, of which two are disclosed nowhere and which together carry EGP 66.53 down to EGP 48.90 if removed, with the disclosed one alone worth 21.2%; (2) the terminal risk-free rate, sourced in the bibliography to a target that does not build it and built instead on the target §7 says was a corrected defect, worth roughly +12% in the other direction; (3) the terminal replacement-cost capital base, whose printed total is 56.6% larger than its two printed operands, worth +14.5% if a reader follows the page.

The headline **shifts materially**: the arithmetic of EGP 66.53 is reproducible to the last digit by six independent rebuilds, but the corrections run from −26.5% to +14.5% and the two largest sit on inputs the delivered documents describe incorrectly rather than on anything a reader could have caught, so the central is not defensible as printed until the level shifts are disclosed and the terminal risk-free is re-sourced.
