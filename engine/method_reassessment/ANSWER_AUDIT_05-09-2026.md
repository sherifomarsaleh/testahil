## ADNOCDIST — the audit disagrees with its own study, and the defect it names is ours

Published centre **4.4113 / 4.5821** against AED 4.02 on 3-Sep-2026 (+9.7% / +14.0%). The
class-primary cash-flow read is 4.784 / 5.101 (+19.0% / +26.9%) — the blend [R-LENS-03]
retired, which this study's own held findings already record.

**VERIFIED BY HAND, 5 September, since the verification agents could not run.** The
arithmetic below reproduces from the study's committed numbers alone, and the study's own
input register states the intent that makes it a defect: the 17% "**is** the realised
first-half outcome", and `compute.py:865` then applies it *on top of* that realised half.
Second half of 2026 implied at **0.5847 AED/L** against **0.4363** in the half just filed —
a 34.0% jump nothing discloses, compounding through all five explicit years. Written up in
full at `engine/adnocdist_study/AUDIT_05-09-2026.md` finding 7. **This one is no longer
unverified.** The rest of the ADNOCDIST stack below, and all of ADNOCDRILL, still is.

**The one defect the audit calls ours, and it is large.** The FY2026 commercial margin per
litre steps +17% on an anchor that is *already* the realised H1-2026 outcome:

    FY2025 0.3674 x 1.17 = 0.430  ~=  the 0.4363 anchor

so the second half of 2026 must earn **0.585 AED/L** against 0.370 in the latest quarter and
0.367 for the full prior year. One peak quarter escalated for ever, in a model where the
terminal carries 75% of value.

Removing the step: cash-flow read 4.78 -> 4.36-4.41; the blend -> 4.06-4.10. Anchored on the
latest quarter instead, 4.00.

**Everything else the audit priced either offsets or runs against the answer** — blend
retired +8.4%, zero-real terminal +7.2%, one-basis country risk +6.8% against a 4.5-year
tenor -4.0%, seasonality +2.9%, minority and Egypt -0.6 to -1.7%, working capital -0.5%. The
evidenced both-ways stack leaves the cash-flow read at 4.75 (+18%); the our-defect-only stack
at 4.37 (+8.7%).

**The reverse read is what settles it, and it settles it AGAINST a dissent.** At 4.02 the
corrected model implies terminal growth of +0.58% nominal, -1.4% real, or a discount rate of
8.03%, or a beta of 0.76 — every one of them believable for a fuel retailer facing electric
displacement. **A reverse read landing on a believable number is evidence against dissent**,
so no [R-GAP-02] dissent would be supportable here even if one were wanted.

Sign test: 7 up, 6 down, 1 both ways. **No lean.**

The audit's verdict is that the study is not deliverable at 4.41 / 4.58 and should be
re-struck on the cash-flow lens with the step removed. That verdict is UNVERIFIED and the
step's arithmetic is the first thing a verifier should attack.

or a terminal — and in both cases the class primary sits closer to the market than the
published blend does. That is [R-LENS-03]'s own case made twice more, and it is consistent
with PHDC's precedent, where the cash-flow lens landed within 2.2% of the market and the
blend landed 28% below.

It is **two names**. The lens census commissioned to test this book-wide did not finish. Two
