## Part B — Principles

1. **One date, one price of time.** A cash flow arriving on a date is discounted at that date's forward rate whether it sits in the explicit window, a residual annuity or the terminal. The term structure is the rule; a flat rate is the exception that must be argued. (Already written at protocol line 314. Now executable.)
2. **Fisher consistency everywhere.** Every nominal quantity in a model — growth, price, cost, terminal growth, capitalisation rate — is a real component plus *one* house inflation path per market, dated and sourced. A rate that embeds 15% inflation may not sit beside a growth rate that embeds 5%. A gate asserts it.
3. **The DCF is the central. Everything else is a cross-check that shapes the range.** Lenses are published side by side and never averaged into the answer. A blend of a present value with a run-off value and a historical-cost floor at typed weights is not a valuation, and the house's own promotion rule already forbids it.
4. **The valuation layer is calibrated on its own clock, like the cone and the statements.** A fair value struck at a past origin is graded against the price that followed, out of sample, and the method's bias is a number with a confidence interval. Price grades the method; it never enters a study. ([R-LENS-01] permits exactly this and forbids exactly that.)
5. **Where a standard can be read off the delivered file, it is read off the delivered file.** Every rule in Part C is a script that opens the study's committed record and fails. Attestations survive only for judgements a script cannot make.
6. **Symmetric guards.** The gap gate fires above as well as below. The calibration measures over-optimism with the same instrument. No parameter is chosen because it moves a name toward its price; parameters enter only through leave-one-name-out, era-split, out-of-sample tests — the same bar the cone clears.
7. **A correction that is computed is applied or blocks.** A review that quantifies a defect and ships without it has not finished.

### Three classes of change, and what each needs before it ships
Every change in Part C is labelled, in the plan and in each study's committed record, as one of:
- **Class A — compliance and specification fixes.** The house's own written rule is applied where it was not (the glide on PHDC/TMGH; the minority rule; the Kd gate; corrections reconciling to their log; drivers inside the study's own record). No out-of-sample evidence is needed to apply a rule already adopted; each class-A change ships with a **pre-registered revert condition** written into the valuation calibration's pre-registration (for example: *if the pooled contemporaneous bias under the glide is further from zero than under flat, LONO-stable and in both eras, the glide reverts book-wide and protocol line 314 is amended*), so "apply now" is accountable and never per-name.
- **Class B — level and design choices.** Terminal ERP norm, terminal inflation choice, terminal real-rate convention, long-run Kd norm, glide length, the country-premium scaling λ, beta shrinkage prior, the CDS-vs-rating basis *as a level*, the terminal capital basis, any lens set that is not the class primary. Each is pre-registered as a **finite candidate set derived from a named external principle** (CBE target, Damodaran's mature-market ERP, Fisher, Koller's value driver) with a default, and adopted only where the valuation calibration shows the stacked pooled bias moving toward zero (WS6). Every class-B level in a delivered study carries either its calibration score id or the literal word **untested**.
- **Class C — outside enforcement.** Every class-A rule and every promoted class-B level ships with a script that opens the delivered record and fails.

### WS1 — The cost-of-capital standard, in code  `[R-COC-01]`
**Problem.** Causes 1, 6, 7. The r2 glide, the Kd-integrity gate and the CDS-primary basis exist as prose; two of five studies breach the first, all breach the second, the third is split.
**Change.** `engine/cost_of_capital.py`: one function that takes a market, a beta record, the debt book and the house macro path (WS2) and returns the full schedule — explicit-window Ke/Kd/WACC, a glide tied to the policy-rate path, a norm-built terminal (terminal rf = the central bank's target + the stated real-rate convention; terminal Kd = the long-run corporate norm; terminal ERP normalised), and the cumulative discount factor by year. Hard asserts inside it: `WACC_TERM < WACC_EXP` where the market is in transition; the Kd three-assert gate (currency composition sourced; independently computed effective rate over two periods; Kd within 150bp of it and ≤ 50bp above the peak-year rate); one ERP basis as CENTRAL (CDS, per line 327) with the rating basis published as a sensitivity; the country premium scaled by a stated λ, default 1.0, with the 1.52 scaling published beside it; beta shrinkage (Vasicek toward the market-class prior, Blume as cross-check) and Dimson terms kept only where the lag/lead coefficients are individually significant at 10%; tier-2 betas unlevered and re-levered. Every input carries a source and a date. A sovereign quote older than 14 days at build (a convention, stated as one, not a derivation) does not fail silently: it triggers a dated STOP-AND-ASK to the principal recorded in `fetch_attempts.json`, and the build proceeds with the age printed on the cost-of-capital table — a gate with no release path is the stall [R-CAL-01] forbids. **The terminal ERP norm is constructed, not asserted:** Damodaran's mature-market ERP plus the country premium at the sovereign's medium-term target rating, with the long-run average of the Egypt row across the vintages WS6 sources as the pre-registered alternative — both stated before either is applied to PHDC or TMGH. Tier-2 betas carry a record shape (`peers`, `unlevered`, `relevered_at`) that `assert_beta_provenance` inspects — ARCC ships a raw peer median with the relever step skipped, a class-A breach worth about 3/sh.
**Deliverables.** `engine/cost_of_capital.py`; `scripts/check_cost_of_capital.py` (opens every `engine/*_study/` committed cost-of-capital record, asserts the schedule shape and every rule above; ratcheted, population-anchored, negative-controlled); `wacc_builder.py` reduced to a thin caller; the five studies migrated.
**Test.** The schedule reproduces AMOC's and ARCC's existing glides to 1e-6 (they already comply) and moves PHDC/TMGH by the glide alone. Promotion of any *parameter* (λ, basis, shrinkage) is by WS6's out-of-sample record, not by this workstream.
**Effort.** 6–8 agent-runs, 3 days. **Depends on** WS2 (macro path) for the terminal anchors; can start on a stub.

### WS3 — Lens architecture v2: DCF central, cross-checks as range, per class  `[R-LENS-03]`
**Problem.** Cause 2.
**Change.** `research_protocol.LENS_REGISTRY` keyed on `lessons_register.CLASSES` by import — the ten registered classes, including the two developer sub-classes — never a second taxonomy for the same companies. By class: developer → DCF + RNAV (the digest's own LENS BY CLASS rule, never asserted — PHDC shipped with no RNAV). **RNAV is specified before it can be a central:** a *present-value* RNAV on one clock — land at cost with a labelled market cross-check (never market as the base), absorption on the company's own delivery rate, discounted at the WS1 schedule, no gross NAV; where land value per square metre is an undisclosed gap the class primary stays the DCF and RNAV is a cross-check (SIGCM clause 8); refiner/processor → DCF + EV/EBITDA on own history + replacement cost; fertiliser/cement → DCF + EV per tonne; bank → DDM + RI; holdco → SOTP. Relative multiples are non-circular (forward earnings × a multiple from peers or own *history*, never the current price). `assert_lens_design(study_record)` fails on a typed weight, a nominal-stasis lens, a circular multiple, a class with a missing primary.

### WS6 — The valuation calibration  `[R-VCAL-01]` — the walk-forward that answers the question
*(Directory `engine/valuation_calibration/`, deliberately not `*_walkforward`: the lessons gate anchors its population on that suffix and would fail a directory with no harvested lesson.)*
**Problem.** Cause 9 in its deepest form: no fair value has ever been graded against what happened. The principal's test — *does calibration bring the fair value closer to the actual price* — has no instrument.
**Change.** At each year-end origin from 2013 to 2023, rebuild the house fair value with point-in-time information only and grade it against the price 1, 2 and 3 years later and against realised total return.
- **Population.** FULL on the names with parsed statements (the five, then each name the campaign adds); LIGHT on the 26 further EGX names with ≥10 years of prices, using a fixed point-in-time proxy model (trailing statement lines from the filings the walk-forward already parses, or where absent a declared reduced form) — declared as a proxy, never dressed as the full method.
- **Frozen at origin.** Drivers from the statement walk-forward at that origin; cost of capital from the point-in-time sovereign curve, CPI print, policy rate and ERP vintage — which requires **`engine/macro_history/EG.json`**, a dated archive assembled from CBE, treasury auction and Damodaran vintages (the long pole; see risks); the lens set and bridge from WS3/WS4.
- **Score — two pre-registered series, because a fair value struck today is not a forecast of the price in three years.** At a 27–37% cost of equity a fair value that agrees with the price at t *should* sit 15–30 log points below the price at t+1 by construction, so a raw log(FV_t / P_{t+h}) with a zero-bias acceptance is mis-specified. Pre-registered instead: (i) **contemporaneous agreement**, log(FV_t / P_t) at every origin — the direct measure of the house lean; (ii) **gap closure**, whether log(FV_t / P_t) predicts the subsequent one-, two- and three-year total return net of carry — the measure of whether the lean is information. Bias, MAE, sign, block-bootstrap CI over origins (blocks 2–4, seed 42), split by era (pre/post the 2016 and 2022–24 devaluations), leave-one-name-out across the panel; the effective independent n (about the origin count) printed beside the cell count. **Two fair-value series**, because mechanical drivers at past origins cannot reproduce a 2025 study built on judgement drivers: (a) the mechanical-method fair value at every origin including 2025, and (b) the as-delivered fair value at 2025 only, reconciled to (a) with the difference attributed to named judgement drivers. Origins use the filing's EGX disclosure date, not a CMS timestamp. **Decomposition** by re-running each origin with one lever at its realised value: cost-of-capital path, level, terminal, lens, bridge, drivers — so the plan's Part A shares become measured, not inferred. **Benchmarks:** FV = P_t (no skill), FV = trailing P/E × EPS, FV = book. A method that cannot beat "the price is right" has not earned a fair value.
- **Promotion — sequential, in a pre-registered order, with a stop rule.** Levers are evaluated one at a time on the current stack in the order written into the pre-registration (glide → terminal anchors → basis → λ → shrinkage → lens set), and a lever is promoted only while the stacked pooled contemporaneous bias moves toward zero **without crossing it by more than the bootstrap half-width**; promotion stops the moment it would. This replaces any per-lever "improves on three of four names" rule, which can stack five individually-justified moves into an overshoot. The symmetric guard: a positive bias is a finding exactly as a negative one is.
- **The pre-registration is hashed and committed before any score exists**, and `check_valuation_calibration.py` asserts that the pre-registration commit precedes every score file — the only mechanical proof that no lever was fitted to the gap.
- **What it is not.** Not an input to any study. Not a way to fit fair values to prices — the promotion bar is out-of-sample and cross-name, the CRPS-selection precedent applies in full.

**Effort.** 12–16 agent-runs, 8–10 days (the archive is 3–4 of them). **Depends on** WS1–WS4 for the method it grades; the data assembly starts day one.

### WS7 — Output gates: the process audits its own answer  `[R-GAP-01 amended, R-ENF-05]`
**Problem.** Cause 9 and 10; the principal must never again prompt "check the cost of capital, the revenue, the cost".
**Change.** (a) The gap gate becomes **two-sided**: a central more than 10% *above* price gets the same eight-heading review — the instruction was one-sided and the plan recommends symmetry because the diagnosis found a one-sided defence is how the lean survived. **Mandatory inside this programme, not only at publish:** the programme's own numbers put TMGH's corrected capacity case above 97.80 and possibly above the 147.12 on the site, so every re-issue that lands more than 10% either side of price gets its review before its files are staged. (b) Every study prints a **"what you must believe" table** — the reverse-DCF: the discount rate, growth, margin and conversion the price implies under the study's drivers — as a standing diagnostic beside the answer. It lives in a separate `diagnostics.json`, **never in the numbers file every builder reads** (a price-derived rate inside the cost-of-capital builder is what protocol r2 item 4 prohibits), and `assert_reverse_dcf_present` also asserts that no builder imports it. (b′) **The selection lean gets an instrument:** every study commits `contested_judgements.json` — every choice worth more than 5% of value, both framings' values, the side adopted and why — with a sign test printed on the answer card and a CI flag when a study resolves all such choices one way at p < 0.05. (c) The four construction gates (WS1–WS4) and the two actuation asserts (WS5) run in CI over every study, negative-controlled; each ratchet list is written by its own gate's first run against a population counted elsewhere, in the same commit as the gate — never seeded by hand. (d) **A computed correction is applied or blocks:** a `GAP_REVIEW` that quantifies a correction either lands it in the same commit or registers it in a `gap_outstanding` entry with a date — a review that leaves a computed correction unapplied fails the gate; every review carries the study SHA and numbers-file edition it audited, so a re-issue cannot inherit a stale clean bill (the L-064/L-065 species). The answer card also prints the site's published value beside the committed one, so the direction of every future site move is visible before any publish. (e) The bear/full range is asserted to be a present-value envelope that contains the central and is stated with its basis (scenario spread is not a probability range and is labelled as such).
**Deliverables.** `check_valuation_gap.py` amended + negative control; `research_protocol.assert_reverse_dcf`; `scripts/check_corrections_applied.py`; the CI workflow.

## Part E — Acceptance criteria

The plan is complete when all of the following hold and are printed by the gates, not attested:

1. `check_cost_of_capital`, `check_macro_coherence`, `check_lens_design`, `check_bridge`, `check_walkforward_actuation`, `check_corrections_applied`, `check_valuation_calibration` are green in CI with negative controls, and the ratchet lists carry only names not yet re-issued.
2. On the five re-issued names, forward drivers sit inside each name's own walk-forward p10–p90 or carry a priced exception; every claimed correction reconciles to its log.
3. **The valuation calibration's pooled contemporaneous bias, log(FV/P) at every origin, has a 90% block-bootstrap CI that includes zero**, is LONO-stable in sign, and holds in both eras; the gap-closure series shows whether any residual lean predicts returns; the method beats "FV = price" and "trailing P/E × EPS" on MAE; the decomposition attributes any residual bias to a named lever. **This record, and the gates, are the acceptance instrument.**
4. **Not an acceptance criterion but a graded prediction**, written into `PREDICTIONS_02-09-2026.md` before any re-issue and graded after: that the median |central / price − 1| across the five falls inside 15%, with at most one name outside ±35% and that one supported by its own statement record (the EGCH condition). A prediction can fail without the programme failing; an acceptance criterion on price would be a target a standing goal would learn to hit.
5. The two-sided gap gate fires on nothing among the five, or every firing carries a complete review whose computed corrections are applied.
6. The publish queue holds the four files per name on the new standard (study docx and PDF, bibliography, 16-sheet workbook), each opened and read.

**Phase 2 acceptance, per market stop:** every name in the market carries its four files, its walk-forward record at the stated scope, lessons registered, gates green; the two-sided gap gate fires on nothing or every firing carries a complete review with its corrections applied; the market's names sit inside the pooled walk-forward CI with no new sign-stable bias; the batch is staged and published on the principal's word. The EGX stop additionally answers, in writing, whether the corrected method generalises before UAE begins.

**Explicit non-criterion.** Matching the price is not the goal and is not measured as success. A name may finish 40% below price if — and only if — its statement record supports the drivers, its cost of capital is the house schedule, its lenses are present values, and the out-of-sample record shows no method bias. That is what "a realistic fair value" means here.

## Part F — Risks, and what guards them

- **Over-correction into optimism.** The live example is PHDC: the glide alone lifts its DCF to +83% above price because its profit path is 3× too high. Guarded by fixing both layers in one pass (WS5 before WS8 on any name whose walk-forward shows an over-forecast), the explicit-horizon rule, the two-sided gate, the symmetric calibration score, and the promotion rule: nothing enters because it raises values.
- **Curve-fitting to price** (the CRPS-selection mistake in a new costume). No study-level tuning; parameters move only on pooled, leave-one-name-out, era-split evidence; the reverse-DCF is a diagnostic with an assert that it feeds nothing.
- **The macro-history archive.** Point-in-time sovereign, CPI, policy-rate and ERP vintages for 2013–2023 must be assembled and sourced; where a vintage is missing the origin is dropped and the window shortened — never interpolated (the walk-forward's own rule).
- **A name that legitimately stays far below price** (EGCH). The plan does not force it home; it forces its drivers to face its own record and its rate to be the house schedule, then publishes the disagreement with its reverse-DCF.
- **Scope creep into the other 85 names.** WS9 produces a queue, not a rebuild; the campaign wrapper's order and hard stop stand. The 27 EGX names with a published fair value and no study directory enter the WS9 report as "no study — provenance unknown" and are cleared only through the queue.
- **Reading the lean as Egyptian only.** The site carries names 15–38% below price on ADX, DFM, KRX and NASDAQ; the construction gates run over every market from day one and the sweep report says where else the same defects sit.
- **The reference set.** ADNOCLS remains the model report for structure and depth; its cost-of-capital, lens and bridge sections are regenerated from the new standard rather than displaced by a fourth exemplar.
