**02 September 2026.** Prepared on the principal's instruction of the same day: *"A reassessment of the fundamental analysis method and of the fundamental calibration is needed. The goal is an accurate fundamental analysis, an accurate calibration to project the financial statements, and from those a realistic fair value, because we are ridiculously pessimistic."*

Everything in Part A was read or computed from the repository by a panel of independent agents — eight evidence readers, three diagnosticians from different schools, and three adversarial skeptics per root cause — and every cause below survived refutation. Where a claim was narrowed under challenge, the narrowed form is what appears here. Nothing is asserted from memory.

**What already-written rules alone would do.** Applying only rules the house has already adopted — the glide, the minority rule, the gap reviews' own computed corrections, Fisher-consistent lenses — puts PHDC's blended central at 16.8 (+10%) and TMGH's capacity case at 89–96 (−8% to −1%) **before any change to the level of the cost of capital**, and AMOC and ARCC are already within 3–8% on their DCF alone. The pessimism is method incoherence, not a view about the companies. That is the finding, and it is also the reason the fix is tractable.

**The warning inside that finding — read this before concluding "cut the rate".** Verified this session on PHDC's own code: under the house glide (26.25% → a norm-built 16.26%, terminal growth 7%) its DCF lens goes from 14.86 to **27.83, which is 83% above the price**, and the terminal becomes 74% of enterprise value. PHDC's statement projection compounds revenue at 44% nominal for five years, and its own walk-forward found net profit **over**-forecast by 3× in 97% of tested cells — an optimistic statement layer that a punitive rate and two floor lenses were silently cancelling. Correcting the profit path alone at the flat rate takes the DCF from 14.86 to 10.44. **PHDC carries offsetting errors, and TMGH's are all one-way.** Two consequences run through every workstream below: the two layers are fixed *together* or the answer swings from one absurdity to the other; and the rate glide requires an explicit window long enough for growth to converge to the terminal rate, or the terminal swallows the valuation.

## Part B — Principles

1. **One date, one price of time.** A cash flow arriving on a date is discounted at that date's forward rate whether it sits in the explicit window, a residual annuity or the terminal. The term structure is the rule; a flat rate is the exception that must be argued. (Already written at protocol line 314. Now executable.)
2. **Fisher consistency everywhere.** Every nominal quantity in a model — growth, price, cost, terminal growth, capitalisation rate — is a real component plus *one* house inflation path per market, dated and sourced. A rate that embeds 15% inflation may not sit beside a growth rate that embeds 5%. A gate asserts it.
3. **The DCF is the central. Everything else is a cross-check that shapes the range.** Lenses are published side by side and never averaged into the answer. A blend of a present value with a run-off value and a historical-cost floor at typed weights is not a valuation, and the house's own promotion rule already forbids it.
4. **The valuation layer is calibrated on its own clock, like the cone and the statements.** A fair value struck at a past origin is graded against the price that followed, out of sample, and the method's bias is a number with a confidence interval. Price grades the method; it never enters a study. ([R-LENS-01] permits exactly this and forbids exactly that.)
5. **Where a standard can be read off the delivered file, it is read off the delivered file.** Every rule in Part C is a script that opens the study's committed record and fails. Attestations survive only for judgements a script cannot make.
6. **Symmetric guards.** The gap gate fires above as well as below. The calibration measures over-optimism with the same instrument. No parameter is chosen because it moves a name toward its price; parameters enter only through leave-one-name-out, era-split, out-of-sample tests — the same bar the cone clears.
7. **A correction that is computed is applied or blocks.** A review that quantifies a defect and ships without it has not finished.

## Part C — Workstreams

Each names the problem, the change, the files, the test that promotes it, the rule it creates or amends, and its dependencies. Effort is in agent-runs (one focused agent session) and calendar days assuming the parallelism stated in Part D.

### WS1 — The cost-of-capital standard, in code  `[R-COC-01]`
**Problem.** Causes 1, 6, 7. The r2 glide, the Kd-integrity gate and the CDS-primary basis exist as prose; two of five studies breach the first, all breach the second, the third is split.
**Change.** `engine/cost_of_capital.py`: one function that takes a market, a beta record, the debt book and the house macro path (WS2) and returns the full schedule — explicit-window Ke/Kd/WACC, a glide tied to the policy-rate path, a norm-built terminal (terminal rf = the central bank's target + the stated real-rate convention; terminal Kd = the long-run corporate norm; terminal ERP normalised), and the cumulative discount factor by year. Hard asserts inside it: `WACC_TERM < WACC_EXP` where the market is in transition; the Kd three-assert gate (currency composition sourced; independently computed effective rate over two periods; Kd within 150bp of it and ≤ 50bp above the peak-year rate); one ERP basis as CENTRAL (CDS, per line 327) with the rating basis published as a sensitivity; the country premium scaled by a stated λ, default 1.0, with the 1.52 scaling published beside it; beta shrinkage (Vasicek toward the market-class prior, Blume as cross-check) and Dimson terms kept only where the lag/lead coefficients are individually significant at 10%; tier-2 betas unlevered and re-levered. Every input carries a source and a date; a quote older than 14 days at build fails.
**Deliverables.** `engine/cost_of_capital.py`; `scripts/check_cost_of_capital.py` (opens every `engine/*_study/` committed cost-of-capital record, asserts the schedule shape and every rule above; ratcheted, population-anchored, negative-controlled); `wacc_builder.py` reduced to a thin caller; the five studies migrated.
**Test.** The schedule reproduces AMOC's and ARCC's existing glides to 1e-6 (they already comply) and moves PHDC/TMGH by the glide alone. Promotion of any *parameter* (λ, basis, shrinkage) is by WS6's out-of-sample record, not by this workstream.
**Effort.** 6–8 agent-runs, 3 days. **Depends on** WS2 (macro path) for the terminal anchors; can start on a stub.

### WS2 — One house macro path per market  `[R-MACRO-01]`
**Problem.** Cause 3. Five studies, five inflation paths; terminal growth incoherent with terminal inflation; replacement-cost reinvestment charged on inflation-only growth; `Cost_of_Capital_Reference.md` absent.
**Change.** `engine/macro_path.py` + `engine/macro_paths/{EG,AE,SA,QA,IN,KR,US}.json`: per market, a dated and sourced inflation ladder (central-bank target path to terminal), FX path, policy-rate glide, sovereign curve point, terminal inflation, long-run corporate Kd norm, real-rate convention. Every study imports it; no study may carry its own inflation number. `research_protocol.assert_macro_coherence(model_record)`: every growth rate in a model's committed record is stored as (real, inflation-path id) and recomputes to its nominal; terminal g ≡ terminal inflation + a stated real g (default zero); terminal reinvestment = g_real / ROIC on the *same* capital basis as NOPAT, with inflation-only growth requiring no reinvestment beyond the maintenance capex already in FCFF (the Koller value-driver identity on one basis — closes the ARCC defect); the NEP lens, where kept, at E/(Ke − π) or in real terms. **Explicit horizon rule:** the explicit window runs until the modelled growth path is within 2pp of terminal growth (`assert_horizon_converged`); a five-year window on a name compounding at 44% nominal, capitalised at a normalised terminal rate, makes the terminal 75–87% of value and is the second half of the PHDC swing. The file the protocol has referenced since July gets written: `engine/Cost_of_Capital_Reference.md`, generated from the JSON, never typed.
**Deliverables.** the module, the seven JSON files with sources, `scripts/check_macro_coherence.py` (+ negative control), the reference document, L-048/L-055 promoted from lessons to a gate.
**Test.** Every model on the book re-expresses its growth rates against the path with zero unexplained residual; ARCC's terminal reinvestment recomputes on one basis.
**Effort.** 5–6 agent-runs, 3 days. **Depends on** nothing.

### WS3 — Lens architecture v2: DCF central, cross-checks as range, per class  `[R-LENS-03]`
**Problem.** Cause 2.
**Change.** `research_protocol.LENS_REGISTRY` by class: developer → DCF + RNAV (the digest's own LENS BY CLASS rule, never asserted — PHDC shipped with no RNAV); refiner/processor → DCF + EV/EBITDA on own history + replacement cost; fertiliser/cement → DCF + EV per tonne; bank → DDM + RI; holdco → SOTP. The DCF (or the class primary) is `fair.base`. The cross-checks are published in the same table and define `fair.bear`/`fair.full` as the envelope of *present-value* lenses (one clock), never averaged. Normalised earnings is Fisher-consistent or dropped. Book value is a disclosed floor, not a lens. Relative multiples are non-circular (forward earnings × a multiple from peers or own *history*, never the current price). `assert_lens_design(study_record)` fails on a typed weight, a nominal-stasis lens, a circular multiple, a class with a missing primary.
**Deliverables.** the registry, the assert, `scripts/check_lens_design.py`, the five studies' lens tables rebuilt.
**Test.** On the five: the central becomes the DCF (PHDC 14.86, AMOC 9.77, ARCC 57.60 before WS1/WS2 effects); WS6 later tests whether *any* blend beats DCF-only out of sample — if one does, it is promoted; the typed blend is retired now because it never cleared the bar it was required to.
**Effort.** 4–5 agent-runs, 2 days. **Depends on** nothing.

### WS5 — Statement calibration v2: from measurement to actuator  `[R-FCAL-01 amended]`
**Problem.** Causes 5, 8, 11. The walk-forward measures and never acts; studies build below their own record; the log does not reconcile to the claim.
**Why this workstream is not optional.** PHDC's profit over-forecast (+1.12 log, 97% of cells) is what turns the corrected rate into a +83% DCF; TMGH's sales under-forecast (−0.88 log) is what turns the uncorrected rate into a −60% one. The valuation layer cannot be fixed on top of an unactuated statement layer.
**Change.** (a) A pre-registered *decision rule* replaces "watch flag by default": a bias is corrected when it is era-stable, the corrected forecast beats FREEZE out of sample under the block bootstrap, and the correction strength comes from shrinkage toward zero by its own standard error — not a typed 0.5. (b) `assert_forward_inside_record(study, walkforward)`: every forward driver in a study sits inside its own walk-forward's p10–p90, or the study names the mechanism and prices the exception. (c) `assert_corrections_reconcile`: the study's claimed adoptions equal `corrections_log.json`. (d) The revenue rule gets what the record says it lacks — the method under-forecasts revenue on four of five names, so the volume/sales driver class acquires a growth term estimated from the record and tested against FREEZE and TREND. (e) The over-forecast on PHDC's profit is treated the same way in the other direction — the instrument is symmetric.
**Deliverables.** `engine/walkforward/decision_rule.py`; the two asserts; `scripts/check_walkforward_actuation.py`; the five training records re-scored under the rule (no new data needed); `Fundamental_Walkforward_Prompt.md` amended.
**Test.** On the five records, the rule's adopted corrections beat FREEZE out of sample; TMGH's log reconciles; every study's forward drivers pass (b) or carry a priced exception.
**Effort.** 6–8 agent-runs, 4 days. **Depends on** nothing for (a)–(c); (d) benefits from WS6.

### WS6 — The valuation-layer walk-forward  `[R-VCAL-01]` — the calibration that answers the question
**Problem.** Cause 9 in its deepest form: no fair value has ever been graded against what happened. The principal's test — *does calibration bring the fair value closer to the actual price* — has no instrument.
**Change.** At each year-end origin from 2013 to 2023, rebuild the house fair value with point-in-time information only and grade it against the price 1, 2 and 3 years later and against realised total return.
- **Population.** FULL on the names with parsed statements (the five, then each name the campaign adds); LIGHT on the 26 further EGX names with ≥10 years of prices, using a fixed point-in-time proxy model (trailing statement lines from the filings the walk-forward already parses, or where absent a declared reduced form) — declared as a proxy, never dressed as the full method.
- **Frozen at origin.** Drivers from the statement walk-forward at that origin; cost of capital from the point-in-time sovereign curve, CPI print, policy rate and ERP vintage — which requires **`engine/macro_history/EG.json`**, a dated archive assembled from CBE, treasury auction and Damodaran vintages (the long pole; see risks); the lens set and bridge from WS3/WS4.
- **Score.** log(FV_t / P_{t+h}); bias, MAE, sign, block-bootstrap CI over origins (blocks 2–4, seed 42), split by era (pre/post the 2016 and 2022–24 devaluations), leave-one-name-out across the panel. **Decomposition** by re-running each origin with one lever at its realised value: cost-of-capital path, level, terminal, lens, bridge, drivers — so the plan's Part A shares become measured, not inferred. **Benchmarks:** FV = P_t (no skill), FV = trailing P/E × EPS, FV = book. A method that cannot beat "the price is right" has not earned a fair value.
- **Promotion.** Any parameter (λ, ERP basis, glide shape, shrinkage, lens set) changes only if it improves the pooled out-of-sample score under LONO and both eras. The symmetric guard: a positive bias is a finding exactly as a negative one is.
- **What it is not.** Not an input to any study. Not a way to fit fair values to prices — the promotion bar is out-of-sample and cross-name, the CRPS-selection precedent applies in full.
**Deliverables.** `engine/valuation_walkforward/` (pre-registration first, then `panel.py`, `score.py`, `decompose.py`, `report.py`); `engine/macro_history/EG.json` with sources; the training record; `scripts/check_valuation_calibration.py` (population anchored on the price libraries per [R-ENF-04]; negative-controlled); the rule in both governing documents.
**Test.** The pre-registered score on the five, then the panel; the record is the acceptance criterion in Part E.
**Effort.** 12–16 agent-runs, 8–10 days (the archive is 3–4 of them). **Depends on** WS1–WS4 for the method it grades; the data assembly starts day one.

### WS7 — Output gates: the process audits its own answer  `[R-GAP-01 amended, R-ENF-05]`
**Problem.** Cause 9 and 10; the principal must never again prompt "check the cost of capital, the revenue, the cost".
**Change.** (a) The gap gate becomes **two-sided**: a central more than 10% *above* price gets the same eight-heading review — the instruction was one-sided and the plan recommends symmetry because the diagnosis found a one-sided defence is how the lean survived. (e) The bear/full range is asserted to be a present-value envelope that contains the central and is stated with its basis (scenario spread is not a probability range and is labelled as such).
**Deliverables.** `check_valuation_gap.py` amended + negative control; `research_protocol.assert_reverse_dcf`; `scripts/check_corrections_applied.py`; the CI workflow.
**Effort.** 4–5 agent-runs, 2 days. **Depends on** WS1–WS5 for what it checks.

3. **Median |central / price − 1| across the five ≤ 15%**, and no name sits outside ±35% without a statement-layer reason that its own walk-forward record supports (the EGCH condition).
4. **The valuation walk-forward's pooled bias at the two-year horizon has a 90% block-bootstrap CI that includes zero**, is LONO-stable in sign, and holds in both eras; the method beats "FV = price" and "trailing P/E × EPS" on MAE; the decomposition attributes any residual bias to a named lever.
5. The two-sided gap gate fires on nothing among the five, or every firing carries a complete review whose computed corrections are applied.
6. The publish queue holds two files per name on the new standard, each opened and read.

**Explicit non-criterion.** Matching the price is not the goal and is not measured as success. A name may finish 40% below price if — and only if — its statement record supports the drivers, its cost of capital is the house schedule, its lenses are present values, and the out-of-sample record shows no method bias. That is what "a realistic fair value" means here.

## Part F — Risks, and what guards them

- **Over-correction into optimism.** The live example is PHDC: the glide alone lifts its DCF to +83% above price because its profit path is 3× too high. Guarded by fixing both layers in one pass (WS5 before WS8 on any name whose walk-forward shows an over-forecast), the explicit-horizon rule, the two-sided gate, the symmetric calibration score, and the promotion rule: nothing enters because it raises values.
- **Curve-fitting to price** (the CRPS-selection mistake in a new costume). No study-level tuning; parameters move only on pooled, leave-one-name-out, era-split evidence; the reverse-DCF is a diagnostic with an assert that it feeds nothing.
- **The macro-history archive.** Point-in-time sovereign, CPI, policy-rate and ERP vintages for 2013–2023 must be assembled and sourced; where a vintage is missing the origin is dropped and the window shortened — never interpolated (the walk-forward's own rule).
- **A name that legitimately stays far below price** (EGCH). The plan does not force it home; it forces its drivers to face its own record and its rate to be the house schedule, then publishes the disagreement with its reverse-DCF.
- **Scope creep into the other 85 names.** WS9 produces a queue, not a rebuild; the campaign wrapper's order and hard stop stand.
- **The reference set.** ADNOCLS remains the model report for structure and depth; its cost-of-capital, lens and bridge sections are regenerated from the new standard rather than displaced by a fourth exemplar.
