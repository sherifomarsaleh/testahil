## What did not pass, in one place

1. **Item (d), single-name calibration.** Ten windows over 2.25 years, skill −0.0054 — negative,
   against a requirement of greater than zero, and well short of five years. The cause is the
   December 2022 listing, not the method. The pooled 19-name set that actually sets the band
   does clear the bar on 271 windows over 4.25 years with excellent uniformity. §3 of the study
   says exactly this to the reader, including the negative number.
2. **Item (e), presentation of history depth.** Three audited years are presented as statement
   columns, which is what the item asks for; a fourth (FY2022) was obtained and is used for
   context rather than presented as a column. Recorded as partial rather than claimed as a
   clean pass.
3. **The Abu Dhabi index.** Not obtainable for the beta regression. Worked around with the
   company's own shares against its other home market's index, cross-checked twice, and
   recorded in three places.
4. **One estimate inside the revenue build.** The company guides total net new restaurants but
   not the country split; the allocation follows observed growth and is flagged as an estimate
   wherever it appears.
