[R-COC-01 AMENDED] THE 150bp COST-OF-DEBT BOUND IS RE-POINTED, NEVER SWITCHED OFF, AND A STUDY MAY DECLARE ITS DISCOUNTING CONVENTION [AMENDED 03-Sep-2026, per instruction]. THE BOUND EXISTS TO STOP AN INVENTED COST OF DEBT by holding the adopted rate against one computed independently from the filings, and on most books the trailing effective rate is exactly that. ON SOME BOOKS IT IS NOT, AND THE REASON IS MECHANICAL RATHER THAN A MATTER OF OPINION: interest CAPITALISED into assets under construction never reaches the expensed charge, so the numerator is not the interest actually incurred; and a book that RE-BASED WITHIN THE PERIOD is described by an average balance that existed for none of it. Dragging the adopted rate toward such a number is the smoothing this method forbids everywhere else. WHAT A RECORD MUST DO TO RE-POINT THE BOUND IS HARDER THAN THE TEST IT REPLACES: name a mechanism from a CLOSED list (capitalised interest, a book re-based in period, a facility drawn mid-period — an open list would let any study opt out by inventing a reason, and 'the rate looked wrong' is not a mechanism); carry the DISCLOSURE that establishes it, from the filings rather than asserted; and supply a CONTRACTUAL ANCHOR — every facility with its balance, its own rate and the note that rate comes from, foreign legs at LOCAL-EQUIVALENT cost — from which THE ADOPTED RATE MUST REPRODUCE. A weighted average either comes out or it does not, which is why this is verifiable from outside the study; a record that declares the exception and supplies no anchor has switched the check off rather than re-pointed it, and FAILS. NOTHING ELSE MOVES: a book with no declared mechanism still faces the 150bp bound and the 50bp peak test exactly as before, SILENCE IS NOT AN EXCEPTION, and adding a mechanism to the list is a rule amendment. SEPARATELY, END-OF-YEAR ARRIVAL WAS AN ASSUMPTION AND NOT THE RULE: this rule requires ONE DATE, ONE PRICE OF TIME — which a mid-period or stub schedule obeys, and which the terminal test is what actually enforces — and nowhere mandates year-end arrival. A record may DECLARE its convention (the cumulative discounting time of every explicit year AND the slice of calendar each forward rate owns, without which the factors do not reproduce) and the factors are then checked against the declaration; a record that declares nothing still gets the end-of-year test, because accepting any factors where none is declared would DELETE the check rather than generalise it, and a declaration that does not reproduce its own factors FAILS — it reads as evidence, which is worse than an assumption. [R-ENF-05] THE STUDY AUDITS ITS OWN ANSWER: THE REVERSE READ AND THE SIGN TEST [ADOPTED 02-Sep-2026, method reassessment WS7].
