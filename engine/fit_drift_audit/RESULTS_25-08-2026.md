# Fit drift, re-measured against main @ 37fcdb2 — 25-Aug-2026

Re-derived from scratch. The 24-Aug measurement (76 of 92 on a superseded fit,
18 material, all AE at about -6.4%) predates [R-SHAPE-01]'s reshape_mid_band and
the rest of the per-stock program reaching main, and is VOID. Nothing here is a
research change: no lens, driver, cost-of-capital construction or calibration
procedure moves, and no delivered number is edited.

## Method, and why not the ledger note

`refresh_pages.fit_drift()` reads the struck (nu, width_cal) out of the newest
LEDGER note. [R-WIDTH-01] forbids inferring overlay state that way, so this
measurement does not parse a note at all. It re-strikes every covered name
through the production chain (`strike_cohorts.strike`, seed 42, 50,000 paths) at
`anchor_idx=-1` and compares the resulting 90% cone half-width, IN LOG SPACE,
against the PUBLISHED `dist`.

Three properties make that comparison isolate the fit and nothing else:

* every page currently stands on its library, so `anchor_idx=-1` IS the published
  anchor — verified, 0 of 90 anchors mismatched;
* HAR looks only backwards from the anchor, so sigma_h is identical on both sides;
* log half-width is drift-invariant, so q_annual and the carry anchor cannot
  contaminate it.

The per-name overlay multiplier is therefore included by construction: `strike()`
calls `adaptive_width.live_width_mult()` itself. Materiality is the engine's own
metric, imported rather than reimplemented — `auto_refresh.BAND_TOL` = 5% on the
relative move of the published 90% cone.

## Two mechanisms, not one

**AE (18 names) — the market fit.** AE's overlay is off, so every AE multiplier is
exactly 1.0 and the whole move is the pooled fit. The published cones sort into
cohorts by the fit they were struck under, and the arithmetic predicts the
measurement: 17 names struck at nu=10.0/cal=0.979 against a live nu=4.5/cal=0.965
predict -6.39% and measure -6.83%..-5.98%; 7 struck at nu=10.0/cal=0.923 predict
-0.71%; 2 at nu=10.0/cal=0.916 predict +0.05%. The [R-SHAPE-01] reshape held the
90% edge constant by construction, so it is NOT the cause — the cause is that
these cones predate the 23-Aug AE refit.

**EG (3 names) — the per-name width overlay, and the note cannot see it.** EG's
market fit moved nu=6.0/cal=0.951 -> nu=5.0/cal=0.958, worth only -0.90%. EMFD,
ORHD and OCDI are material because their own overlay multipliers have moved to
0.9174, 0.9319 and 0.9549 as their libraries lengthened past the gate. HELI runs
1.1070 — the overlay WIDENS a name whose own volatility runs hot, exactly as the
standing rule says and against the reading that calls it a narrowing device.

## Two defects in `fit_drift()`, both demonstrated

Measured against the re-strike, the note-based method flags 77 names and would
call 18 material. It is wrong in both directions:

* **It MISSES 4** — EMFD, OCDI and ORHD, whose drift is entirely the overlay the
  note never records, and DU, whose note carries no fit at all. This is precisely
  the failure [R-WIDTH-01] describes: the pooled width_cal in the note is not the
  width the cone was built on.
* **It FALSE-ALARMS on 1** — ADCB. Its published cone is anchored 2026-08-21 while
  its newest ledger row is 2026-07-24, because `refresh_cone_one` refreshes a
  displayed cone mid-cycle and deliberately writes NO ledger row. The newest note
  therefore describes a strike the page no longer shows. 4 of 90 names are in this
  state today (ADCB, KAKAO, SAMSUNG, TMPV).

A note is a record of the last LEDGER strike. It was never a record of the
published cone, and the two separate by design every time a mid-cycle refresh runs.

## Negative result: the cheap analytic route does not stand in

Recovering the published effective 90% edge as `log(p95/p5)/2 / sqrt(dvar*h)` and
comparing it to `cal * mult * q95(t(nu))` agrees to a median 0.48% but reaches
7.40% on TMPV and disagrees on materiality for it — the terminal distribution of a
HAR path simulation is not exactly a scaled t. It is also NOT cheaper: 967s against
~25 min, because `load_clean` dominates and the MC does not. Recorded so the next
session does not re-derive it.

## Proposal — NOT implemented here

Material drift survives, so the [R-CAL-01] 5% gate has something to gate. But
wiring it to the re-strike path as it stands would put a ~25-minute measurement
inside every page refresh, and re-striking a cone whose prices have not moved
republishes a different forecast on the same data, which this repo already calls a
decision rather than a refresh.

The cheap and correct fix is architectural: record the EFFECTIVE fit
(nu, width_cal * overlay_mult) on the TICKERS entry at the moment a cone is struck.
`strike_cohorts.strike()` already computes `width_overlay_mult` and returns it, so
nothing new has to be measured. Drift detection then becomes a dictionary
comparison against the live profile — no note parsing, no re-simulation, no blind
spot — and the 5% gate can be applied to it directly, re-striking only what is
material and reporting the rest. That change touches what a strike RECORDS, not
what it COMPUTES, so no published cone moves on the day it lands.

Held for an instruction: whether a material fit drift should re-strike
automatically is a product decision, not a mechanical one.
