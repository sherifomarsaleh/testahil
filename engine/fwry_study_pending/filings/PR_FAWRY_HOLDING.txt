  Fawry Establishes “Fawry Holding for Financial Investments”
  30 November 2025 – (Cairo, Egypt) Fawry (the "Company", FWRY.CA on the Egyptian Exchange), Egypt's
  leading provider of e-payments and digital finance solutions, has announced the establishment of “Fawry
  Holding for Financial Investments”.

  The company announced that the FRA’s (The Federal Railroad Administration) Incorporation and Licensing
  Committee Has approved the establishment of “Fawry Holding for Financial Investments” on Thursday, 27
  November 2025.

  In a statement issued today, Fawry for Banking Technology and Electronic Payments explained that the new
  holding company comes as part of the group’s broader strategy to support its growing investment activities,
  growing pipeline of opportunities, and the ongoing restructuring of existing subsidiaries. The new structure
  aims to build a unified and robust economic entity with an institutional framework capable of managing
  diversified operations, directing investments toward clear strategic objectives, and overseeing both existing
  and future subsidiaries within a single legal structure.

  Fawry Holding for Financial Investments has been established with an authorized capital of EGP 50 million
  and a paid-in capital of EGP 5 million. Fawry for Banking Technology and Electronic Payments owns 99.99%
  of the new company.
