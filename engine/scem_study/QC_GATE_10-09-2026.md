## What is genuinely strong here, and should not be lost in a rebuild

Six things got materially better at this edition and none of them should be disturbed.
**The beta record now conforms** — `assert_beta_provenance()` has the five fields it needs,
the regressor is `raw_indices/EG/EGX30.csv` read to 2026-09-08, the tier-3 fall is reasoned on
ARCC's own failing regression rather than on a false negative search, and the withdrawn
composite is kept with its delta rather than deleted. **The FY2024 balance sheet comes out of
the audited filing** at 5,695.6 / 1,959.8, replacing four press outlets. **The equity roll
closes to the pound** — 3,735.80 + 2,284.54 = 6,020.34 exactly — which retires the EGP 1,039mn
hole the 07-09 gate listed as an unread disclosure, and the study says plainly that the earlier
gap was a sourcing error rather than a distribution. **The terminal risk-free rate is read live
from the house macro path** instead of typed, which is the right fix even though its published
explanation was not updated with it. **The workbook reproduces the model on all 716 formula
cells and reproduces cell for cell against an independent sandbox rebuild across 1,522
populated cells.** **The gap review is the strongest document in this directory**: it covers all
eight headings, walks the move from 111.62 with the single lever named and priced, discloses
the newer library price and what it does to the gap, and says in terms that the correction runs
*away* from the traded price and would have been made anyway.

The free-cash-flow waterfall foots by hand on every year and reaches the present value inline.
The bridge stands on the latest disclosed sheet, foots to the stated equity value, charges the
cash exactly once and publishes three framings of the minority. Margins are outputs of a
two-line disclosed cost stack and the bottom-up build reproduces the filed year to 0.10% on
revenue and 0.26% on EBITDA in a test that could have failed. The far forecast years are
published as a range with the basis and the count named, and the study refuses to extend that
range to years it cannot measure. The band record carries its count beside its percentage and
earns no flag.

The defects above are almost all in the pictures, the words and the register — not in the
arithmetic. That is what makes them dangerous, and it is why reading the rendered pages and
auditing from outside the study are gates rather than formalities. The single most useful
thing this audit found is the one no programmatic check in this repository can find: **nine
figures that were never rebuilt, carrying the previous answer into the current document, past
a figure gate that verifies their pixels and an opacity gate that verifies their alpha.**
