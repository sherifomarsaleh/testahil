Sinai Cement Company S.A.E. · Egyptian Exchange: SCEM · EGP · edition of 07-09-2026

Audited from OUTSIDE the study per [R-ENF-02] by a reader who did not build it. Every row
names the artefact, the command or the number that carries it. Nothing here is
self-certified, and where a row fails it says so plainly.

**VERDICT: NOT DELIVERABLE.** Nine hard fails, listed at the foot of this document. The
model underneath is in good order — the workbook reproduces cell for cell, the bridge
foots, the waterfall foots by hand on all five years, margins are outputs, the terminal
stands on a disclosed scalar life. What fails is the PAPER and the PROVENANCE: two
delivered documents of one edition contradict each other on whether the audited statements
were obtained; the study's own committed record says `growth_destroys_value: holds=false`
and the document asserts the opposite five times including in a figure title; the beta
sensitivity table's "(adopted)" label sits on the wrong column; and the beta itself rests
on a composite regressor that SIGCM calls a hard fail, while the sanctioned EGX30
regression has been sitting committed in this repository since 23-Aug-2026, unused.

## What the filings disclose that the model does not consume

Asked explicitly against the Sweep Register, because a self-audit that only re-checks the
work it did will keep missing the work it never did. Four answers, and the register cannot
help with any of them because it is a month stale and carries none of the six filings.

**1. The FY2024 balance sheet is in the filing and the model reads it from the trade press.**
`filings/SCC-AFS-E-1224.pdf` is on disk and was opened for the disposal gain. `ta_fy24` and
`tl_fy24` — the two figures Appendix A calls "the disclosed column" — still carry Global
Cement, cemnet, Daily News Egypt and Arab Finance as their source. The filing discloses
them; the model does not consume them from it.

**2. The equity roll does not close, and the missing EGP 1,039mn is a disclosure not read.**
§7 states it honestly: rolling FY2024 equity forward by FY2025 profit gives EGP 7,060mn
against a filed EGP 6,020mn, and the difference "is consistent with a distribution the
statements do not itemise". A distribution of that size passes through the statement of
changes in equity and the financing section of the cash-flow statement in the same filing.
Neither is registered anywhere in this study. §5 then tells a reader that "the company has
no dividend on record and its own statements confirm it twice over, to the pound" — a claim
that cannot stand beside an unexplained EGP 1,039mn hole in the same roll-forward.

**3. Note 3/2 discloses five depreciation rates and the model consumes one.** That is the
right choice and it is defended (machinery is 69.5% of note 4's gross cost, and it is the
one class disclosed as a scalar). But the other four classes and note 4's full fixed-asset
movement table — additions, disposals, accumulated depreciation by class — are read for the
life and for nothing else. Note 4's own additions are the direct disclosed check on the
capital-expenditure run rate the forecast escalates, and the study builds that run rate from
the cash-flow statement instead without reconciling the two.

**4. The disclosed corporate term.** §7 records that note 1 states the company's term ends
on 4 September 2032 while the model capitalises a perpetuity, and records that Egyptian
terms are routinely extended. That is the right treatment of a disclosure. It is listed here
because it is the one item in this class the study found on its own.

## What is genuinely strong here, and should not be lost in a rebuild

The workbook reproduces the model on all 715 formula cells and reproduces **byte for byte
against a sandbox rebuild across 1,519 populated cells**. The free-cash-flow waterfall foots
by hand on every year and reaches the present value inline. The bridge stands on the latest
disclosed sheet, foots to the stated equity value, divides to the stated per share, charges
the cash exactly once and publishes three framings of the minority. The terminal stands on a
disclosed scalar life that replaced a figure whose own validation was two errors cancelling,
and the correction runs **against** the value, which is the only direction that proves the
discipline is not fitting. Margins are outputs of a two-line disclosed cost stack and the
bottom-up build reproduces the filed year to 0.10% on revenue and 0.26% on EBITDA in a test
that could have failed. The far forecast years are published as a range with the basis and
the count named, and the study refuses to extend that range to years it cannot measure. The
band record is generated, carries its count beside its percentage, and earns no flag. The
gap review covers all eight headings and audits the gap it was actually written against.

The defects above are almost all in the words, the labels and the register — not in the
arithmetic. That is what makes them dangerous, and it is why reading the rendered pages and
auditing the provenance from outside the study are gates rather than formalities.
