**VERDICT: NO DRIVER MOVES. Seven of seven committed drivers returned no input, and one access record is now known to be stale.**


## The access record is stale and must be re-dated

Perplexity reports the company IR site as "searched but not found/retrievable".
**`sinaicement.com` returns HTTP 200 from this environment on 2026-09-17** — and more than
that, **this study has already downloaded documents from it**: its bridge record says the
reviewed interim statements for the three months ended 31 March 2026 were "downloaded from the
company's own website and read by OCR off the rendered pixels (the file carries a 37-byte text
layer across 37 pages)".

This study's own negative search, dated 2026-08-06, says every host was "refused by egress
policy". **That was true then and is false now.** Two records of one fact, disagreeing because
the world moved — which is exactly why a negative search carries a date. **Re-dated, not
deleted.**

## What the site carries, and the gap it reveals

Six PDFs, the most recent labelled **"Financial statements Mar 2026"**.

**There are no June 2026 statements on this company's own website as of 17 September 2026.**
Set beside the other names in this wave that is stark rather than neutral: ARCC published its
Q2-2026 financials in August and its investor presentation in September; AMOC carries
consolidated and standalone accounts at 30 June 2026. **SCEM's own channel stops at March.**

### The two halves nobody joined up

On **14 September 2026** the EGX Listing Committee imposed two EGP 10,000 obligations on this
company for non-compliance with **Articles 34 and 48**, and Perplexity notes it cannot say what
conduct lay behind either. **The company has filed no half-year statements.**

A penalty on a periodic-disclosure article, landing in the same window as an absent half-year
filing, is one coherent story — and **each engine saw one half of it**.

**It is a hypothesis, not a finding**, falsifiable by one document: the EGX committee decision.
If it holds, this study's information set stops at March **because the company stopped filing**,
not because anybody failed to look — and that changes what this study can fairly be held to.

## The corporate-action lead

**31 August 2026 board decision; FRA no-objection reported 10 September** — a proposal to amend
Article 3 of the articles of association, adding quarrying and quarry exploitation, sale and
export of raw materials, technical and technology-support services, recycling of industrial
waste, and participation in other projects.

**It is an FRA no-objection to publication of a disclosure report** ahead of an EGM. **Not an
approved amendment, not a project.** No capacity, spend, timing, contract or target is stated
anywhere in either return. Every one of the five activities touches a committed driver, and
**reading a project into a change of legal purpose would be inventing one**.

Both returns trace it to the same vendor feed, so their agreement is **one source read twice**.

Also reported: the exchange removed the effect of a non-compliant sale of **113 shares**
executed 30 August. De minimis, recorded, not a corporate action.

## Driver by driver — seven of seven, nothing found

Sales volume, realised price, EBITDA margin glide, cost of debt, terminal risk-free, treasury
income, capex: **no input on any of them**, from either engine.

**That is the honest result of the pass and it is not a failure of the engines.** It is what
the situation looks like when an issuer's own channel has stopped publishing — and the correct
output is a dated negative search rather than a manufactured lead.
