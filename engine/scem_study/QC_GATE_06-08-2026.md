## What the gates caught (they earned their keep)

**Recalculation gate (q)** found five real defects before delivery: a circular reference between the DCF cash line and the balance sheet; a bridge cell pointing at *total assets* instead of *cash*; the normalised-earnings lens pointing one row off (it returned the P/E multiple, 7.00, instead of the value per share, 57.97); a cash-flow row label beginning with "=" that Excel parsed as a formula; and two wrong expected values.

**Driver test (r)** forced three expectations to be corrected rather than the model — in each case the model was right:

1. **Terminal growth destroys value here.** Raising g from 3% to 7% lowers fair value (EGP 57.28 → 50.50) because terminal ROIC (9.4%) sits below terminal WACC (16.3%), so growth must be bought with reinvestment that earns less than it costs. Asserting the textbook direction would have asserted the wrong thing.
2. **The tax rate pulls both ways.** A higher rate cuts NOPAT (−6.5%), the explicit-window PV (−6.7%) and the terminal value (−12.6%), but *raises* derived FY2024 treasury income (+16.8%) and with it the cash balance, because treasury income is solved from disclosed after-tax profit. Net cash is 37% of equity value, so the cash leg wins the per-share headline by +0.7%. Both legs are now asserted separately so neither can silently break.
3. **Replacement cost lowers the value.** A dearer build cost raises terminal invested capital, cutting terminal ROIC (−13.3%) and raising the reinvestment rate (+15.4%). One-directional and clean; the original assertion was simply backwards.

## Open limitations carried into delivery

- **Item (d) fails** and is reported as a failure. The price map is illustrative only.
- **Provenance.** The audited statements could not be retrieved — the environment's egress policy refused 13 external hosts including the company's own site and the exchange portal. Revenue and profit after tax are carried as disclosed via press reporting of the exchange filing; every line between them is derived and labelled. The information-sweep validator raises 2 provenance errors on exactly this point; the flag was left to fire rather than closed by downgrading it.
- **The balance sheet does not reconcile.** Rolling FY2024 equity forward by FY2025 profit with no distribution gives EGP 7,065mn against a reported ~EGP 5,200mn. The EGP 1,865mn gap implies a FY2025 distribution no obtainable source reports. Disclosed in §7 and in the workbook, not plugged.
- **No PDF.** The reference set ships .docx/.pdf/.xlsx. PDF conversion needs LibreOffice import filters that are absent, and the package repositories are unreachable through the same egress policy. DOCX and XLSX are delivered.
