### 2.1 The two halves nobody joined up

Perplexity reports that on **14 September 2026** the EGX Listing Committee imposed two
EGP 10,000 obligations on Sinai Cement — **for non-compliance with Articles 34 and 48** of
the listing rules — and notes it "does not specify the factual conduct behind either article
breach".

**The company's own website has published no half-year statements.** A listing-rule penalty
for a periodic-disclosure article, landing in the same window as an absent half-year filing,
is a coherent single story — and **neither return connected them**, because each was looking
at one half.

**It is a hypothesis, not a finding.** Article 34's subject matter is not established here,
the EGX decision itself is not readable from this environment, and a company can breach a
disclosure article for reasons having nothing to do with a missing filing. What makes it
worth writing down is that it is **falsifiable by one document** — the EGX committee decision
— and that if it holds, the study's information set stops at March because **the company
stopped filing**, not because anybody failed to look.

## 3. THE CORPORATE-ACTION LEAD

**31 August 2026 board decision; FRA no-objection reported 10 September 2026** — a proposal
to amend Article 3 of the articles of association, broadening the corporate purpose to
quarrying and quarry exploitation, sale and export of raw materials, technical and technology
support services, recycling of industrial waste from cement production, and participation in
other projects.

**What it is:** an FRA no-objection to *publication of a disclosure report*, preparatory to
calling an extraordinary general meeting. **It is not an approved amendment and not a
project.** No capacity, spend, timing, contract or financial target is stated anywhere in
either return.

**Why it is still worth registering:** every one of the five proposed activities touches a
committed driver of this study — quarrying and raw-material sourcing touch cost; export
authority touches the volume and realised-price mix; recycling touches the fuel and materials
stack. **None of those effects is established by the disclosure**, and reading one into it
would be inventing a project from a change of legal purpose.

Both returns carry it and both trace it to the same vendor feed, so their agreement is **one
source read twice**, not corroboration — the same caution as ELEC's Zain lead.

Also reported: EGX removed the effect of a non-compliant sale of **113 shares** executed
30 August. De minimis, recorded, not a corporate action.
