# Mechanical lens, declaration 6 — one year is not an anchor

**Sealed and committed BEFORE any figure under it was computed.** A fact about commit
order, checkable from outside: the commit introducing this file must be an ancestor of
the commit introducing any score computed under it.

Supersedes declaration 4 **in the origin anchor and in the two macro paths, and in
nothing else.** Origins, horizons, cost of capital, bridge, terminal construction,
point-in-time discipline, benchmarks, bootstrap and the era and leave-one-name-out tests
stand exactly as written. Declaration 5 is **withdrawn** and its withdrawal is recorded
in the module rather than deleted.

---

## 1. One defect, four costumes

Every one of these takes a single year's figure and treats it as permanent. They were
found one at a time, over a day, each looking like its own problem:

| # | what is frozen | measured cost |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | an imported commodity priced in the **wrong currency** | dollar coal falling ~90% by construction |
| 2 | the **currency** itself, as a rate | origin 2017 asserts ×17.557 against a realised ×1.077 |
| 3 | **inflation**, as a rate | origin 2017 asserts ×3.643 against a published ×1.607 |
| 4 | **every driver level** — volume, price per tonne, cost per tonne, overhead | origin 2020 values a large profitable manufacturer at 0.8% of its price |

**They are the same error.** A rate or a level observed once is carried forward as though
the world stopped. This declaration closes the fourth and applies the second and third
where they were found but not yet fixed.

## 2. The anchor

**The bottom-up structure is not the defect and is not touched.** The runs project
`volume x price per tonne` and `cost per tonne`, segment by segment, and that is right.
What is wrong is that every LEVEL in that structure is read at the origin year alone, so
a depressed year propagates through a correctly-built model — a proper buildup standing
on one bad brick.

**Each driver level is anchored on the TRAILING MEDIAN of the three years ending at the
origin**, rather than on the origin year alone. Growth, escalation and every path applied
to that level are unchanged.

Three things make this an estimator rather than a judgement, which matters because this
lens is forbidden judgement drivers by construction:

- **The lens already does exactly this.** Capex intensity is a trailing three-year median
  today. This extends an estimator the declaration already uses; it does not invent one.
- **A median is not a choice about which year was exceptional.** Nobody decides that 2020
  was COVID; the estimator is blind to why a year is unusual and simply refuses to let one
  observation set a level.
- **The window is the one already in the file** — three years, the same as capex — and is
  therefore borrowed rather than minted. A window chosen here would be the free parameter
  the promotion rule forbids.

**Where fewer than three years are available the origin year stands**, as it does now, and
the cell is unchanged.

## 3. The two paths, applied where they were found and not yet fixed

Identical in construction to what already runs on the other name, and for the same
reason — the house macro path derives its forward currency by relative purchasing-power
parity and states that it is never set by hand, and it carries a LADDER to a terminal
rather than a rate:

- **Inflation** is the origin's own published forward ladder, compounded, point-in-time.
- **The currency** drifts at the inflation differential against long-run foreign
  inflation, on that same ladder.

## 4. What this gives up

**A trailing median lags a genuine step change.** A company that has truly re-based —
an acquisition, a new plant, a lost contract — is anchored below or above where it
actually is for up to two years. That cost is real and it is accepted: the alternative
on offer is a single observation, which is wrong more often and by more.

**The direction is not predicted here and may not be.** A median raises a depressed
origin and lowers an elevated one, and which dominates across nine origins and five names
is measured after this file is committed, never before.

## 5. Falsifier

Unchanged: if the mechanically rebuilt series does not resemble the series the house
actually delivers, once that record is long enough to compare, this calibration is
grading a method the house does not use and every finding under it must be withdrawn.
