## What this does NOT buy, stated rather than discovered later

**1. No evidence that this house's lean is INFORMATION rather than merely a lean.**
[R-VCAL-02 CLAUSE TWO] states this cost in terms. The pooled bias is **+0.3761** — fair
values sitting on average 46% above the prices they were struck against. Whether that
cheapness predicts anything is clauses D and E, and **the first vintage matures
11-06-2027.** Adoption asserts SOUNDNESS, never EDGE.

**2. Clause A no longer bounds how cheap the method may lean.** Three things guard that
and none is a hope: [R-GAP-01]'s delivery audit stays **two-sided**, so a study more than
10% above its price still owes its eight-heading review; [R-VCAL-01]'s promotion guard
stays **symmetric**; and the forward record is kept from the first day.

**3. The backtest is one market.** Ten completed runs, every one Egyptian — which is the
campaign's own fixed order with a hard stop after EGX, not an unmeasured gap. This book has
already paid for reading a one-market finding as a general one: [R-TERM-01 CLAUSE TWO] was
adopted because a defect measured only where it hurt **reverses sign** in a pegged market.
**A clause passing here is evidence about EGX**, and the UAE leg is where it is tested.

**4. The mechanical series is 22 cells on 5 names.** Small, and the leave-one-name-out
readings show how much rests on each: dropping one name moves the pooled figure by up to
0.14.

**5. Four cells remain open with their causes named and not fixed.** One name's miss is
dominated by an inflation path no origin could have seen, and cannot be corrected without
feeding a past origin information it did not have. Its residual is a real defect —
two of three segments projected on a top-down macro floor rather than their own driver —
whose fix needs a backlog series the run does not commit. **It is named rather than
estimated, and the direction is why: building it on a proxy would move those values toward
the price**, which is exactly the circumstance in which a house must not approximate.
