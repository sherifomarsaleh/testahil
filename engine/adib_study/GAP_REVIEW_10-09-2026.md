## 3 · MACRO COHERENCE

**THIS HEADING IS WHAT THIS EDITION FIXES.** The terminal risk-free is now read from the
house Egyptian macro path — 10.50%, being terminal inflation of 7.00% plus the house real
convention of 3.50% — where the previous edition carried 12.50% built on a real convention
of 5.5%. Terminal growth is 7.00%, the same inflation, so one economy sits in both the growth
and the rate that discounts it.

**THE SOVEREIGN QUOTE IS STALE AND THE STUDY SAYS SO IN ITS OWN RECORD:** the risk-free is
dated 2026-08-06, **34 days** before this review, past the house path's own 14-day bound. It
is disclosed and deliberately accepted rather than switched off, because refreshing a house
path is a house-level act and not a step of one name's rebuild.

## 4 · DISCOUNT RATE

Cost of equity **29.81%** = normalised risk-free 19.58% + beta 1.0747 × equity risk premium
9.52%. Terminal cost of equity **18.02%** under a declared `same_beta` construction.

**COUNTRY RISK IS CHARGED ONCE.** The risk-free is the 23.00% local government yield less
this sovereign's own 3.42% default spread, on the same basis as the premium added back. The
retired construction that added the premium to the RAW yield is computed and published at
**33.23%** — 342 basis points higher — so the double count is priced rather than asserted
away. A rating-basis alternative gives 31.52%.

**A BANK'S COST OF FUNDING IS NOT ITS COST OF DEBT AND THIS MODEL DOES NOT TREAT IT AS ONE.**
There is no weighted average cost of capital here: deposits are the raw material of the
business and are inside the margin, not in the discount rate. The dividend lens discounts
equity flows at the cost of equity, which is the only rate a bank valuation needs.

## 5 · TERMINAL

Terminal growth 7.00% nominal against a terminal cost of equity of 18.02% embedding the same
7.00% inflation — an 11.0-point spread, and no real decline hidden in it.

**THE DIVIDEND PATH IS WHAT THE TERMINAL RESTS ON AND IT IS NOT ASSUMED — IT IS SOLVED FROM
THE CAPITAL RULE.** Equity is held at 10.5% of assets throughout, so the payout is whatever
is left after funding growth: 18.2% in 2026 rising to **62.0% by 2030** as the balance sheet's
growth decelerates. Dividends per share run 1.64 → 3.22 → 5.33 → 6.67 → **8.28**.

**THE STUDY ASSUMES ONE CAPITAL RAISE AND IT IS DISCLOSED:** EGP 3,000mn in 2026, because at
10.5% equity-to-assets the forecast growth cannot be funded from retained profit alone in
that year. It dilutes, and it is in the number.

## 6 · BALANCE SHEET

The forecast stands on the H1-2026 reviewed sheet. Financing-to-assets is **45.96%** at
H1-2026 against 42.46% in the base year, and equity-to-assets **10.51%** against 9.98% — the
bank is lending more of its balance sheet and is better capitalised than a year ago.

**AVERAGE BALANCES, NEVER YEAR-END.** Every ratio in the projection is struck on average
total assets — 405,158 in 2026, 772,970 by 2030 — because a fast-growing lender's closing
sheet flatters every ratio computed on it.

## 7 · CLAIMS AGAINST THE RECORD

**THE COST OF RISK IS THE CLAIM MOST WORTH CHECKING, AND IT RUNS AGAINST THIS STUDY.**
H1-2026 reviewed prints an expected-credit-loss charge of **EGP 2.176mn — a cost of risk of
essentially zero** on a book of this size. The base year printed 1.25%. **The forecast
charges 1,825.6 in 2026 rising to 5,177.4 by 2030**, which is a normalisation the company's
most recent reviewed half does not show any sign of.

That is the correct treatment — a half-year at almost no loss charge is not a run rate — but
it must be stated on the record as what it is: **the model is charging a normal loss cycle
that has not started, against a bank currently reporting none.** If the benign charge
persists, this study is too low, and it is too low for a reason it chose.

**THE FAR-YEAR RANGES ARE FROM THE WALK-FORWARD, NOT FROM JUDGEMENT.** The 2028 parent
profit point of 17,161 sits inside a measured band of 4,986 to 25,394 — a multiple of 0.29×
to 1.48× — drawn from eight scoreable origins of this method's own history on this bank. The
band is wide because the record is wide.

## The audit's conclusion

**No error found that would close this gap, and the two largest judgements both run AGAINST
the answer rather than for it:** the margin path opens below the latest reviewed half and
falls for five years, and the loss charge normalises from a reviewed period showing
essentially none. **A study that is wrong here is wrong by being too pessimistic**, and the
correction that moved this edition narrowed the gap without being aimed at it.

**What would make this study the one that is wrong:** the full year to 31 December 2026. If
the margin holds nearer the H1 6.44% than the forecast 5.94%, and the cost of risk stays
near zero rather than normalising to 1.25%, the dividend path is understated from year one
and the central rises toward the market. **Both are single lines in a filing that is
months away, not matters of opinion.**
