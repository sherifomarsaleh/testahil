   "The price closed EGP 54.40 on 2026-09-09, the last session in the history this study

   Table 8 prints 17.82% and the cell beside it says "the same beta times a normalised 7.0%

   read nor a contested-judgement record". (Rows 13, 22.)
5. **The expert appendix carries zero tables** against the model report's six. No worked
   valuation table for any expert, no divergence table, no ranges figure. (Row 12.)
6. **The waterfall never reaches present value in the delivered document.** (Row 21.)
7. **No band record in §3** — zero hits for coverage, resolved forecasts, or a 90% band.
   (Row 17.)

The beta is the strongest artefact here: regressed against the published index of the exchange
the stock is listed on, re-derived independently from outside the study and matching to ten
decimal places, and passing the usability gate with the coefficient at 6.6× its own standard
error. Country risk is counted once, both equity-premium bases are published, and the retired
double-counting construction is printed for contrast. The workbook recalculates with zero
mismatches under an evaluator that did not write it, and every delivered artefact reproduces
cell-for-cell from its builder — 983 workbook cells, 839 study items, 549 bibliography items,
zero differences. Sixteen consolidated fiscal years foot against their own arithmetic. The
walk-forward tested 14 correction candidates and adopted none, and said why. The horizon ruling
is a real decision on a real instruction with the number in front of the principal, its
arithmetic checks, and the gate it leaves red is red in the open rather than ratcheted.
