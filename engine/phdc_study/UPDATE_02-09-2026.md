## What this edition is

A class-A interim edition under the method reassessment of 2 September 2026: it
applies only what an existing rule already required and the review of
1 September 2026 found missing, and it changes no method. Three corrections:

1. **The bridge stands on the latest disclosed balance sheet.** Net debt, the
   associates, the investment property, the borrowings and the book value now
   come from the reviewed statement of 31 March 2026 (`bs_1q2026.json`,
   registered line by line and footed), not from 31 December 2025. Net debt
   EGP 24,133.2mn → 23,244.7mn.
2. **Minority interests are deducted, at their share of value.** The 30 August
   edition deducted nothing for them while dividing by parent shares. The
   company does not disclose the subsidiaries that carry the minority with their
   own economics, so the share of value is proxied by the minority's filed share
   of FY2025 profit after tax (4.68%), applied to equity value —
   the same construction as TMGH's edition of the same day. Book
   (EGP 1,432.7mn) and the three-year mean profit share
   (5.94%) are published beside it. On the base case the
   deduction is EGP 2,042.8mn, 0.71 a share.
3. **Normalised earnings are capitalised at cost of equity less growth**, the
   growth being the 12% the cash-flow model itself carries. E/ke in a currency whose
   cost of equity embeds Egyptian inflation is a perpetual real decline, not
   conservatism. The lens moves from 5.17 to 8.39.

The discount rate (26.25%), the forecast and the lens weights are unchanged.
