# CBE INVESTIGATION INTO THE FINANCING OF REAL-ESTATE DEVELOPERS — REGISTERED LEAD

**Received 18 September 2026 from the principal:** *"the CBE is running an investigation into
financing Real Estate developers"*, and *"THE CBE affects all real estate developers in
egypt"*.

**NOTHING IS CONSUMED. This is a lead, registered, not an input.**

## SCOPE — CLASS, AND THE CLASS IS NAMED RATHER THAN ASSUMED

Read off each study's own committed class record, the book holds **three real-estate
developers**:

| Ticker | Class as committed | Market | Bound by this? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PHDC** | real-estate developer, off-plan, percentage-of-completion | **EG** | **YES** |
| **TMGH** | real-estate developer, off-plan, point-in-time on handover | **EG** | **YES** |
| MODON | real-estate developer, off-plan, percentage-of-completion | **AE (Abu Dhabi)** | **NO** — the Central Bank of Egypt does not govern an Abu Dhabi issuer |

Excluding MODON is deliberate. "Affects all real-estate developers in Egypt" is a statement
about a jurisdiction, and sweeping a UAE developer in because it shares a class would be the
cross-attribution error this house has already paid for once.

## THE PRIMARY SOURCE IS NOT REACHABLE FROM HERE — DATED NEGATIVE SEARCH, 2026-09-18

- `https://www.cbe.org.eg` — **HTTP 200**, the homepage answers.
- `https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/news-publications/news` — **REJECTED at the edge**: a 269-byte
  body reading *"The requested URL was rejected. Please consult with your administrator."*

So the central bank's own news channel is blocked to this environment while its homepage is
not. **The investigation is therefore unverified here**, and stays a lead until the CBE's own
statement, a circular to banks, or a filing referring to it can be opened.

## NEITHER ENGINE FOUND IT, AND THAT IS PART OF THE RECORD

The 17-09-2026 external-news pass ran two independent engines on TMGH. **Perplexity returned
zero mentions of the central bank. Gemini returned five, and every one is inflation data or
the monetary stance** — the August CPI release of 10 September, and a line on tight policy
constraining the secondary resale market. **No investigation into developer financing appears
in either return.**

Both passes also ran an explicit "searched, not found" on company-specific regulatory action.
That negative search is now known to be incomplete for a class-level event, which is a
limitation of a search pointed at one issuer rather than at its regulator.

## WHAT IT WOULD TOUCH IF CONFIRMED — NAMED, NOT PRICED

Unpriced on purpose: pricing an unverified regulatory event would be inventing a number.

- **Developer access to credit**, and therefore the cost of debt and the leverage path.
- **The financing of off-plan instalment receivables**, which is the working-capital engine of
  both these companies.
- **Buyer mortgage availability**, and therefore absorption and the delivery schedule.
- On **TMGH** specifically: the CEO's stated 99.6% collection rate, already registered as
  broadcast commentary to be scored and never consumed, and the reported slowdown in the
  secondary market attributed to tight monetary conditions.
- On **PHDC** specifically: the undelivered order book and the instalment terms behind it.

## WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

1. The CBE statement or circular is the document that settles it. It is not reachable from
   here and is registered as such rather than worked around.
2. Until it is read, **no driver moves on either name** and no cost-of-capital input changes.
3. If it is confirmed, it is a **macro/regulatory input for the Egyptian developer class**, and
   under the house rule a study may not carry a macro number of its own — so it would enter
   through the house path and the class, never as a one-name adjustment.
