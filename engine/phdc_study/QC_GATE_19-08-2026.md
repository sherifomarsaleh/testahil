### Stop-and-inform: two primary documents sought and NOT obtained

Both are named on page 1 of the bibliography, not buried.

1. **The 1H2026 results release does not exist publicly.** The company's investor-relations site, the
   content interface behind it and the wire service that carried earlier Palm Hills releases were all
   checked on 19 August 2026; the newest financial result on any of them is the 1Q2026 release of
   20 May. **Consequence:** the company's own backlog figure, its own net-debt definition applied to
   the half, first-half contracted sales and first-half cash collection are unavailable for this
   period. The study therefore anchors backlog on **note 72 of the reviewed statements** — the
   contractual value of undelivered-unit contracts concluded since 1 January 2023, EGP 149.12bn —
   and says so, rather than carrying the company's wider EGP 263bn definition from 1Q2026 forward.
2. **The FY2025 annual audited statements are not published,** and neither is a FY2025 results
   release. **Consequence:** the audited-year depth is 2 (FY2023, FY2024) against a target of 4, and
   FY2025 appears in the historical income statement marked *"company release only"* for revenue,
   EBITDA and net profit, with cost of revenues, gross profit and gross margin left blank rather
   than reconstructed.

Neither gap was filled with an unofficial substitute. Both are disclosed in the delivered documents.

**Verdict on the prior stack.** Three failures, in order of consequence: (i) the discount rate was set
below the local risk-free rate; (ii) the whole revenue and margin build rested on assumed unit
economics with no disclosed base period, where the filings disclose construction executed and the
price-to-build-cost ratio can be measured directly; (iii) net debt and the share count were hand-set
rather than read off the statements. This edition replaces all three. It also replaces the valuation
architecture: the contested judgement — whether the Residents' Association float is the company's
money — was not identified at all in the prior edition, and it is worth more than any other single
item in the model (EGP 15.82 against EGP 5.79).
