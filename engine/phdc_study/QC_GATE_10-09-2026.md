## The direction of this edition, which is what the answer row is for

The central sits **+46.5% above** the traded price — the widest gap this study has carried — and
this edition **raised it again**, by **+18.1%** (17.8617 → 21.0897), on a single input: the
terminal real interest rate, 5.5% → 3.5%. Three instruments were pointed at that.

1. **The reasoning for the change is sound and is stated in the study's own voice.** A restrictive
   policy real rate is a description of a monetary condition, not a perpetual property of a
   company, and carrying it into perpetuity charges a developer for ever for something temporary.
   Reading the house macro path rather than a number typed into the study's own file is the right
   direction of travel. READ FIRST says so explicitly and pre-empts the obvious objection: *"THE
   DIRECTION IS NOT THE REASON. This correction happens to raise the value and move it further
   above the traded price; it would have been made had it done the opposite."*

2. **The sign test says there is no lean — but it was computed on an answer this study no longer
   publishes.** Five material judgements, two up and three down, p = 1.00. The three downward ones
   are large and the study took them. That is real evidence in its favour. It is also stamped
   `2026-09-02` with every adopted value at 17.1517, so as a statement about THIS edition it is
   not evidence of anything.

3. **The reverse read is the most useful thing in the delivery and it is honest.** At 14.40 the
   price is paying for cash conversion of **6.34%** against the study's **8.71%** and against a
   filed range of **3.94% to 17.87%** that this company has actually printed. The market's number
   sits inside the range the filings support. As `diagnostics.json` puts it, "the disagreement is
   238 basis points on ONE driver a reader can check, which is a more useful statement than 'the
   study is +46.5% against the price'." That is right, and it is the strongest part of this study.

**What a reader cannot see.** The document never states the +46.5%; the only framing it publishes
is the summary table's "vs the central **−32%**". Both framings belong in the same table. And the
gap review, the one artefact that argues the direction, is internal.
