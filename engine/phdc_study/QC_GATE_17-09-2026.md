## VERDICT

**NOT DELIVERABLE at this edition.** The committed record is in good order and the three
things the recalibration changed all verify. What blocks delivery is the **delivered
document and workbook**, which no programmatic gate in this repository can see and which
reading the rendered PDF page by page found in quantity: the study publishes two different
fundamental ranges, states a gap count of six above a table of five, tells a reader the
model runs five years when it runs fifteen, labels four 2040 figures as 2030 under a
caption that contradicts its own cell, prints a waterfall that misses EGP 41,200mn, and
ships a workbook whose DCF sheet computes a 2026 gross profit EGP 1,139.6mn above the one
the document prints.

**Shortest list of what blocks it**

1. §7 says "**Six** things are not disclosed" above a table of **five** rows
   (`docx_phdc.py:1114-1127`); the bibliography's own gap table carries six.
2. The cash-flow lens range is published **two ways**: 4.01–46.50 (headline, summary
   table, §1.5) and 6.14–40.29 (Figure 1's ticks, §1.9 grid, §4, Expert 1, §C.5). Nothing
   reconciles them and the asymmetric +200bp / −100bp schedule shift that produces the
   first pair is disclosed nowhere.
3. **Five years vs fifteen**, in five places including two that label the same figure both
   ways: §1.1 prose "runs five years" against §1.1's own bridge row "Present value of the
   explicit 15 years"; Expert 1's "Present value of five explicit years 49,462" against
   page 5's "explicit 15 years 49,462.4"; the A.1 caption "Five forecast years" over a
   2026–2040 table; `Fundamental Valuation!A4`.
4. The §A.3 funding block: four figures labelled **2030** are **2040** values
   (`statements.py:270-273` takes `rows[-1]` of a fifteen-row projection), under a caption
   asserting interest "would exceed operating profit outright" against a printed **75%**
   (the 2030 figure is 201%).
5. **Expert 2's working table is EGP 41,200mn out of balance** — a reader following the
   printed rows reaches −20,812 against a printed 20,388, on a row labelled "balancing".
6. The delivered **workbook publishes two 2026 gross profits**: 14,246.0 (Income Statement,
   agreeing with the document) and 15,385.6 (`DCF!B10 = B9*Assumptions!$B$8` at 38.32%),
   **8.0% apart** — and 38.32% is the construction `bottom_up_model.py:154-165` explicitly
   rejects.
7. **Gross margin is an INPUT and four artefacts say it is an OUTPUT.**
   `bottom_up_model.py:223` is `cost_per = rev_per * (1 - GM_FORWARD)`; the code says so in
   terms, the document, the workbook and the attested ground-up record say the opposite.
8. The §3 cone is labelled "close 14.40 (3 Sep 2026)" while its own resolve dates and its
   own `asof.mc_data` put its origin on **23 August at 15.20**.
9. `DELIVERED_FY24 = 2000.0` — a typed, round, **unsourced** number with no registry entry,
   from which every per-unit revenue and cost figure in the forecast descends. Not in §7's
   gaps, not in the Driver Ledger.
10. The **1H2025 earnings release**, held on disk and inside the stated information set, is
    consumed for the land bank alone. Everything else in it — EGP 142.8bn of half-year new
    sales, the regional split, backlog of 203bn, 43% gross margin, operating cash of 3.17%
    of revenue (below the study's own 3.9% floor), 1,700 contracted units ready for
    handover — is unconsumed and unnamed, and the release is absent from the bibliography's
    primary-documents table.

The land-bank workbook gap named in the brief is confirmed and is the **smallest** of these.
