## The company

**Palm Hills Developments S.A.E.** — "PHD" — listed on the Egyptian Exchange as **PHDC**
(also quoted as PHDC.CA). Arabic registered name as it appears on the company's own filed
statements: **بالم هيلز للتعمير**. Real-estate developer: off-plan residential in West Cairo,
East Cairo, Alexandria and the North Coast, plus seven operating hotels, and a stated Abu
Dhabi expansion.

**SEARCH IN BOTH LANGUAGES.** On this exchange the Arabic trade press and the EGX's own Arabic
disclosure feed carry material the English coverage never repeats, and a one-language search
misses the half of the record that matters most.

## AND ONE MORE, ADDED TODAY, WHICH IS THE MOST USEFUL THING YOU COULD FIND

**THE LAND BANK'S CURRENT SIZE.** This study carries **37 million square meters**, from the
1H2025 release of 13 August 2025 — which is **more than a year behind its own information
set**. The land bank is an operating KPI, so it appears in earnings releases and investor
presentations and **never in a financial statement**, however recent. The company said in that
same release it was "actively seeking to replenish our landbank in 2025".

**FIND THE LATEST DISCLOSED LAND-BANK FIGURE AND THE DOCUMENT AND DATE IT COMES FROM.** A
figure in an article is a lead; the release, presentation or filing behind it is the answer.

## The drivers this company actually turns on

Ask about these rather than about generic real-estate topics:

- **New sales (pre-sales) value and units sold** — the top of the funnel, and the figure PHD
  leads its own releases with
- **Deliveries / units handed over** — revenue is recognised on completion, so deliveries and
  not sales drive the income statement
- **Average selling price per unit, and per square metre**
- **Launches: which projects, when, how many units, at what price** — volume is set by the
  launch calendar
- **Collections, and the receivable/instalment profile** — an off-plan developer's cash is its
  collection cycle
- **Construction spend and contractor cost inflation**
- **Land acquisition: any plot bought, bid won, or government land allocation**, with area and
  consideration
- **Debt: any new facility, securitisation, bond or sukuk, and its COUPON**
- **The Abu Dhabi expansion** — a project targeting EGP 300bn in sales was described as
  launching "before year end or first quarter next year" as at August 2025. What happened?
- **Egyptian mortgage availability, interest rates, and any change to off-plan sales
  regulation**

## Rules for the return, and they are not formalities

1. **EVERY CLAIM CARRIES ITS SOURCE, ITS DATE, AND A LINK.** A number with no document behind
   it cannot enter this model at all — historicals come from the company's own issued
   statements and releases, never from a vendor, a broker, or the press as a numbers source.
2. **SAY WHEN YOU FOUND NOTHING.** "Searched for the FY2025 results release, not found" is a
   real result and will be recorded as a dated negative search. Do not pad.
3. **DO NOT ESTIMATE, INTERPOLATE OR RECONCILE.** If two sources disagree, report both and say
   they disagree.
4. **DISTINGUISH what the company disclosed from what somebody wrote about it.** An EGX filing
   reported by a newspaper names where a document was lodged; it is not that document.
5. Flag anything that would move a driver above, and say which one.
