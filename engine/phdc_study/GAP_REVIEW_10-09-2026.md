## Verdict

**No error found in the company's favour.** The lever that moved this edition took a house
macro rate off a house file, was owed under [R-MACRO-01], and would have been taken in
either direction.

**What would make this the study that is wrong is specific and it is one number:** the cash
conversion rate. The forecast holds 8.71%, the market implies 6.34%, and the company's own
filed record spans 3.94% to 17.87%. If the half to 30 June 2026 — due, and not out at this
date — prints conversion near the bottom of that span rather than near the forecast, the
market's number is the better reading and the bear case at EGP 4.98 is the relevant one.
**The filing that settles it is weeks away**, which is a rare position for a disagreement
this size to be in.
