## 1 · The finding that matters: the published discount rate is below the sovereign

The 11-Jun-2026 edition carried a single hardcoded input on its Assumptions
sheet — **`WACC (nominal EGP) 0.18`** — with no beta, no risk-free and no equity
risk premium behind it. Egypt's own 10-year sovereign yield is **23.00%**. An
18% nominal EGP discount rate for a levered equity therefore prices Palm Hills
as **safer than the government that taxes it**, which cannot be right. The
edition's cash flows are unambiguously nominal — its own sheet escalates selling
prices at 14% a year — so this is not a real-versus-nominal mismatch. It is a
discount rate roughly 800bp too low, applied to a long-dated developer model
where that error compounds hardest.

Rebuilt bottom-up under the house v2 method, every input sourced live today:

| | rating-ERP basis | CDS-ERP basis |
|---|---|---|
| observed EGP 10-year sovereign yield | 23.00% | 23.00% |
| less Egypt's own default spread | 6.37% | 3.41% |
| **normalised rf\*** | **16.63%** | **19.59%** |
| ERP (Damodaran, Egypt row) | 13.94% | 9.41% |
| beta | 1.0493 | 1.0493 |
| **cost of equity** | **31.26%** | **29.46%** |
| marginal cost of debt, pre-tax | 25.50% | 25.50% |
| after tax at 22.5% | 19.76% | 19.76% |
| weights (E 56.4% / D 43.6%, equity at market) | | |
| **WACC** | **26.25%** | **25.24%** |

Country risk enters once, through the CRP inside the ERP, and the observed yield
is normalised on the matching basis — rating with rating, CDS with CDS. Using the
raw local yield beside a CRP-loaded ERP is the v1 double-count that reached GBCO.

## 4 · The crux: cash conversion, not the discount rate

Palm Hills sells off-plan on long instalment plans, so what its accounting profit
is worth depends on how fast contracted sales become cash. **The company does not
disclose its collection schedule** — no down-payment percentage, no instalment
tenor, no post-handover tail. The 11-Jun-2026 edition assumed all three. This
edition does not; it measures conversion from the three years of disclosed
cash-flow statements instead, and the measurement is the problem:

| | CFO | revenue | CFO / revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | 756.7 | 17,462.1 | **4.3%** |
| FY2024 | 4,854.8 | 27,167.3 | **17.9%** |
| FY2025 | 1,424.2 | 36,169.3 | **3.9%** |

*(FY2024's CFO is the filing's figure; the FY2024 results release reported
3,131.9mn for the same year and was later restated. The filing is used.)*

Priced across that observed range, at the bottom-up WACC and around it:

| cash conversion \ WACC | 22.25% | 24.25% | **26.25%** | 28.25% | 30.25% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **3.9%** (FY2023 and FY2025 actual) | 14.88 | 11.52 | **9.09** | 7.25 | 5.80 |
| 6.0% | 21.60 | 16.91 | **13.54** | 11.02 | 9.05 |
| **8.7%** (three-year mean) | 30.24 | 23.84 | **19.28** | 15.87 | 13.23 |
| 12.0% | 40.80 | 32.31 | **26.28** | 21.79 | 18.33 |
| **17.9%** (FY2024 actual) | 59.67 | 47.45 | **38.80** | 32.39 | 27.46 |

*EGP per share. Spot 15.20.*

Read down the column, not across it. **Moving the discount rate by 800bp — the
whole of the error in section 1 — changes value by less than moving cash
conversion from one observed year to another.** At 26.25%, the two years where
conversion was 3.9% imply EGP 9.09 and the year it was 17.9% implies EGP 38.80.

**What the market is implying.** Solving the same model back to the 23 Aug 2026
close of EGP 15.20 gives a cash-conversion rate of **6.78%** — above the 3.9% of
FY2023 and FY2025, well below the 17.9% of FY2024. That is the most defensible
single statement this update can make about the price.

## 5 · Why no single fair value is published

Three reasons, and they compound:

1. **The crux is undisclosed.** Value turns on a schedule the company does not
   publish. Filling it, as the prior edition did, produces a precise-looking
   number resting on nothing.
2. **The walk-forward record on this name.** Trained over ten annual origins on
   PHDC's own FY2011–FY2025 history, this repository's fundamental method
   over-forecast net profit by **+1.12 log — about three times — robustly, in 97%
   of cells**, and lost to freezing last year's number at every horizon. That
   record is the reason years 3–5 go out as ranges.
3. **The per-project table is unsourced.** The prior edition carried land area,
   buildable percentage, unit mix, average unit area, price per square metre and
   construction cost per square metre for fifteen named projects. **None of it is
   disclosed by the company.** It is not reused here, and the forecast is
   recorded at SEGMENT level with that gap stated, per R-SIGCM-02.

## 7 · What closes the gaps

| gap | what closes it |
|---|---|
| collection schedule (the crux) | down payment, instalment tenor and post-handover tail, from an investor presentation or directly from the company |
| per-project unit economics | project-level disclosure of unit mix, area, price/sqm and cost/sqm |
| marginal cost of debt | the coupon on any tranche of the 4-Feb-2026 EGP 2.015bn securitisation — sizes, tenors and national-scale ratings (AA+/AA/A+/A-) are public, pricing is not |
| FY2025 operating drivers | the FY2025 results release, which the company has not published — FY2025 has audited financials and no units, new sales, deliveries or construction spend |
| H1-2026 | nothing newer than 1Q2026 is posted to the result centre as at this build date |
| risk-free freshness | the EGP 10-year quote used is dated 6 Aug 2026, 24 days old |
| peers | Egyptian developer KPI and multiple set (TMGH, ORHD, EMFD, HELI, PRDC, OCDI are all covered names with libraries in this repo) |
