Cleopatra Hospitals Group Secures Regulatory Approvals to
Acquire up to 90% in Cairo Specialized Hospital
Cairo, Egypt | 5 May 2025

Cleopatra Hospitals Group S.A.E. (“CHG” or the “Company”) has secured all necessary regulatory approvals to increase its
ownership in Cairo Specialized Hospital (CSH) from 57% to up to 90% of the company’s shares, strengthening its strategic position
within the Group’s network, noting that a final value has not yet been determined and the Company is committed to complying with
Article 44 of the Listing and Delisting Rules.

CSH, which has been part of CHG for more than 10 years, is recognized for its excellence in specialized medical services. The
hospital operates with a capacity of approximately 190 beds and currently serves nearly 300,000 patients annually. It is worth
mentioning that the hospital has undergone a comprehensive renovation cycle of its infrastructure and medical equipment over the
past ten years, reflecting its critical role in Egypt’s healthcare landscape. Its revenues have grown at a compound annual growth rate
(CAGR) of 37% over the past three years, while the net profit CAGR reached 47% during the same period, demonstrating consistent
operational strength.

About Cleopatra Hospitals Group

The Group is the largest private hospital group in Egypt by number of hospital beds and number of operating hospitals. The company
holds majority stakes and operates seven leading hospitals in the Greater Cairo Area: Cleopatra Hospital, Cairo Specialized Hospital,
Nile Badrawi Hospital, Al Shorouk Hospital, El Katib Hospital, Sky Hospital, and Cleopatra October Hospital offering a full array
of general, emergency healthcare services and rehabilitation services. The Group also operates two polyclinics located in strategic
neighbourhoods of East and West Cairo and holds a majority stake in Bedaya for Medical Services, Egypt’s leading IVF and Fertility
Centre.
