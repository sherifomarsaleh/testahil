# SWDY — basis-break register

**Written before modelling, per [R-FCAL-01] §1.** INTERNAL. Every break carries its
overlap year, its chain factor where one exists, and its treatment. A unit driver is
scored only inside its own definition window.

| id | break | overlap | chain factor | treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-1 | **The segment taxonomy is re-cut at FY2017.** Through FY2016 the releases publish three segments — Wires & Cables, Electrical Products, Turnkey Projects. From FY2017 they publish six: Wires & Cables, Turnkey Projects, Meters, Transformers, Electrical Products, Renewables–IPP. | FY2016/FY2017 | none available; the older years are not restated onto the finer cut | The scored revenue drivers are the THREE groupings that survive every re-cut — cables, contracting, other — and the three foot to total revenue to the pound in every year FY2011–FY2025. A finer split would be scoreable in one era and not the other. |
| B-2 | **The segment taxonomy is re-cut again at FY2022.** From FY2022 the releases publish Wire, Cable & Accessories (which the footnote states "include the regular Wires & Cables as well as the former Other Electrical Products"), Engineering & Construction, Digital Solutions (Meters plus the technology arm), Electrical Products and Infrastructure Investment. | FY2021/FY2022 | none published | As B-1. The FY2022 note means the cables leg absorbs a slice of what was previously "other", so cables is scored as a leg and never as a like-for-like line across that boundary. |
| B-3 | **The unit KPI disclosure is DISCONTINUED after 3Q2022.** The "results at a glance" investor sheets publish cable tonnage, price per tonne and cost per tonne from FY2012 to 3Q2022 and then stop; the earnings presentations that replace them carry no tonne. | none | none | The unit drivers D1, D2, D3 and the unit basis of D4 and D7 are scored ONLY inside FY2012–FY2020, their own definition window. FY2021's sheet exists but has no text layer and its OCR loses the column alignment, so its block is REFUSED rather than carried — the identity tonnes x price does not reproduce the sheet's own cable revenue. The count of scoreable cells is published per driver. |
| B-4 | **FY2015 is RE-PRESENTED between the FY2015 audited statements and the FY2016 release.** Revenue is EGP 20,571,703,734 as originally reported and EGP 18,894,528 thousand in the following year's comparative column — 8.1% on the top line. | FY2015 | 0.9185 (18,894,528 / 20,571,704), recorded and never applied | POINT-IN-TIME IS ABSOLUTE. Every origin sees FY2015 as first reported in that year's own filing. The restated figure is carried beside it and never substituted. The chain factor is recorded so a reader can see the size of what was NOT applied. |
| B-5 | **The par value of the share changes from EGP 10 to EGP 1 on 22 May 2018**, annotated in the commercial register on 8 August 2018, and the issued count changes four times inside the window (223,418,000 → 218,418,000 → 2,184,180,000 → 2,170,777,876 → 2,140,777,876). | 2018 | x10 on the count at the split | The share count in the valuation-input block is THAT YEAR'S OWN committed capital divided by THAT YEAR'S par, footed against the count the same note states. Today's count is never carried back: at FY2016 it would be wrong by a factor of 9.6. |
| B-6 | **The currency regime moves twice inside the window** — the float of November 2016 and the float of March 2022 — taking the pound from about 7 to about 48 to the dollar. | 2016, 2022 | not a chain factor; a regime | The eras are named in the pre-registration at those two cuts. AND THE ERA CUT IS NOT THE STABILITY TEST [R-FCAL-01 AMENDED 07-09-2026]: a correction requires the sign to hold at EVERY admissible cut with five cells each side, and the era boundary is one of them rather than the one. |
| B-7 | **The interest-rate disclosure changes shape at FY2025.** FY2024 discloses a three-way EGP/USD/EUR split of the borrowing rate (28.68% / 6.49% / 3.92%); FY2025 discloses a two-way EGP / blended-hard-currency split. | FY2024/FY2025 | none | The borrowing rate used by D15 is formed as the finance charge over interest-bearing loans and borrowings, which is disclosure-independent, so the change affects the study's own cost-of-debt evidence table and not the walk-forward driver. |
| B-8 | **FY2012 and FY2017 annual consolidated statements are not published in the results centre.** Both years' figures come from the comparative column of the FOLLOWING year's audited statements and from their own earnings releases. | FY2013, FY2018 | none | Recorded rather than treated as present. Neither year is an origin whose own audited statements this run holds, and both are inside the panel rather than at its edge, so no origin depends on a year that exists only as a comparative. |
