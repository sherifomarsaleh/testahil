 Earnings Release: El Sewedy Electric Reports Consolidated Results for Q1 2011, ended March
 31st, 2011.

 May 26th, 2011, Cairo-Egypt.




           A Challenging First Quarter for El Sewedy Electric given recent political events in
           the region, yet results were positive with a 21% increase in revenues and flat EBITDA
           growth versus Q1 2010.

 El Sewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange) the leading Wire & Cable and Integrated
 Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced its consolidated financial results for the
 First Quarter ended March 31st ,2011.

      •    Consolidated revenues increased by 21% to reach LE 3,553 million for Q1 2011, versus LE 2,934 in Q1
           2010, 4% lower than Q4 2010.
       •     Gross profit dropped 8% to reach LE 448 million during Q1 2011, versus LE 490 million in Q1 2010,
             9% lower than Q4 2010.
       •     EBITDA stood at LE 335 million in Q1 2011, versus LE 342 million in Q1 2010, 29% higher than Q4
             2010.

        • Net Profit after Minority Interest dropped 32% to reach LE 171 million in Q1 2011 versus LE 251
             million for Q1 2010, 76 % higher than Q4 2010.

       •     The first quarter was a challenging period for El Sewedy given the political events that negatively
             impacted several of our key markets, mainly Egypt, Syria and Libya. However, we were able to grow
             our export sales and indeed saw a substantial increase in our international operations mainly in the Gulf
             Area.
       •     We continued to see a marked increase in revenues in all of the segments in Q1 2011 versus Q1 2010,
             with the exception of the electrical products.
       •     Q1 2011 Net Profit was affected as a result of an increase in taxes versus Q1 2010 and a decrease in
             foreign exchange gains which stood at LE 12 million in Q1 2011 versus LE 46 million in Q1 2010.
             Commenting on the Q1 2011 results, Ahmed El Sewedy C.E.O. of El Sewedy Electric stated:
              “We believe that our strategy over the last 3 years of focusing on the Middle East, Africa and Europe
             and building our capacity in diversified countries, has made our Company more stable and able to
             withstand any problems, whether political or economic, in any of the countries in which we operate in.
             Even though we have seen a drop in cables sold, mainly from Egypt, Libya and Syria, we have
             continued to see marked improvement in our Gulf and African operations. The US$ 440 million tender
             that was awarded to our subsidiary, Doha Cables for the supply of 42,000 tons has provided El Sewedy
             with our highest backlog cables orders in our history.
              We remain optimistic about growing the business further and are bullish on further increasing our
             business in Europe and generating new business in Africa. We believe that Africa is a very important
             region and our focus will be on continuing to offer the complete solution in Africa which should further
             enhance the profitability of the Group.”

 Wires & Cables Segment
 Wires & Cables segment revenues increased 11% to reach LE 2,719 million in Q1 2011 versus LE 2,450
 million in Q1 2010. Gross profit in Q1 2011 decreased 21% to reach LE 276 million versus LE 348 million in
 Q1 2010.

 In terms of volumes, the segment sold 35,276 tons of cables in Q1 2011 versus 39,928 in Q1 2010, a decrease
 of 12%. Gross profit per ton decreased 14% in Q1 2011 to reach LE 5,510 per ton versus LE 6,421 during Q1
 2010. The drop in tons sold was mainly due to the recent political events that occurred mainly in Egypt, Syria
 and Libya. However it is worth noting that the first quarter saw a marked improvement in revenues in both
 Qatar and Ethiopia which softened the impact of the turmoil in the aforementioned countries.

 Gross profit per ton also declined due to the change in revenue mix with Qatar representing a higher
 contribution to the revenues, thus bringing down the combined gross profit per ton. We continue to be positive
 on the Gulf region, Europe and Africa for this segment.

 Turnkey Projects
 Our Turnkey and Contracting business recorded a very strong performance this quarter with revenues increasing
 139% to reach LE 525 million in Q1 2011 versus LE 220 million in Q1 2010. Gross profit in Q1 2011 was LE
 94 million versus LE 67 million in Q1 2010, an increase of 40%.

 The increase in revenues was mainly the result of the continuation of the execution of EPC contracts in Egypt
 and Africa (mainly Zambia, Ethiopia, Cameroon and Mozambique.) The drop in gross profit margin from 30%
 in Q1 2010 to 18% in Q1 2010 is mainly a result of the more competitive environment in Egypt.

 Backlog as of March 31st, 2011 is approximately LE 3 billion.

 Meters Segment
 Revenues from the meters segment reached LE 152 million in Q1 2011 versus LE 98 million in Q1 2010. Gross
 profit however reached LE 34 Million in Q1 2011 an increase of 123% from Q1 2010 which stood at LE 15
 million. Our main markets in this segment still continue to be Germany, 17%, Slovenia 9%, Poland 8%, other
 Europe 29% and others 37% as a percentage of total revenues during Q1 2011.
