ended June 30th, 2014.
September 1st, 2014

       Cairo- Egypt.

               El Sewedy Electric Posts Q2 2014 Net Profit after Minority of EGP 110
               million, on Total Group Revenues of EGP 4.2 Billion. On a Normalized
               Basis, Q2 2014 Net Profit reached EGP 242 Million.

               Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire &
               Cable and Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced
               its consolidated financial results for H1 2014 ended June 30th, 2014.


           Q2 2014 versus Q2 2013

           •   Consolidated revenues in Q2 2014 increased by 2% to reach EGP 4.2 billion versus EGP
               4.1 billion in Q2 2013.
           •   Gross profit in Q2 2014 remains flat reaching EGP 685 million.

           • EBITDA in Q2 2014 decreased by 29% to reach EGP 287 million versus EGP 407
               million in Q2 2013 as a result of an impairment of EGP 132 million related to the Yemen
               cables factory. Normalized Q2 2014 EBITDA stands at EGP 420 million.

           • Net profit after Minority increased by 34% to reach EGP 110 million during Q2 2014
               versus EGP 82 million during Q2 2013. Normalized net profit after minority stands at
               EGP 242 million.
           H1 2014 versus H1 2013

          •    Consolidated revenues increased by 11% to reach EGP 8.5 billion for H1 2014, versus
               EGP 7.7 billion in H1 2013.
          •    Gross profit increased by 5% to reach EGP 1,298 million during H1 2014, versus EGP
               1,234 million in H1 2013.
          •    EBITDA decreased by 10% to reach EGP 628 million in H1 2014, versus EGP 701
               million in H1 2013. Normalized H1 2014 EBITDA stands at EGP 870 million

          • Net profit after Minority Interest increased by 49% reached EGP 215 million in H1 2014
               versus EGP 144 million for H1 2013. Normalized net profit after minority stands at EGP
               457 million.

           Segment Analysis Q2 2014 versus Q2 2013

           •   Revenues in Q2 2014 increased by 2% to reach EGP 4.2 billion versus EGP 4.1 billion in
               Q2 2013. The Wire and Cable segment achieved revenues of EGP 3,222 million in Q2
               2014, 3% higher than Q2 2013. Turnkey revenues in Q2 2014 were 4% lower than Q2
               2013 reaching EGP 486 million. Meters revenues decreased by 18% reaching EGP 192
               million, whilst transformers revenues increased by 41% reaching EGP 195 million.
               Electrical Products revenues decreased by 10% to reach EGP 73 million.
           •   Gross Profit remains flat at EGP 685 million in Q2 2014 versus EGP 684 million in Q2
               2013.The gross profit in the Wire and Cable segment increased by 10% in Q2 2014
               versus Q2 2013, (EGP 479 million versus EGP 437 million). Turnkey gross profit
               decreased by 38% in Q2 2014 to reach EGP 64 million versus EGP 103 million in Q2
               2013. The Meters division gross profit decreased by 11% in Q2 2014 to reach EGP 71
               million whilst the transformers division gross profit increased by 43% in Q2 2014 to
               reach EGP 47 million. Electrical products’ gross profit decreased by 26% in Q2 2014 to
               reach EGP 23 million.
           Segment Analysis H1 2014 versus H1 2013

           •   Revenue during H1 2014 increased by 11% versus H1 2013 reaching EGP 8.5 billion.
               The Wire and Cables revenues increased by 12% to reach EGP 6,664 million. Turnkey
               revenues increased by 18% during H1 2014 reaching EGP 1,088 million. Transformers
               revenue increased by 48% during H1 2014 reaching EGP 344 million, whilst Meters
               revenues decreased 34% to reach EGP 346 million. Electrical products’ revenues
               increased by 2% to reach EGP 149 million.

           •   Gross profit during H1 2014 witnessed a 5% increase to reach EGP 1,298 million. The
               Wire and Cables gross profit increased by 18% to reach EGP 932 million. Turnkey gross
               profit was 17% lower during H1 2014 reaching EGP 141 million. Transformers gross
               profit increased by 2% whilst meters gross profit decreased by 27% to reach EGP 119
               million. Electrical products’ revenues decreased by 6% to reach EGP 52 million.

Wires & Cables Segment
Wires & Cables segment revenues increased by 12% to reach EGP 6,664 million in H1 2014
versus EGP 5,943 million in H1 2013. Gross profit in H1 2014 increased by 18% to reach EGP
932 million versus EGP 790 million in H1 2013. Gross profit margin reached 14% in H1 2014
versus 13% in H1 2013.
In terms of volumes, the segment sold 87,448 tons of cables in H1 2014 versus 70,732 tons in H1
2013, an increase of 24%. There was also a 23% improvement in gross profit per ton, which
averaged EGP 8,099 in H1 2014 versus EGP 6,567 in H1 2013.
Volumes in the Egyptian operations were 30% higher in H1 2014 versus H1 2013 with the main
increase coming from exports, mainly to the Gulf in addition to a pickup in local sales. The
Algerian operations continued to improve, with volumes increasing 13% in H1 2014 versus H1
2013. Volumes in KSA and Qatar both witnessed double-digit growth y-o-y.
As for the Yemen cables operations, due to the ongoing challenging political environment in the
country, the decision was taken to impair the investment in the Yemeni subsidiary, which had a
cables capacity of 9,600 tons. Total impairment was EGP132 million, of which EGP 17 million
directly impacted the COGS. The balance of the impairment was related to fixed assets and
receivables.

   Turnkey Projects
   Turnkey revenues witnessed an 18% increase, reaching EGP 1,008 million in H1 2014 versus
   EGP 852 million during H1 2013. Gross profit decreased by 17% to reach EGP 141 million in H1
   2014 versus EGP 170 million in H1 2013, mainly as a result of a decrease in gross profit margin
   which declined from 20% in H1 2013 to 14% in H1 2014. The decline in gross profit margin is
   due to a change in the revenue mix, with a higher contribution this year of lower margin countries
   versus those of 2013, which were predominantly Sub Saharan Africa and the Gulf.
   Turnkey backlog as of June 30th, 2014 stands at EGP 4.1 billion. Of the backlog Egypt represents
   23%, Sub Saharan Africa 17% and the GCC and Middle East 57%.

   Meters Segment

   Revenues from the meters segment decreased by 34% in H1 2014 versus H1 2013 to reach EGP
   346 million versus EGP 524 million in H1 2013. Gross profit decreased by 27% to reach EGP
   119 million in Q1 2014 versus 164 million in H1 2013.
   Meters backlog as of June 30th, 2014 stands at Euros 27.4 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment increased 48% in H1 2014 versus H1 2013 to reach
EGP 344 million, whilst gross profit increased by 2% to reach EGP 55 million in H1 2014.

The strongest performance in this segment was from the Egyptian operations in which both
revenues and gross profit witnessed double-digit growth, as was the case for Sudan.

As at June 30th, 2014 the backlog in the transformers segment stood at USD 40 million.

Other Electrical Products
Revenues from the Electrical products segment increased by 2% in H1 2014 versus H1 2013, to
reach EGP 149 million, while gross profit decreased by 6 % to reach EGP 52 million.

Net Debt
Net debt as of June 30th, 2014 increase to reach EGP 3,327 million versus EGP 3,185 million as
of March 31st, 2014. The main reason for the increase in net debt was a result of an increase in
Inventory, which reached EGP 4 billion as of June 30th, 2014 (105 DOH) versus EGP 3.2 billion
as of March 31st (85 DOH). Account receivables reached EGP 4.4 billion as of June 30th (100
DOH) down from EGP 4.5 billion as of March 31st (103 DOH). Payables remain unchanged at
EGP 1.5 billion as of June 30th 2014 (39 DOH). Total cash conversion cycle increased to 166
DOH as of June 30th 2014 versus 149 DOH as of March 31st 2014.
