ELSEWEDY ELECTRIC (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wires & Cables and Integrated Energy
Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announces its consolidated financial results for the nine‐month period
ending 30 September 2019, reporting revenues of EGP 34,546.5 million, up 14% year‐on‐year. The company’s net profit
after minority interest fell by 22% to EGP 2,717.2 million, representing a net profit margin of 7.9%.

Financial Highlights
       Revenues rose 14.0% y‐o‐y to EGP 34,546.5 million in 9M 2019. Although revenues from the core wires and
        cables segment fell by 3.7% year‐on‐year, overall top line growth was driven primarily by the turnkey projects
        segment, the second‐largest contributor to revenue, which grew by 55.7% y‐o‐y.
       Gross Profit saw a 2.2% y‐o‐y increase to EGP 5,297.3 million in 9M 2019. The company’s gross profit margin
        normalized from 17.1% in 2018 to 15.3% in 2019.

       EBITDA posted a 5.6% y‐o‐y decrease to EGP 4,081.5 million in 9M 2019, yielding an EBITDA margin of 11.8%
        compared to 14.3% in 9M 2018.
       Net Profit after Minority Interest recorded EGP 2,717.2 million in 9M 2019 compared to EGP 3,503.5 million
        posted in the same period last year, representing a 22.4% y‐o‐y decline. Meanwhile, the net profit margin stood

Note from Our CEO
As we near the conclusion of 2019, ElSewedy Electric can reflect on a year that has brought its fair share of both growth
and challenges. The first nine months of the year saw dynamic growth in the company’s turnkey segment, driving an
overall increase in our top line and helping consolidate our position as an industry leader, not solely in our core Egyptian
market but across our expanding geographic footprint. Developments at our turnkey segment have proven especially
encouraging in this regard, with several EPC projects being rolled out across Egypt, the GCC and our new market of
Tanzania providing a dynamic source of new value. Meanwhile, stable performance at our wires and cables segment
proves that our core business provides a continued solid basis for sustainable value creation over the long term. These
developments have driven y‐o‐y top‐line growth of 14%, with the company registering revenues of EGP 34.6 billion for
the nine‐month period.

ElSewedy continued to execute several high‐revenue projects during the period. The company’s focus remains on
markets requiring special investment in infrastructure projects, including Egypt, African countries, and the GCC area,
where we continue to build on our expertise in construction and operation of power generation, transmission and
distribution, and water projects. Our pioneering hydroelectric dam and power station project in Tanzania has moved
beyond the mobilization phase and we are on track to achieve the set milestones as planned. We are also pleased with
the progress at our Solar PV project at Benban, Egypt, which reached commercial operation during the third quarter of
2019. The plant has been connected to the grid, yielding a new source of recurring income for the coming 25 years. The
quarter also saw our Egyptian transmission and distribution arm secure two new contracts valued at EGP 635 million,
and we are confident that these contracts and others will continue to drive ElSewedy’s expansion over the coming
years.

Ahmed El Sewedy

Revenue Analysis by Segment
El Sewedy’s wires and cables business remained the company’s primary contributor to total revenues during 9M 2019,
with its contribution standing at 48.9% for the period, down 8.9% y‐o‐y from 57.8% in 9M 2018. Parallel to this decrease,
the turnkey projects segment’s top line contribution jumped to 38.5% in 9M 2019 against 28.2% in 9M 2018, a 10.3%
point expansion.

Meanwhile, 9M 2019 saw the contribution from the meters and transformers segments decrease by 0.8 and 0.3
percentage points to record 7.3% and 3.7%, respectively. The other electrical products segment saw its contribution fall
by 0.2 percentage points to 1.7% in 9M 2019 verus 1.9% in 9M 2018, remaining the lowest contributor to overall
revenue.

Revenue from El Sewedy’s wires and cables segment fell by 3.7% y‐o‐y to EGP 16,878.8 million in 9M 2019, while revenues
from the turnkey projects segment recorded an impressive 55.7% y‐o‐y increase to EGP 13,297.0 million in 9M 2019 from
EGP 8,541.0 million one year previously.

Revenues from the meters segment grew by 3.0% y‐o‐y to reach EGP 2,519.8 million in 9M 2019. Transformers followed
suit, with segment revenues climbing 4.1% y‐o‐y to EGP 1,275.8 million in 9M 2019. Meanwhile, revenue from other
electrical products contracted by a slight 0.8% y‐o‐y, recording EGP 575.1 million in 9M 2019.

Gross Profit Analysis by Segment
Consolidated gross profit for 9M 2019 came to EGP 5,297.3 million, representing a 2.2% y‐o‐y increase from its 9M 2018
level of EGP 5,182.4 million. At 38.3%, ElSewedy’s wires and cables segment remains the company’s single largest
generator of gross profit, although its margin of dominance has declined compared to previous periods, with gross profit
from the segment contracting by 9.8% y‐o‐y to record EGP 2,031.5 million in 9M 2019. The turnkey projects segment’s
gross profit contribution climbed to 37.2% in 9M 2019 due to an 18.7% y‐o‐y increase in segmental gross profit to EGP
1,969.0 million against EGP 1,659.0 million in 9M 2018.

The meters segment recorded a gross profit of EGP 662.0 million in 9M 2019, up 4.7% y‐o‐y, with its contribution to total
gross profit relatively stable at 12.5% in 9M 2019 against 12.2% in 9M 2018. 9M 2019 saw gross profit from the
transformers segment increase by 20.6% y‐o‐y to EGP 396.6 million, contributing 7.5% of consolidated gross profit
against 6.3% one year previously. Meanwhile, gross profit from other electrical products fell by 23.0% y‐o‐y to EGP 238.2
million in 9M 2019, contributing 4.5% of total gross profit for the period.

About Elsewedy Electric
Established in 1938 by the Elsewedy family, Elsewedy Electric has since grown to become a regional and tactically global
integrated cables and electrical products manufacturer and a turn‐key services provider with more than 14,000
employees working at 22 production facilities in 15 countries. Structured to face the challenges of the world’s fastest
growing markets and the complexity of the critical electricity industry, Elsewedy Electric capitalizes on its deep product
and sector specialization and unprecedented insight into local markets. www.elsewedyelectric.com
