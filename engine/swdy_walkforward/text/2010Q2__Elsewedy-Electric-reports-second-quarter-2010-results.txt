         EARNINGS RELEASE: ELSEWEDY ELECTRIC REPORTS SECOND QUARTER 2010 RESULTS
        22nd August, Cairo, Egypt



        ELSEWEDY ELECTRIC REPORTS RECORD REVENUES AND OPERATING PROFITS IN THE SECOND
        QUARTER OF 2010. RECOVERY FROM SLOW 2009 IS ON TRACK

        Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange) the leading wire & cable and
        integrated energy solution provider in the middle east and Africa , announced this week it’s
        consolidated financial results for the second quarter of 2010 .

               •    Net revenues of LE 5,985 million for the 6 months ending June 30th 2010, 29% higher than
                    the same period of 2009. Revenues for the three months ending June 30th 2010 was LE
                    3,052 million , 4% higher than the first three months of 2010 and 37% higher than the
                    second quarter of 2009.
               •    Gross profit was LE 1,005 million in the six months ending June 30th 2010, up 39% from the
                    same period of 2009, while for the second quarter gross profit was LE 516 million up 7%
                    from first quarter of 2010.
               •    Net profit of LE 507 million for the 6 months ending June 30th 2010, 37% higher than the
                    same period last year. Net profit for the three months ending June 30th 2010 was LE 256
                    million, 2% higher than the first three months of 2010 and 45% higher than the second
                    quarter of 2009.

        WIRE & CABLE SEGMENT
        Revenues from the wire & cable segment were LE 2,423 million in the second quarter of 2010 Vs.
        LE 2,450 million in the first quarter, a drop of 1%.

        In terms of volumes, the segment sold 41,121 tons of cables in the second quarter vs. 39,928 in the
        first 3 months, a growth of 3%. For the first six month of 2010, sales were 81,049 tons Vs. 61,347
        tons in the first 6 months of 2009, a growth of 32%. This increase in volumes is mainly driven by our
        new operations in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Yemen and Ethiopia

        Despite the competition in the wire & cable business worldwide and especially in the GCC,
        Elsewedy Cables managed to increase the volume of cables sold by 32% YoY and to maintain its
        gross profit margin at 14% during the second quarter due to the geographical diversification.

        TURNKEY PROJECTS
        Revenues from Turnkey and contracting business came at LE 333 million for the second quarter of
        2010 Vs. LE 220 million in the first quarter. Gross profit for the second quarter was LE 88 Million Vs,
        LE 67 million in the first quarter, up 31%.

        Gross Profit margin for second quarter was 26% down from 31% during the first quarter of 2010.

        Based on the back log currently on hand and the execution schedule , we expect the revenues for
        the full year to be between LE 1,400 and LE 1,600 million , and gross profit margin for the full year
        is expected to be in the range of 20-25%. Backlog as of the end of the second quarter was US$ 510
        million.

        METERS SEGMENT
        Revenues from the meters segment in the second quarter were LE 124 million Vs. LE 98 million in
        the first quarter, up 27%.

        Gross profit was LE 42 million in first six months of 2010 , representing 19% of sales, Improvement
        in sales figures is expected in the second half of this year leading to annual turnover of about LE
        580 million ( assuming exchange rate of Euro\LE 7.3).

        With the new management of Iskraemco in place moving ahead on the restructuring of company we
        expect to see positive results coming from the meters division by 2011.

        TRANSFORMERS SEGMENT
        Revenues from the transformers segment came at LE 89.6 Million in Q2 2010, down 7% from Q1.
        Gross profit margin for the second quarter was 21.3% down from 22.3 % during the first quarter
        2010. This drop was mainly due to the lower sales volumes and the higher depreciation expenses of
        the second quarter. EBITDA margin stood at 18% the same level of the first quarter of 2010 and
        came 8% higher than the same quarter of last year.

        WIND ENERGY SEGMENT
        There is almost no contribution to the top line of Elsewedy as yet from the Wind Energy segment.

        After the acquisition of an additional 60% of the shares of M.Torres in Spain in January 2010, and
        becoming 90% shareholder of the company, Elsewedy Cables have been very active to participate
        in Wind Energy projects around the world, focusing mainly on countries where we have strong
        presence.

        Elsewedy participation would be in the form of being a main developer, a co-developer for wind
        farms, an EPC (Engineering, Procurement and construction) provider or simply a supplier of turbines
        and towers.

        The Wind energy segment is faced with a challenge of the long cycle required to develop a wind
        farm in addition to the strong competition caused by the slowdown in the wind sector market
        especially in Europe.

        As previously announced, Elsewedy Cables was awarded an EPC project in North Africa to develop
        a 60 Mega Watt wind farm , to be executed during the fourth quarter of 2010 and 2011. Wind
        Turbines for that project are in stock, pending site preparation and other logistical issues to be
        resolved. Elsewedy also signed a contract for the supply of 22 turbines in Spain, to be delivered by
        the end of 2011.

        Elsewedy is currently working on getting orders from several projects in Africa, Pakistan, Latin
        America and East Europe. We believe that we will be able to receive two more orders before the
        end of the year, for deliveries in 2011 and 2012

        QATAR OWNERSHIP RE-STRUCTURING.
        Elsewedy sold 24.5% of its share in the trading company in Qatar “Elsewedy Cables – Qatar” the
        transaction resulted in a net profit of LE 73 million that was booked as Income from Equity securities
        in the second quarter.

        As a result the direct and indirect owner ship of Elsewedy in the cable factory in Qatar “Doha
        Cables” was reduced from 48.6% to 45.6%

         Elsewedy still maintains management control over the two entities.
