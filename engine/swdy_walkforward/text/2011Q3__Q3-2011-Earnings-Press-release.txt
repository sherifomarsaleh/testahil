Earnings Release: El Sewedy Electric Reports Consolidated Results for 9M-2011, ended
September 30th, 2011.

December 1st, 2011

Cairo -Egypt.




        El Sewedy Electric posts double digit growth in EBITDA and Net Profit, achieving
        10% and 22% growth respectively in Q3 2011 versus Q2 2011. Net Income in Q3 2011
        reached EGP 196 million, 22% higher than Q2 2011. 9 M 2011 Revenues and EBITDA
        23% and 7% higher than 9M 2010.


El Sewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange) the leading Wire & Cable and Integrated
Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced its consolidated financial results for the
nine months ended September 30th, 2011.

    Q3 2011 versus Q2 2011

   •    Consolidated Revenues remained flat to reach EGP 3,883 million for Q3 2011, versus LE 3,933 in Q2
        2011.
    •   Gross Profit increased by 5% to reach EGP 563 million during Q3 2011, versus EGP 534 million in Q2
        2011.
    •   EBITDA increased by 10% to reach EGP 441 million in Q3 2011, versus EGP 401 million in Q2 2011.

    • Net Profit after Minority Interest increased 22% to reach EGP 196 million in Q3 2011 versus EGP 161
        million for Q2 2011.
    9M 2011 versus 9M 2010

    •   Revenues in 9M 2011 increased by 23% to reach EGP 11.4 billion versus EGP 9.2 billion in 9M 2010,
        whilst Gross Profit increased by 3% to reach EGP 1.55 billion in 9M 2011 versus EGP 1.49 billion in
        9M 2010.

    • EBITDA in 9M 2011 increased by 7% to reach EGP 1.178 billion versus EGP 1.105 billion in 9M
        2010.

    • Net Profit after Minority reached EGP 528 million during 9M 2011 versus EGP 698 million during 9M
        2010.

   •   The third quarter saw a very strong performance in the Turnkey Segment with both revenues and gross
       profit increasing 42% and 67% from the previous quarter. It was a challenging quarter for the Wire and
       Cables segment with revenues and gross profit 8% and 31% lower than the previous quarter. Gross
       Profit during Q3 2011 in the transformers segment was significantly higher than Q2 2011. Whilst in the
       Meters Gross profit was 8% lower than the previous quarter.
   •   EBITDA during Q3 2011 was 10% higher than the previous quarter. Selling, general and
       Administrative Expenses were 7 % lower in Q3 2011 versus Q2 2011 and SG&A/Sales reached 5.4%
       in Q3 2011 versus 5.7% in Q2 2011.
   •   Net Profit After Minority increased 22% to reach EGP 196 million in Q3 2011 versus EGP 161 million
       in Q2 2011.
   •   Revenues in 9M 2011 increased by 23% to reach EGP 11.4 billion versus EGP 9.2 billion in 9M 2010.
       Wire and Cable Revenues were 15% higher in the 9M 2011 versus 9M 2010 reaching EGP 8.5 billion.
       Turnkey Revenues in 9M 2011 were 95% higher than 9M 2010, whilst Meters and Transformers
       revenues were also 5% and 65% higher respectively YoY. Electrical Products revenues were 13%
       lower in 9M 2011 versus 9M 2010.
   •   Gross Profit also increased by 3% to reach EGP 1.55 billion in 9M 2011 versus EGP 1.49 billion in 9M
       2010. The Gross Profit in the Wire and Cable segment was 21% lower in 9M 2011 versus 9M 2010,
       (EGP 785 million versus EGP 996 million). However the other segments performed extremely well
       with the Turnkey Gross Profit increasing 83% in 9M 2011 to reach a Gross Profit of EGP 484 million
       versus EGP 264 million in 9M 2010. The Meters division achieved a 14 % increase in gross profit in
       9M 2011 versus 9M 2010 as well as the transformers division which increased 84% in 9M 2011 to
       reach EGP 90 million. Electrical products’ Gross Profit reached EGP 86 million in 9M 2011, 11%
       lower than 9M 2010.
   •   EBITDA for 9M 2011 stood at EGP 1.18 billion, 7% higher than 9M 2010. However adjusting 9M
       2010 EBITDA for the one-off gain of EGP 73 million related to the partial sale of Qatar Cables,
       EBITDA during 9M 2011 is actually 14% higher than 9M 2010.

Wires & Cables Segment
Wires & Cables segment revenues decreased by 8% to reach EGP 2,743 million in Q3 2011 versus EGP 2,992
million in Q2 2011. Gross profit in Q3 2011 decreased 31% to reach EGP 208 million versus EGP 302 million
in Q2 2011.

In terms of volumes, the segment sold 34,957 tons of cables in Q3 2011 versus 39,967 in Q2 2011, a decrease
of 13%. Gross profit per ton decreased 14% in Q2 2011 to reach EGP 4,849 per ton whilst for the 9M 2011
gross profit per ton stands at EGP 5,339.

Q3 2011 volumes were lower as a result of weaker demand in the Gulf region and Egypt as a result of the
Ramadan and Eid season. Also for Egypt specifically the reduction in volumes was due to a continuation of a
subdued private sector as a result of the ongoing political issues which continued to dominate Egypt during Q3
2011. In addition the drop in the copper price during Q3 2011 put slight pressure in the demand as some clients
                                                            4
tend to wait and see if further drops will occur. The lower volumes in Q3 2011 put pressure on the Gross profit
per ton as with the same overheads and lower quantities the cost/ton component of the cables increased.

As of September 30th, 2011 we have the highest cables backlog in our history of approximately 92,000 tons
geographically diversified in the Gulf, Egypt, Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

Turnkey Projects
The Turnkey and Contracting business recorded a stellar performance this quarter with revenues increasing 42%
to reach EGP 792 million in Q3 2011 versus EGP 557 million in Q2 2011. Gross profit in Q3 2011 was EGP
244 million versus EGP 146 million in Q2 2011, an increase of 67%. Gross Profit Margin improved to 31% in
Q3 2011 versus 26% in Q2 2011.

Revenues in 9M 2011 increased 95% to reach EGP 1.8 billion versus EGP 963 million in 9M 2010, whilst
Gross Profit increased 83% in 9M 2011 to reach a Gross Profit of EGP 484 million versus EGP 264 million in
9M 2010.

The strong performance in this segment continues to be part of the Group’s strategy to leverage on all of its
products and present a value added service that enhances the overall profitability of the Group. We have seen a
significant increase in the backlog number from the previous quarter which as of September 30th 2011 stands at
EGP 6 billion (includes EGP 2 billion of high voltage cables not included previously in the Turnkey backlog
number.) The backlog number includes projects in Egypt, GCC, Middle East and Africa for power generation,
distribution and transmission lines.

Meters Segment
Revenues from the meters segment reached EGP 124 million in Q3 2011 versus EGP 160 million in Q2 2011.
Gross profit reached EGP 32 Million in Q3 2011, 8% lower than Q2 2011. However, compared to 9M 2010,
revenues increased 5% to reach EGP 436 million whilst gross profit increased 14% to reach EGP 99 million in
9M 2011.

The main reason for the drop in revenues during Q3 2011 versus Q2 2011 was due to a change in both the
product mix and the geographical diversification. In terms of meters sold quantities remained flat in Q3 2011
versus Q2 2011. Gross profit margin improved to 25.6% in Q3 2011 versus 21.4% in Q2 2011 and also
improved to reach 22.9% for the 9M 2011 versus 21.2% for 9M 2010.

 Our main markets in this segment continue to be Germany, 19%, Netherlands 9%, Poland 10%, other Europe
30% and others 32% as a percentage of total revenues during 9M 2011. For the 9M 2011 the revenue
contribution from the different products is 21% electromechanical meters, 35% residential electronic meters,
31% system meters and approximately 11% Industrial Electronic meters.

 Backlog as at September 30th 2011 reached Euros 21 million, up 46% from June 30th, 2011 backlog which
stood at Euros 13 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment remains flat at EGP 154 million during Q3 2011 versus EGP 155
million in Q2 2011. Gross profit in Q3 2011 surged by 95% to reach EGP 45 million versus EGP 23 million in
Q2 2011.

During 9M 2011, Revenues increased 65% to reach EGP 426 million versus EGP 257 million during 9M 2010.
Gross Profit increased 84% to reach EGP 90 million versus 49 million in 9M 2010. Egypt, Sudan and Nigeria
operations performed extremely well this quarter versus the previous quarter. Gross profit margins in Egypt and
Sudan saw a marked improvement, whilst quantities in Nigeria improved from the previous quarter.
