Earnings Release: Elsewedy Electric Reports Consolidated Results for
Full year ended December 31st, 2012.

April 2nd, 2013

Cairo- Egypt.

Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire & Cable
and Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced its
consolidated financial results for the full year ended December 31st, 2012.

     Q4 2012 versus Q4 2011

      Consolidated Revenues in Q4 2012 increased by 7% to reach EGP 4.1 billion versus
        EGP 3.8 billion in Q4 2011.
      Gross Profit in Q4 2012 increased by 29% to reach EGP 473 million versus EGP 366
        million in Q4 2011.

      EBITDA in Q4 2012 decreased by 1% to reach EGP 205 million versus EGP 207
         million in Q4 2011.

      Net Loss after Minority reached EGP (- 49) million during Q4 2012 versus EGP (-19)
         million during Q4 2011.


     FY 2012 versus FY 2011

     Consolidated Revenues decreased by 4% to reach EGP 14.5 billion for FY 2012,
      versus EGP 15.2 billion in FY 2011.
      Gross Profit decreased by 4% to reach EGP 1,840 million during FY 2012, versus
        EGP 1,911 million in FY 2011.
      EBITDA decreased by 22% to reach EGP 1,074 million in FY 2012, versus EGP
        1,385 million in FY 2011.
      Net Profit after Minority Interest reached EGP 117 million in FY 2012 versus EGP
        509 million for FY 2011.

               Q4 2012 versus Q42011

                Revenues in Q4 2012 increased by 7% to reach EGP 4.1 billion versus EGP 3.8
                  billion in Q4 2011. The Wire and Cable segment achieved revenues of EGP 3.1
                  billion in Q4 2012, 7% higher than Q4 2011. Turnkey Revenues in Q4 2012 were
                  7% lower than Q4 2011 reaching EGP 607 million. Meters revenues increased by
                  15% reaching EGP 223 million. Both Transformers and Electrical Products revenues
                  increased by 70% and 60% YoY respectively.
                Gross Profit increased by 29% to reach EGP 473 million in Q4 2012 versus EGP 366
                  million in Q4 2011.The Gross Profit in the Wire and Cable segment was 3% higher
                  in Q4 2012 versus Q4 2011, (EGP 226 million versus EGP 218 million). Turnkey
                  Gross Profit increased by 59% in Q4 2012 to reach a Gross Profit of EGP 136
                  million versus EGP 85 million in Q4 2011. The Meters division achieved an 83%
                  increase in gross profit in Q4 2012 versus Q4 2011, reaching EGP 73 million. The
                  transformers division Gross Profit increased 82% in Q4 2012 to reach EGP 12
                  million. Electrical products’ gross profit reached EGP 27 million in Q4 2012, 64%
                  higher than Q4 2011.

                Net Loss after Minority reached EGP (-49) million during Q4 2012 versus EGP (-19)

               FY 2012 versus FY 2011
                Revenue during FY 2012 was 4% lower versus FY 2011, and the Gross Profit
                  witnessed a 4% decrease to reach EGP 1.84 billion. The Wire and Cables Revenues
                  decreased by 2% to reach EGP 11.1 billion, whilst the Gross profit increased by 5%
                  to reach EGP 1,056 million. Turnkey revenues were 26% lower, whilst turnkey gross
                  profit decreased by 31%. Transformers revenue was 19% lower during FY 2012. The
                  segment that over-performed during FY 2012 versus FY 2011 was the meters
                  revenues and gross profit, which increased 33% and 62% respectively.
                EBITDA during FY 2012 was 22% lower to reach EGP 1,074 million versus EGP
                  1,385 million. The main reason for the lower EBITDA was a result of both an 8%

Commenting on the FY 2012 results, Ahmed El Sewedy C.E.O. of Elsewedy Electric stated:
“I would consider 2012 to be one of the most challenging years in the history of the
Company. The impact of the revolutions in Egypt, Syria, and Libya was felt in 2012 as during
2011 we were basically operating on the pre-revolution backlog. Our main segment, which
continues to be Wire and Cables performed reasonably well on a Gross profit level in 2012.
Algeria is performing extremely well and we continued to also see positive performance in
Saudi Arabia. The Saudi government are committed to triple the installed capacity and have
recently issued a tender for 250,000 tons of cables which is the largest tender issued in many
years estimated to be in the range of SAR 15-20 billion.
 It was a difficult year for the Turnkey, however we have opened up new markets such as
Malawi, Liberia, South Sudan, Congo DRC and Chad, amongst others, which we expect to
materialize into contracts. We also were awarded a power generation EPC contract in Iraq
for a 500MW project. We currently have contracts in Iraq in the range of US$250M.
As for the meters we spent the last few years investing in R&D to develop new products and
software applications including a new industrial meter. This segment performed well in 2012
and we expect continued strong performance in this segment during 2013.

Another important market for us is Libya which in 2012 we saw very little demand however
we believe it will pickup in 2013. We were able to send the second shipment of wind turbines
to Libya during the fourth quarter of 2012.
We made a lot of tough decisions in 2012 which we are confident will pay off in 2013, mainly
a number of changes in the management of some of the key operations. In addition we
converted a portion of the debt in Egypt, which was in foreign currency to EGP debt to match
the revenues in local currency. Whilst devaluation is positive for Elsewedy Electric we
preferred to manage our currency exposure proactively thus reducing the impact of any
devaluation of the currency in the form of foreign exchange losses.
We are more optimistic on 2013, even taking into account the fact that demand in Egypt may
not pick up in 2013. We have a strong backlog, which gives us good visibility on 2013”.

Wires & Cables Segment
Wires & Cables segment revenues increased by 7% to reach EGP 3,051 million in Q4 2012
versus EGP 2,852 million in Q4 2011. Gross profit in Q4 2012 however increased by 3% to
reach EGP 226 million versus EGP 218 million in Q4 2011. In terms of volumes, the segment
sold 44,732 tons of cables in Q4 2012 versus 41,899 tons in Q4 2011, an increase of 7%.
Comparing FY 2012 versus FY 2011, volumes increased 5% to reach 164,554 tons. The
operations in Qatar, Algeria and Saudi Arabia continue to be the main contributors to the
growth in volumes. Gross profit increased 5% to reach EGP 1,056 million in FY 2012.
Gross profit per ton in 2012 averaged EGP 4,933 per ton versus EGP 5,271 per ton in FY
2011. The market that was most affected in terms of a drop in gross profit per ton in FY 2012
versus FY 2011 was Egypt. Whilst overall volumes in the Egyptian operations were flat in
2012 the market composition changed which resulted in the drop of GP/ton. Overall tons sold
in the local market, which is amongst the higher margin business, declined by 25% with the
largest drop in volumes coming from the sales to the Utilities, which declined by 50% in
2012.

Turnkey Projects
Turnkey revenues witnessed a 7% decline, reaching EGP 607 million in Q4 2012 versus EGP
650 million during Q4 2011. Gross profit surged 59% to reach EGP 136 million in Q4 2012
versus EGP 85 million in Q4 2011.
Overall revenues declined by 26% whilst gross profit declined by 31% to reach EGP 391.7
million in FY 2012. Whilst revenues related to the supply and installation of HV cables,
mainly in the Gulf area remained relatively flat in FY 2012, the revenues from the turnkey
operations in Egypt and Africa were lower. Given the political and economic climate in
Egypt during 2012, there was a significant reduction in the execution of projects, with gross
profit related to the Egyptian projects declining by 30%. As for the projects in Africa, whilst
2011 was a very strong year in terms of project execution, 2012 was much weaker given the
delay in the commencement of new projects.
A portion of the Libya wind project was recognized in Q4 2012 on the back of the second
shipment of wind turbines from Spain.

Meters Segment
Revenues from the meters segment increased by 15% in Q4 2012 versus Q4 2011 to reach
EGP 223 million versus EGP 194 million in Q4 2011. Gross profit surged by 83% to reach
EGP 73 million in Q4 2012.
For FY 2012 revenues in the meters increased 33%, whilst Gross profit increased 62% to
reach EGP 226 million. Gross profit margin improved to 27% in FY 2012 versus 22.2% in
FY 2011.The improvement in gross profit margin is a combination of a revenue shift to
markets with better profitability, in addition to the Company phasing out the
electromechanical meters.
Meters backlog as of December 31st, 2012 stands at Euros 27.5 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment increase 70% in Q4 2012 versus Q4 2011 to reach
EGP 96 million, whilst gross profit increased by 83% to reach EGP 12 million in Q4 2012.
The main contributors to the improvement in revenues during Q4 2012 were from the
Nigerian and Sudanese operations.

Overall FY 2012 revenues were disappointing with an overall decline of 19% versus FY
2011. Gross profit fell 38% to reach EGP 60.2 million. All the transformers operations
underperformed during FY 2012 with the exception of Zambia. The Egypt operations also
suffered as a result of production delays and incurred late penalty fees, which impacted the
operations. This was as a result of the frequent port closures that took place during 2012.

As at December 31st, 2012 the backlog in the transformers segment stood at USD 48.4
million.
