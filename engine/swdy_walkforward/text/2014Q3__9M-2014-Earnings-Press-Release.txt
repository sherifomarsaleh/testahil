Earnings Release: Elsewedy Electric Reports Consolidated Results for 9M 2014
ended September 30th, 2014.
December 1st, 2014

Cairo- Egypt.

        El Sewedy Electric Posts Q3 2014 Net Profit after Minority of EGP 95
        million, on Total Group Revenues of EGP 4.3 Billion. On a Normalized
        Basis, Q3 2014 Net Profit reached EGP 220 Million.

        Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire &
        Cable and Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced
        its consolidated financial results for 9M 2014 ended September 30th, 2014.


    Q3 2014 versus Q3 2013

    •   Consolidated revenues in Q3 2014 increased by 21% to reach EGP 4.3 billion versus
        EGP 3.5 billion in Q3 2013.
    •   Gross profit in Q3 2014 increased by 55% to reach EGP 687 million versus EGP 443
        million in Q3 2013.

    • EBITDA in Q3 2014 increased by 4% to reach EGP 393 million versus EGP 379 million
        in Q3 2013 as a result of an impairment of EGP 124 million related to MTOI, the Spanish
        wind turbines factory. Normalized Q3 2014 EBITDA stands at EGP 518 million.

    • Net profit after Minority reached EGP 95 million during Q3 2014 versus EGP 46 million
        during Q3 2013. Normalized net profit after minority stands at EGP 220 million.
    9M 2014 versus 9M 2013

   •    Consolidated revenues increased by 14% to reach EGP 12.8 billion for 9M 2014, versus
        EGP 11.2 billion in 9M 2013.
   •    Gross profit increased by 18% to reach EGP 1,985 million during 9M 2014, versus EGP
        1,677 million in 9M 2013.
   •    EBITDA decreased by 6% to reach EGP 1,021 million in 9M 2014, versus EGP 1,080
        million in 9M 2013. Normalized 9M 2014 EBITDA stands at EGP 1,388 million.

   • Net profit after Minority Interest increased by 63% reaching EGP 310 million in 9M 2014
        versus EGP 190 million for 9M 2013. Normalized net profit after minority stands at EGP
        678 million.

    Segment Analysis Q3 2014 versus Q3 2013

    •   Revenues in Q3 2014 increased by 21% to reach EGP 4.3 billion versus EGP 3.5 billion
        in Q3 2013. The Wire and Cable segment achieved revenues of EGP 2,844 million in Q3
        2014, 1% lower than Q3 2013. Turnkey revenues in Q3 2014 were 259% higher than Q3
        2013 reaching EGP 1,112 million. Meters revenues decreased by 28% reaching EGP 151
        million, whilst transformers revenues increased by 17% reaching EGP 121 million.
        Electrical Products revenues increased by 19% to reach EGP 75 million.
    •   Gross Profit increased by 55% to reach EGP 687 million in Q3 2014 versus EGP 443
        million in Q3 2013.The gross profit in the Wire and Cable segment increased by 67% in
        Q3 2014 versus Q3 2013, (EGP 462 million versus EGP 276 million). Turnkey gross
        profit surged by 129% in Q3 2014 to reach EGP 145 million versus EGP 64 million in
        Q3 2013. The Meters division gross profit increased by 9% in Q3 2014 to reach EGP 59
        million whilst the transformers division gross profit increased by 26% in Q3 2014 to
        reach EGP 31 million. Electrical products’ (includes wind) gross profit stood at EGP -11
        million in Q3 2014.
    Segment Analysis 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013

    •   Revenue during 9M 2014 increased by 14% versus 9M 2013 reaching EGP 12.8 billion.
        The Wire and Cables revenues increased by 8% to reach EGP 9,508 million. Turnkey
        revenues increased by 82% during 9M 2014 reaching EGP 2,120 million. Transformers
        revenue increased by 38% during 9M 2014 reaching EGP 465 million, whilst Meters
        revenues decreased 32% to reach EGP 496 million. Electrical products’ revenues
        increased by 7% to reach EGP 223 million.

    •   Gross profit during 9M 2014 witnessed an 18% increase to reach EGP 1,985 million. The
        Wire and Cables gross profit increased by 31% to reach EGP 1,394 million. Turnkey
        gross profit was 22% higher during 9M 2014 reaching EGP 286 million. Transformers
        gross profit increased by 10% whilst meters gross profit decreased by 18% to reach EGP
        177 million. Electrical products’ (including wind) gross profit decreased by 49% to reach
        EGP 41 million.

   Wires & Cables Segment
   Wires & Cables segment revenues increased by 8% to reach EGP 9,508 million in 9M 2014
   versus EGP 8,801 million in 9M 2013. Gross profit in 9M 2014 increased by 31% to reach EGP
   1,394 million versus EGP 1,067 million in 9M 2013. Gross profit margin reached 14.7% in 9M
   2014 versus 12.1% in 9M 2013.
   In terms of volumes, the segment sold 134,096 tons of cables in 9M 2014 versus 118,494 tons in
   9M 2013, an increase of 13%. There was also a 40% improvement in gross profit per ton, which
   averaged EGP 8,809 in 9M 2014 versus EGP 6,275 in 9M 2013.
   Volumes in the Egyptian operations were 18% higher in 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013 with the main
   increase coming from the local market. Exports from Egypt were also higher with the largest
   increase coming from exports to the GCC. The Algerian operations continued to improve, with
   volumes increasing 20% in 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013. Volumes in KSA were 11% higher for the
   9M 2014, whilst Doha Cables’ volumes was 4% higher.

Turnkey Projects
Turnkey revenues witnessed an 82% increase, reaching EGP 2,120 million in 9M 2014 versus
EGP 1,162 million during 9M 2013. Gross profit increased by 22% to reach EGP 286 million in
9M 2014 versus EGP 234 million in 9M 2013, Gross profit margin declined from 20% in 9M
2013 to 13% in 9M 2014.
The substantial increase in revenues in Q3 2014 is mainly due to a substantial pickup in project
execution in Kuwait in addition to booking the remaining portion related to the supply for the
Libya wind project.
Turnkey backlog as of September 30th, 2014 stands at EGP 4.2 billion. Of the backlog Egypt
represents 23%, Sub Saharan Africa 19% and the GCC and Middle East 56%.

Meters Segment

Revenues from the meters segment decreased by 32% in 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013 to reach EGP
496 million versus EGP 731 million in 9M 2013. Gross profit decreased by 18% to reach EGP
177 million in 9M 2014 versus 217 million in 9M 2013.
Meters backlog as of September 30th, 2014 stands at Euros 21 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment increased 38% in 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013 to reach
EGP 465 million, whilst gross profit increased by 10% to reach EGP 86 million in 9M 2014.

The Egyptian operations continues to do well with both revenues and gross profit witnessing
double-digit growth for the 9M 2014. Revenues in Sudan were also 85% higher in 9M 2014
versus 9M 2013. Zambia revenues were slightly lower for the 9M 2014.

As at September 30th, 2014 the backlog in the transformers segment stood at USD 40 million.

Other Electrical Products
Revenues from the Electrical products segment increased by 7% in 9M 2014 versus 9M 2013, to
reach EGP 223 million, whilst gross profit decreased by 49 % to reach EGP 41 million. The
COGS in the other electrical products includes an additional EGP 59 million in Q3 2014 related
to the impairment of inventory in MTOI.

Selling General and Administrative Expenses
There was a significant decline in SG&A in Q3 2014, which reached EGP 194 million mainly due
to the reclassification of EGP 53 million in one of the subsidiaries from SG&A to other operating
expenses.

Provisions
There was an additional provision in Q3 2014 of EGP 83 million for LG’s issued related to the
Libya wind project.

Taxes
Given the new tax law related to dividends that was issued recently, the Company provisioned for
an additional EGP 54 million of taxes (deferred taxes) in Q3 2014.

Net Debt
Net debt as of September 30th, 2014 remains flat at EGP 3,344 million versus EGP 3,327 million
as of June 30th, 2014. Inventory remains flat at EGP 4 billion as of September 30th, 2014 (101
DOH) versus EGP 4 billion as of June 30th (105 DOH). Account receivables decreased by 12% to
reached EGP 3.8 billion as of September 30th (84 DOH) versus EGP 4.4 billion as of June 30th
(100 DOH). Payables increased by 4% to reach EGP 1.6 billion as of September 30th 2014 (40
DOH) versus EGP 1.5 billion as of June 30th (39 DOH). Total cash conversion cycle decreased to
146 DOH as of June 30th 2014 versus 166 DOH as of June 30th 2014.
