Q1-2013 ended March 31st, 2013.

June 5th, 2013

       Cairo- Egypt.

Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire & Cable
and Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced its
consolidated financial results for Q1 2013 ended March 31st, 2013.

           Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012

           •   Consolidated Revenues in Q1 2013 increased by 4% to reach EGP 3.6 billion versus
               EGP 3.5 billion in Q1 2012.
           •   Gross Profit in Q1 2013 increased by 24% to reach EGP 550 million versus EGP 442
               million in Q1 2012.

           • EBITDA in Q1 2013 decreased by 3% to reach EGP 294 million versus EGP 302
               million in Q1 2012.

           • Net Profit after Minority reached EGP 62 million during Q1 2013 versus EGP 98
               million during Q1 2012.


           Q1 2013 versus Q4 2012

          • Consolidated Revenues decreased by 11% to reach EGP 3.6 billion for Q1 2013,
            versus EGP 4.1 billion in Q4 2012.
           •   Gross Profit increased by 16% to reach EGP 550 million during Q1 2013, versus
               EGP 473 million in Q4 2012.
           •   EBITDA increased by 41% to reach EGP 294 million in Q1 2013, versus EGP 208
               million in Q4 2012.
           •   Net Profit after Minority Interest reached EGP 62 million in Q1 2013 versus EGP
               (-49) million for Q4 2012.

       Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012

       •   Revenues in Q1 2013 increased by 4% to reach EGP 3.6 billion versus EGP 3.5
           billion in Q1 2012. The Wire and Cable segment achieved revenues of EGP 2.8
           billion in Q1 2013, 5% higher than Q1 2012. Turnkey Revenues in Q1 2013 were
           23% lower than Q1 2012 reaching EGP 345 million. Meters revenues increased by
           79% reaching EGP 291 million. Transformers revenues decreased by 12% reaching
           EGP 95 million. Electrical Products revenues remain flat at EGP 64 million.
       •   Gross Profit increased by 24% to reach EGP 550 million in Q1 2013 versus EGP 442
           million in Q1 2012.The Gross Profit in the Wire and Cable segment increased by
           51% in Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012, (EGP 354 million versus EGP 234 million).
           Turnkey Gross Profit decreased by 42% in Q1 2013 to reach a Gross Profit of EGP
           67 million versus EGP 116 million in Q1 2012. The Meters division achieved a 121
           % increase in gross profit in Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012, reaching EGP 84 million. The
           transformers division Gross Profit decreased by 27% in Q1 2013 to reach EGP 21
           million. Electrical products’ gross profit remains flat at EGP 25 million in Q1 2013.

       • EBITDA achieved in Q1 2013 stood at EGP 294 million, 3% lower than Q1 2012.
           The Company took the decision to impair part of the investment in the wind related
           to the goodwill and patents. The total impairment related to the wind stood at EGP
           116 million (EGP 48.7 million related to goodwill and EGP 67.4 million related to
           the patents.) Adjusting the EBITDA to exclude the impairment would have resulted
           in an EBITDA of EGP 410 million, 36% higher than Q1 2012.
       •   Net profit after Minority reached EGP 62.2 million during Q1 2013 versus EGP 98.3
           million during Q1 2012.
       Q1 2013 versus Q4 2012
       •   Revenue during Q1 2013 was 11% lower versus Q4 2012, and the Gross Profit
           witnessed a 16% increase to reach EGP 550 million. The Wire and Cables Revenues
           decreased by 8% to reach EGP 2.8 billion, whilst the Gross profit increased by 57%
           to reach EGP 354 million. Turnkey revenues were 43% lower, whilst turnkey gross
           profit decreased by 51%. Transformers revenue remains flat during Q1 2013. Meters
           revenues and gross profit both increased by 31% and 14% respectively.

                    Commenting on the Q1 2013 results, Ahmed El Sewedy C.E.O. of Elsewedy Electric
                    stated:

             “On an operational level we had a strong first quarter. The most significant improvement
             was in the Wire and Cables segment and the Meters. The strong improvement in the gross
             profit margin for the Wire and Cable was mainly from an improvement in the Egyptian
             operations as a result of the devaluation of the currency, as we had high levels of inventory.
             In addition the strategy of the Group to convert a larger portion of the debt in Egypt to EGP
             debt mitigated any material foreign exchange losses.

             The situation in Egypt continues to be challenging, however the government appears to be
             committed to power generation projects of around 5,500MW.

             The markets that are continuing to outperform are Algeria and Saudi Arabia both in terms of
             volume growth and improved profitability.

             As for the other markets we have yet to see any pickup in demand in Libya. Iraq is going well
             in terms of the power generation project being on track in addition to the supply of cables. In
             addition, the Iraqi government has issued new tenders of approximately US$600 million for
             the supply of cables and transformers.

             We remain positive on the meters segment, which has continued to perform extremely well in
             the first quarter. The turnkey’s performance is expected to pick up during this year, given our
             strong and diversified backlog.”

Wires & Cables Segment
Wires & Cables segment revenues increased by 5% to reach EGP 2,803 million in Q1 2013
versus EGP 2,678 million in Q1 2012. Gross profit in Q1 2013 increased by 51% to reach
EGP 354 million versus EGP 234 million in Q1 2012. Gross profit margin improved to
12.6% in Q1 2013 versus 8.7% in Q1 2012. Whilst there was an improvement in profitability
in several of the international operations, mainly Algeria and KSA, the improvement in the
Egyptian operations gross profit margin was predominantly due to the devaluation in the
local currency.
 In terms of volumes, the segment sold 35,730 tons of cables in Q1 2013 versus 36,162 tons
in Q1 2012.
Gross profit per ton in Q1 2013 reached EGP 5,474 per ton versus EGP 4,246 per ton in Q1
2012.

Turnkey Projects
Turnkey revenues witnessed a 23% decline, reaching EGP 345 million in Q1 2013 versus
EGP 445 million during Q1 2012. Gross profit decreased by 42% to reach EGP 67 million in
Q1 2013 versus EGP 116 million in Q1 2012.
The main pressure on margins was from the Egyptian operations, which were lower in Q1
2013. In addition the margins for the supply and installation of HV cables in the Gulf also
declined. However, given the expected contribution of the projects in Sub Sahara Africa
which are expected to be realized in the second half of the year 2013, we expect a pickup in
revenues in this segment.

Turnkey backlog as of March 31st, 2013 stands at EGP 4.7 billion.

Meters Segment
Revenues from the meters segment increased by 79% in Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012 to reach
EGP 291 million versus EGP 163 million in Q1 2012. Gross profit surged by 121% to reach
EGP 84 million in Q1 2013.
Revenues in the meters increased 31% in Q1 2013 versus Q4 2012, whilst Gross profit
increased 14% to reach EGP 84 million. Gross profit margin improved to 28.8% in Q1 2013
versus 23.2% in Q1 2012.The significant improvement in gross profit is mainly due to a
strong shift to markets with better profitability in addition to a more profitable meter type. As
is demonstrated in the table below the average gross profit per meter in Q1 2012 was
exceptionally low due to a substantial portion of the number of meters sold during that
quarter being the simple meters out of Egypt as opposed to the higher margin smart meter
which is mainly supplied from Slovenia.
Meters backlog as of March 31st, 2013 stands at Euros 27 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment decreased 12% in Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012 to reach
EGP 95 million, whilst gross profit decreased by 27% to reach EGP 21 million in Q1 2013.

The operation that outperformed was Zambia, with revenues increasing 50% and gross profit
margin also improving. Revenues in Egypt were slightly lower in Q1 2013 versus Q1 2012;
however the largest drop in revenues was from the Sudanese and Syrian operations.

As at March 31st, 2013 the backlog in the transformers segment stood at USD 45 million.
