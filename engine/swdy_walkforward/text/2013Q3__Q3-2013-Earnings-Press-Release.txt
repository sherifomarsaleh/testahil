ended September 30th, 2013.

December 1st, 2013

       Cairo- Egypt.

Elsewedy Electric (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire & Cable and
Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announced its consolidated
financial results for 9M 2013 ending September 30th, 2013.

           Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012

           •   Consolidated revenues in Q3 2013 increased by 6% to reach EGP 3.5 billion versus EGP
               3.3 billion in Q3 2012.
           •   Gross profit in Q3 2013 increased by 6% to reach EGP 443 million versus EGP 420
               million in Q3 2012.

           • EBITDA in Q3 2013 increased by 17% to reach EGP 284 million versus EGP 243
               million in Q3 2012.

           • Net profit after Minority increased by 32% to reach EGP 46 million during Q3 2013
               versus EGP 35 million during Q3 2012.


           9M 2013 versus 9M 2012

          • Consolidated revenues increased by 7% to reach EGP 11.2 billion for 9M 2013, versus
            EGP 10.5 billion in 9M 2012.
           •   Gross profit increased by 23% to reach EGP 1,677 million during 9M 2013, versus EGP
               1,367 million in 9M 2012.
          • EBITDA increased by 23% to reach EGP 1,080 million in 9M 2013, versus EGP 877
            million in 9M 2012.
          • Net profit after Minority Interest increased by 15% to reach EGP 190 million in 9M 2013
            versus EGP 165 million for 9M 2012.

       Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012

       •   Revenues in Q3 2013 increased by 6% to reach EGP 3.5 billion versus EGP 3.3 billion in
           Q3 2012. The Wire and Cable segment achieved revenues of EGP 2,859 million in Q3
           2013, 10% higher than Q3 2012. Turnkey revenues in Q3 2013 were 15% lower than Q3
           2012 reaching EGP 310 million. Meters revenues decreased by 7% reaching EGP 208
           million. Whilst transformers revenues increased by 72% reaching EGP 103 million.
           Electrical Products revenues decreased by 33% to reach EGP 63 million.
       •   Gross Profit increased by 6% to reach EGP 443 million in Q3 2013 versus EGP 420
           million in Q3 2012.The gross profit in the Wire and Cable segment increased by 8% in
           Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012, (EGP 276 million versus EGP 255 million). Turnkey gross
           profit decreased by 15% in Q3 2013 to reach EGP 64 million versus EGP 75 million in
           Q3 2012. The Meters division gross profit decreased by 8 % in Q3 2013 to reach EGP 54
           million whilst the transformers division gross profit surged by 184% in Q3 2013 to reach
           EGP 25 million. Electrical products’ gross profit increased by 12% to reach EGP 25
           million in Q3 2013.
       •   EBITDA in Q3 2013 increased by 17% to reach EGP 284 million versus EGP 243
           million in Q3 2012.
       •   Net profit after Minority increased by 32% to reach EGP 46 million during Q2 2013
           versus EGP 35 million during Q3 2012.

           9M 2013 versus 9M 2012

Wires & Cables Segment
Wires & Cables segment revenues increased by 10% to reach EGP 2,859 million in Q3 2013
versus EGP 2,595 million in Q3 2012. Gross profit in Q3 2013 increased by 8% to reach EGP 276
million versus EGP 255 million in Q3 2012. Gross profit margin remained flat at 10%.
Revenues in 9M 2013 increased 9% to reach EGP 8,801 million, whilst gross profit increased
28% to reach EGP 1,067 million. Gross profit margin reached 12.1 % in 9M 2013 versus 10.3%
in 9M 2012.
 In terms of volumes, the segment sold 41,913 tons of cables in Q3 2013 versus 39,803 tons in Q3
2012. Volumes during 9M 2013 were flat reaching 118,494 tons versus 117,805 tons during 9M
2012. There was an 11% improvement in gross profit per ton, which averaged EGP 6,275 per ton
in 9M 2013 versus EGP 5,647 in 9M 2012.
Whilst volumes in the Egyptian operations were 3% lower in 9M 2013 versus 9M 2012, the
Algerian operations continued to see a pick up, with volumes increasing 24% in 9M 2013 versus
9M 2012. Volumes in KSA were 16% higher in 9M 2013 versus 9M 2012, whilst volumes in the
Qatar operations were flat y-o-y.

Turnkey Projects
Turnkey revenues witnessed a 15% decrease, reaching EGP 310 million in Q3 2013 versus EGP
364 million during Q3 2012. Gross profit also decreased by 15% to reach EGP 64 million in Q3
2013 versus EGP 75 million in Q3 2012.
Revenues during 9M 2013 were 9% lower than 9M 2012 reaching 1,162 million, whilst gross
profit also declined by 9% to reach 234 million.
The most notable reason for the decline in both revenues and gross profit in this segment
continues to be from the Egyptian operations. The environment in Q3 2013 was extremely
challenging given the imposed curfews and the generally difficult market conditions.
Turnkey backlog as of September 30th, 2013 remains strong at EGP 4.2 billion. Of the backlog
Egypt represents 20%, Sub Saharan Africa 24% and the GCC and Middle East 50%.

Meters Segment
Revenues from the meters segment decreased by 7% in Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012 to reach EGP
208 million versus EGP 223 million in Q3 2012 whilst gross profit decreased by 8% to reach EGP
54 million in Q3 2013 versus 58 million in Q3 2012.
Comparing 9M 2013 versus 9M 2012, revenues increased 19% whilst gross profit increased 42%.
Even though there was a reduction in the number of meters sold, the change in product and
market mix resulted in the gross profit margin improvement to reach 30% in 9M 2013 versus 25%
in 9M 2012.
Meters backlog as of September 30th, 2013 stands at Euros 10 million.

Transformers Segment
Revenues from the transformers segment increased 72% in Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012 to reach
EGP 103 million, whilst gross profit increased by 184% to reach EGP 25 million in Q3 2013.

All the transformers operations performed well during 9M 2013 versus 9M 2012 with the overall
gross profit margin also improving to reach 24% during 9M 2013 versus 17% in 9M 2012. The
strongest performance in this segment was from the Egyptian operations in which both revenues
and gross profit witnessed double-digit growth, as was the case for both Zambia and Sudan.

As at September 30th, 2013 the backlog in the transformers segment stood at USD 45 million.

Other Electrical Products
Revenues from the Electrical products segment decreased by 33% in Q3 2013 versus Q3 2012, to
reach EGP 63 million, whilst gross profit increased by12 % to reach EGP 25 million.

Net Debt
Net debt as of September 30th, 2013 reached EGP 5,043 million versus EGP 4,643 million as of
June 30th, 2013. The main reason for the increase in net debt was a result of an increase in
accounts receivable, which reached EGP 4.7 billion as of September 30th, 2013 (113 DOH) versus
EGP 4.4 billion as of June 30th (106 DOH). Inventory remained relatively flat at EGP 3.8 billion
(106 DOH), whilst payables declined from EGP 1,100 million (31DOH) to reach EGP 1,017
million (28 DOH) as of September 30th 2013. Total cash conversion cycle increased to 191 days
as of Q3 2013 versus 182 days as of Q2 2013.
