August 30th, 2016
ELSEWEDY ELECTRIC (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wire &
Cable and Integrated Energy Solution Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announces its
consolidated financial results for six-month period ended June 30th, 2016 reporting a net profit
after minority interest of EGP 1,631 million on revenues of EGP 10,554 million, up 141% y-o-y.
On a quarterly basis, the company posted a bottom line of EGP 873 million in Q2 2016 on
revenues of EGP 5,860 million, up almost two-fold compared to the EGP 438 million booked in
the same period last year.

Meanwhile, our backlog continues to grow at record rates with total backlog as
of June 30th, 2016 reaching EGP 20.4 billion. Additionally, our backlog
remains diversified with Egypt now representing 58% whereas Sub Saharan
Africa and GCC represent 23% and 16%, respectively.

Meters Segment
Revenues from the meters segment increased by 25% y-o-y in H1 2016
standing at EGP 410 million compared to the EGP 327 million posted in the
same period last year. Revenue growth for the segment was primarily driven
by increased contributions from Europe and the GCC, standing at 46% and
16%, respectively of total segment revenues. Gross profit growth mirrored the
increase in revenues, growing by 21% y-o-y to EGP 111 million in H1 2016
versus 92 million in H1 2015.

As of June 30th, 2016, total meters backlog stood at EUR 148 million.

Transformers Segment
The transformers segment posted revenues of EGP 370 million in H1 2016, up
77% y-o-y, owing largely to stronger operations in both the Egyptian and
Algerian markets. Gross profit meanwhile stood at EGP 122 million in H1
2016, up 42% y-o-y compared to H1 2015 figure of EGP 85 million. As at
June 30th, 2016 the transformers backlog stood at USD 48 million.

Other Electrical Products
Revenues from the electrical products segment increased by 6% y-o-y in H1
2016 to EGP 158 million, while gross profit inched down 1% to stand at EGP
80 million.

Selling General and Administrative Expenses
SG&A as a % of sales increased to reach 5.3% in H1 2016 versus 4.4% in H1
2015.

Net Debt
Net debt as of June 30th, 2016 decreased by 16.5% to reach EGP 3,075 million
versus EGP 3,682 million as of March 31st, 2016.

Inventory increased by 19% to reach EGP 4.2 billion as of June 30th, 2016
versus EGP 3.5 billion as of March 31st, 2016. Account receivables, including
long term receivables, remained flat at EGP 10.0 billion as of June 30th, 2016.
Payables meanwhile increased by 7% to reach EGP 7.0 billion as of June 30th,
2015 versus EGP 6.5 billion as of March 31st, 2016.

About Elsewedy Electric
Established in 1938 by the Elsewedy family, Elsewedy Electric has since grown to become a
regional and tactically global integrated cables and electrical products manufacturer and a turn-
key services provider with more than 10,000 employees working at 30 production facilities in 15
countries. Structured to face the challenges of the world’s fastest growing markets and the
complexity of the critical electricity industry, Elsewedy Electric capitalizes on its deep product
and sector specialization and unprecedented insight into local markets.
