ELSEWEDY ELECTRIC (SWDY.CA on the Egyptian stock exchange), the leading Wires & Cables and Integrated Infrastructure Solutions
Provider in the Middle East and Africa, announces its consolidated financial results for the period ending 31 March 2026, recording
revenues of EGP 75,298 million in Q1 2026, representing a 26.8% y-o-y increase. The company’s net profit after minority interest
increased by 16.9% y-o-y to reach EGP 4,845 million in Q1 2026, yielding a net profit margin of 6.4% versus 7% in Q1 2025.



Note from Our CEO

The start of the year was quite encouraging, despite the continued impact of geopolitical tensions across the
region.

Against this backdrop, I am pleased to report that Elsewedy Electric delivered revenues of EGP 75.3 billion,
reflecting year‑on‑year growth of 26.8%, while net profit amounted to EGP 4.8 billion, representing an
increase of 16.9% compared to the same period last year.

We continue to closely monitor developments in the geopolitical landscape to ensure timely, well‑informed
decision‑making that preserves the Group’s financial resilience and operational strength. Our diversified
geographic footprint, combined with our broad portfolio of products and services, continues to underpin our
strategy, enabling us to navigate ongoing uncertainties while driving sustainable long‑term growth.




Ahmed El Sewedy

Financial and Operational Highlights
   •    Revenues grew by a solid 26.8% year-on-year, reaching EGP 75.3 billion in Q1 2026. This strong performance was supported
        by broad-based growth across all business segments and favourable foreign currency translation effects. The Wires, Cables
        & Accessories segment remained the key contributor to total revenues, followed by the Engineering & Construction (E&C)
        Projects segment.
   •    Gross profit came in at EGP 13.1 billion, recording a solid year-on-year increase of 31.6%, reflecting the strong performance
        of all business lines but primarily within the Wires, Cables & Accessories and the Engineering and Construction segments.
   •    Selling, General & Administrative (SG&A) Expenses rose by 25% year-on-year to EGP 4.0 billion, driven by the impact of
        currency fluctuations, global inflationary trends, and strategic hiring initiatives across the Group. Despite the absolute
        increase, SG&A expenses as a percentage of total revenue remained almost flat, from 5.4% in Q1 2025 to 5.3% in Q1 2026,
        reflecting enhanced operational efficiency.
   •    EBITDA increased by 25.4% year-on-year to EGP 9.3 billion, supported by strong revenue growth. The EBITDA margin stood
        at 12.4% in Q1 2026, slightly below the 12.5% recorded in Q1 2025, mainly due to higher SG&A expenses during the quarter.
   •    Elsewedy’s net income after minority interest recorded a solid growth of 16.6% y-o-y to reach EGP 4,833 million in Q1 2026,
        with an associated margin of 6.4% versus 7.0% in Q1 2025.
   •    Net bank debt increased to EGP 24,022 million as of 31 March 2026 compared to a net debt of EGP 19,789 million at year-
        end 2025.

Financial and Operational Review
Revenues The Group reported consolidated revenues of EGP 75.3 billion in Q1 2026, reflecting a strong year-on-year increase of 26.8%.
This solid top-line performance was driven by the robust contributions across business segments, favourable foreign exchange
movements, and higher-than-average pricing during the quarter. The Group’s broad international footprint also played a key role in
supporting revenue growth.

The Wires, Cables & Accessories segment remained the primary revenue driver, contributing 60% of total revenues, followed by the
Engineering & Construction (E&C) Projects segment, which accounted for 24%.

About Elsewedy Electric
Established in 1938 by the Elsewedy family, Elsewedy Electric has since grown to become a regional and tactically global integrated
infrastructure solutions provider with more than 20,000 employees working at 31 production facilities in 15 countries. Structured to
face the challenges of the world’s fastest growing markets and the complexity of the critical electricity industry, Elsewedy Electric
capitalizes on its deep product and sector specialization and unprecedented insight into local markets. www.elsewedyelectric.com
