# ADIB (EGX) — basis-break register

Built **before** any modelling, per [R-FCAL-01 §1]. INTERNAL.

Every break below states the overlap year, the chain factor where one exists, and the
treatment. A driver is scored only inside its own definition window.

| # | break | overlap year | chain factor | treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Identity and franchise.** The issuer is National Bank for Development S.A.E. until the FY2013 filings and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) – Egypt S.A.E. thereafter. Same legal entity, same EGX line, renamed after ADIB PJSC took control; the conventional loan book was run off and replaced with Islamic financing. | FY2012/FY2013 | none — the entity is continuous | The series is NOT chained. It is one entity throughout. The break is a **franchise** break and is handled by the era split in B2. |
| B2 | **Loss era → profit era.** FY2009–FY2013 is a distressed, recapitalising bank: cumulative attributable losses of EGP 2.46bn over FY2010–FY2012, accumulated losses of EGP 3.69bn on the FY2012 balance sheet, three capital injections. FY2014 onward is a profitable Islamic bank. | FY2013 (first profitable year) | none | **Structural break for the correction rule** [R-FCAL-01 §5]: any correction estimated across it is reset. Origins are run from FY2014 (first year with five years of consolidated history) AND, separately, from FY2016 (first origin whose whole five-year window sits inside the profit era). Both are reported; neither is selected. |
| B3 | **Consolidated non-bank subsidiary.** FY2010 and FY2011 print `Sales revenue` inside the funds block and `Cost of sale` inside the fee block — a trading subsidiary consolidated line-by-line. FY2012 removes both and restates FY2011 net fees from −16,340 to +57,927. | FY2011 | net fees: restated/original = 57,927 / −16,340 (sign change; no usable factor) | The fee **ratio** driver is defined only from FY2012. FY2010–FY2011 fee figures enter the panel as filed, are flagged, and are never inside a trailing fee window used at any origin (the earliest origin's window starts FY2010 only for balance-sheet drivers). |
| B4 | **Filing unit.** FY2010 and FY2011 are filed in whole EGP; FY2012 onward in EGP thousands. | FY2011/FY2012 | 1/1,000 | Division by 1,000 applied and marked on the row. It is the only arithmetic applied to any filed figure in the panel. |
| B5 | **IFRS 9 / CBE expected-credit-loss regime.** The impairment line is `Cost of credit` / `(Impairment loss) reversal` through FY2018, `Impairment charge for credit losses` FY2019–FY2020, and `Expected credit losses` FY2022 onward, following the CBE's IFRS 9 adoption for Egyptian banks (2019 transition, `General Reserve Risk IFRS 9` first appearing in the FY2017 equity note). | FY2018/FY2019 | none disclosed | Cost of risk is scored across the whole window because the **charge** is continuous even though its label is not; the label change is recorded so a sign flip is read as a regime effect and not as a driver failure. |
| B6 | **EGP devaluations.** Nov-2016 float (EGP/USD 8.9→~18), Mar-2022, Oct-2022, Jan-2023 and Mar-2024 steps (19.2→30.6→45.3). Every balance-sheet and income line is nominal EGP. | continuous | EGP/USD from World Bank PA.NUS.FCRF, in `macro_eg_wdi.json` | These are the macro path, not breaks in definition. They are the whole point of the macro/company split in §4: every origin is re-run on the knowable inflation path and on perfect foresight. |
| B7 | **Share capital.** Issued capital EGP 1,999,503k (FY2011–FY2015) → 2,000,000k (FY2016–FY2021) → 4,000,000k (FY2022) → 5,000,000k (FY2023) → 6,000,000k (FY2024) → 12,000,000k (FY2025), against a par value of EGP 10. A separate `Paid under capital increase` balance of EGP 1,861,418k sat in equity from FY2014 to FY2021 and was absorbed into capital in FY2022. | each increase year | share count = issued capital / 10 | The count is **footed** at every origin against the year's own filed capital and never carried back. Weighted-average counts implied by filed EPS differ from the year-end count in every increase year and are recorded beside, not substituted. |
| B8 | **Discontinued operations.** FY2021 and FY2022 print a `Profit for the period from Discontinued Operations` line (−22,859 and −5,806) below net profit from continuing operations. | FY2021–FY2022 | none | Kept out of the driver build (it is not a driver) and inside the scored `np`, because `np` is what the company reported and is what a naive benchmark would freeze. |
| B9 | **Basis of the FY2021 row.** No English FY2021 consolidated filing exists on the issuer's site — the file published under that name is the Arabic filing. FY2021 is taken from the comparative column of the FY2022 audited consolidated statements. | FY2021 | none | Tier A and same issuer, but it is a **comparative**, so if the FY2022 auditors restated FY2021 this panel carries the restatement, not the original. That is the one row in the panel where the point-in-time rule cannot be fully honoured, and it is stated rather than smoothed. FY2021 is an origin; the risk is that its own trailing window is marginally cleaner than a FY2021 reader would have had. |
