RAYA HOLDING AT GLANCE
•   Raya Holding is an investment holding company headquartered in
    Cairo, Egypt, managing a diversified investment portfolio of
    companies.

•   Raya Holding operates through a diversified portfolio of 10
    companies, strategically positioned across high-growth sectors of
    the regional economy.

•   Our investment portfolio is segmented into:
         FINTECH | Aman Holding
         TECHNOLOGY & INFRASTRUCTURE | Raya Information
        Technology [RIT]
         RETAIL & DISTRIBUTION | Raya Trade and Raya FMCG
         MANUFACTURING | Raya Electric, Raya Foods, and Raya Auto
         BUSINESS PROCESS OUTSOURCING | Raya Customer
        Experience [RCX]
         HOSPITALITY | Raya Smart Buildings and Raya Restaurants
         Logistics | Ostool

•   Raya Holding accommodates a wide international customer base
    from on-ground operations spanning Egypt, KSA, UAE, Poland,
    and Nigeria.
