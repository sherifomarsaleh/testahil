## 4 · DISCOUNT RATE

Explicit cost of equity **28.54%** at a beta of 0.891; explicit weighted cost of capital
**23.12%** on market-value weights of 42.55% equity / 57.45% debt. Terminal cost of equity
**17.04%**; terminal weighted rate **13.45%**; terminal risk-free 10.50%, read from the house
path.

**THE COST OF EQUITY REPRODUCES FROM ITS OWN INPUTS UNDER A NAMED CONSTRUCTION,** which it

points of cost of equity before the terminal rate moved it up.

**THE DEBT WEIGHT IS 57.45% AND IT IS THE LARGEST STRUCTURAL FACT IN THIS RATE.** GB Corp is
a lender as well as a manufacturer; the weights are struck on market-value equity against
disclosed borrowings, never book.

## 5 · TERMINAL

**TERMINAL VALUE IS 85.1% OF THE AUTO LEG'S ENTERPRISE VALUE, and that is the single most
important number in this review.** Present value of the explicit years EGP 6,964.6mn against
a discounted terminal of EGP 39,780.8mn, enterprise value EGP 46,745.4mn.

The explicit window is the recovery, and the terminal is the business that recovery arrives
at. At an explicit free-cash-flow path of 151 → 980 → 2,964 → 4,068 → 5,384 the last year is
still climbing steeply, so the terminal is not capitalising a steady state so much as
capitalising the end of a ramp. **A reader who doubts the ramp should not read past this
heading**: 85% of the auto leg rests on the year after the last one this study forecasts.

Terminal growth 7.00% nominal at zero real, against a terminal rate embedding the same 7.00%
inflation. No real decline hidden in the gap.

**AND THE LOWER TERMINAL RATE ENLARGED THIS.** A correction that improves a rate also
enlarges the part of the answer that rate governs. The terminal share rose with it.

## Verdict

**No error found in the company's favour.** The lever that moved this edition is a house
macro rate this study reads rather than chooses.

**Three things could make this the study that is wrong, and all three are named in the
filings rather than in a forecast:**

1. **The MNT-Halan mark.** The reviewer's conclusion on the 30 June statements is QUALIFIED
   on exactly this line. If the carrying value is written down rather than up, the lower
   branch is the ceiling and not the floor.
2. **The terminal, at 85.1% of the auto leg.** The last explicit year is still climbing
   steeply; a ramp that stalls does not merely lower the explicit years, it removes most of
   the answer.
3. **The absence of a conglomerate discount.** This study declines to type one. A reader who
   believes a holding company's parts are not freely realisable is entitled to apply one, and
   at the 10% the delivered edition carried the nearer branch falls to about EGP 41.2 — still
   well above the price, which is the honest report of that objection rather than a defence
   against it.
