## 2 · WHY EACH TRACED ITEM MOVES NOTHING

**The H1-2026 figures are already consumed, from the filing.** `rev_h1_2026 = 48,474.4`
and `ni_h1_2026 = 1,262.0` are committed inputs sourced to GB Corp's own reviewed
statements. The return reported the same profit and a revenue range whose lower end is a
press error our primary source resolves. The external pass tested a number we held; it
did not supply one. **Nothing to rebuild.**

**The CBE rate is not a study input.** [R-MACRO-01] — a study may not carry an
inflation-class number of its own, and every such input maps to the house ladder. An
unchanged policy rate therefore cannot move this model whichever way it went.

**The Tanzania resolution is immaterial and is not a capital event.** USD 2.33mn
transferred before 2025 plus USD 415k in 2025, against a group whose half-year revenue is
EGP 48.5bn. Not an equity issuance, refinancing, dividend decision, auditor change or
ownership change.

**The import-policy item is directionally supportive and is not a disclosure.** The same
report records no formal change in clearance rules since 2024. There is no GB Corp volume,
price or share figure in it, and no evidence that any tariff entered force. Moving a volume
or margin driver on it would be inventing an input.

**The USD 900mn–1.0bn commentary may not enter, and cuts the other way.** It is press and
unnamed-source material, apparently scoped to the Egyptian business alone, with no
prospectus, offer range or company confirmation behind it. SIGCM clause 1 forbids it as a
source for a number. It is on the watch list precisely because it runs AGAINST the branch
we publish higher — a lead that would hurt our own more favourable answer gets the same
treatment as one that helps it.

## 3 · THE ONE THING THAT DOES CHANGE — AND IT IS NOT A DRIVER

MNT Tech Holding's temporary EGX listing (approved 14 September 2026, six-month window to
complete the offering) supplies **no tradable price, no transaction price, no revised
ownership percentage and no accounting remeasurement.** Par value is legal capital, not
valuation. The crux is which basis marks a 41.61% stake in an unlisted company, and a
temporary listing does not decide it — so the two-sided answer stands for exactly the
reason it was two-sided.

What it does change is the **falsifier's date**. The study's own words, checked against the
delivered PDF rather than remembered:

- *"GB Corp's largest single component is a minority interest in an unlisted company"* —
  **still true.** A temporary listing with no completed offering is not a public market.
- The expert cross-examination's *"a company that has never had a public exit"* —
  **still true**, and now carries a dated path toward one, with an offering deadline around
  mid-March 2027.
- No occurrence of "IPO" or "flotation" anywhere in the delivered document.

**So the document is incomplete, not wrong**, and the incompleteness is one catalyst
sentence. That is a DISCLOSURE change and it is registered here for the study's next
edition rather than triggering a re-issue: rebuilding sixteen sections, sixteen sheets, a
bibliography and an expert appendix to add a catalyst — while every number in all of them
is unchanged — is a re-issue that moves nothing, and the study is in any case held from
publishing on the method [R-GAP-02 clause three], so no reader is receiving the incomplete
version meanwhile.

**THE COST OF DEFERRING IS STATED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED LATER.** If GBCO is published
before that next edition, it reaches a reader without the most recent dated catalyst on the
one judgement its whole answer turns on. The release condition is therefore explicit: **this
item is added to §5 Catalysts and to the two-sided judgement's falsifier before GBCO
publishes**, whichever edition that happens to be.
