## 2 · THE MATERIAL EVENT — a catalyst, not a mark

**MNT Tech Holding: temporary EGX listing approved 14 September 2026.** 1.6bn shares,
EGP 0.10 par, EGP 160mn issued capital; FRA no-objection 13 September; ticker
`HALN.CA`, non-bank financial services; a six-month window to complete the offering or
the temporary listing lapses, subject to possible FRA extension.

**SOURCE STATUS: ARABIC PRESS REPORTING AN EGX/FRA ACTION — not the disclosure
itself.** Under SIGCM clause 1, press is never a source for a number, so this cannot
enter the model as one. It is recorded as a lead.

**WHY IT DOES NOT RESOLVE THE CRUX.** GB Corp's two-sided answer turns on which basis
values MNT-Halan: the reviewed carrying value of EGP 15,733.5mn, or 41.61% of the June
USD 1.4bn round. A temporary listing supplies NO tradable price, NO transaction price,
NO revised ownership percentage and NO accounting remeasurement. Par value is legal
capital, not valuation; EGP 160mn says nothing about what the company is worth.

**WHAT IT DOES CHANGE, AND IT IS NOT A DRIVER.** The study says the filings do not
decide between the two bases, so neither does the study. That remains true — but there
is now a DATED PATH by which the market may decide, with an offering deadline around
mid-March 2027. That belongs in the study's catalysts section and gives the two-sided
judgement's falsifier a date it did not have. It is a disclosure change, not a
valuation change.
