Lens 2 · Technical — what is the chart doing?

Bull case:
Bear case:

In plain terms: Beltone is four platforms in one wrapper — Egypt’s fastest-scaling brokerage and largest non-bank asset manager, a fast-growing NBFI lender, a venture arm, and (from Q1-2026) Baobab, a pan-African microfinance bank bought from its own majority shareholder. Marked platform by platform the parts are worth ~EGP 3.00 a share before any fairness discount; at 2.97 the price pays for nearly all of it. The whole argument is whether the enlarged book lifts its return on equity from ~11% toward 18%.
